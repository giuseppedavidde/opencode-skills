# Options Trading Crash Course
**Authors**: Mark Elder, Brian Douglas | **Chapters**: 9 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Binary Outcome Model** — Options trading reduces to a binary decision: predict whether an asset price goes up (call) or down (put) before expiry. Payout is fixed (~90% of wager), loss is total wager.

**Two-Analysis Framework** — All trading decisions rest on two pillars: **Fundamental Analysis** (macro economics, company financials, news events) and **Technical Analysis** (price history, chart patterns, trends). Beginners start with technical analysis for quick confidence; fundamentals provide long-term edge.

**Risk-First Mindset** — "Focus on not losing money rather than on making money" (Ch.9). Every trade must define max risk before entry. Never risk >10% of total investment fund on a single trade.

**Strategy-Market Fit** — Match strategy to market condition (bullish, bearish, neutral). A bull call spread works in bull markets; a bear put spread in bear markets; a butterfly in neutral markets.

**Repair over Panic** — When a trade goes against you, repair strategies (rolling down, butterfly conversion, delta hedging) can lower breakeven points instead of taking a full loss.
