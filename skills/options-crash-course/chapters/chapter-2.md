# Chapter 2: How to Start Trading

## Core Idea
Start with binary options through an intuitive broker. Binary options simplify trading to a yes/no decision on price direction, making them ideal for beginners. The focus is on choosing the right platform and understanding what stock market investing really entails.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Derivative-First Approach** — trade derivatives (binary options, CFDs) rather than buying underlying shares. Benefits: zero commissions, earn on both up and down moves, lower capital requirements
- **Risk-Free Practice** — use demo accounts to simulate real market conditions without risking capital
- **Short-Term vs Long-Term** — short-term binary/Forex/CFD trading avoids long-term risks (inflation, systemic risk, political uncertainty)

## Key Concepts
- **Stock Exchange**: A regulated market where shares of listed companies are exchanged
- **Derivative**: A financial contract whose value depends on an underlying asset (stock, currency, etc.)
- **CFD (Contract for Difference)**: Trade price movements without owning the underlying; zero stamp duty, lower commissions
- **Binary Option**: Predict only direction (up/down), not magnitude; fixed payout
- **Demo Account**: Practice platform with virtual money, essential for beginners
- **Investment Return Formula**: inflation + risk-free return + risk margin ≈ 11% gross

## Anti-patterns
- "Playing" the stock market instead of treating it as serious investment
- Starting with direct share purchases through traditional banks (high fees)
- Trusting forum advice without verification
- Focusing on "how much can I earn?" instead of "how do I learn?"
- Long-term holding in current market conditions (high systemic risk)

## Key Takeaways
- Binary options are the simplest entry point: predict direction, pick timeframe
- Demo accounts are essential for learning without financial risk
- Use derivatives (options, CFDs) not direct shares for best cost structure
- Short-term trading avoids long-term market risks
- Earnings vary widely; no fixed return rate exists
