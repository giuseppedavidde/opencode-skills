# Chapter 6: Volume Price Analysis — The Next Level

## Core Idea
Three premier candles (Shooting Star, Hammer, Long Legged Doji) form the core of VPA candle analysis. Combined with Stopping Volume and Topping Out Volume, they provide complete entry, holding, and exit signals. Volume grades the significance of each candle pattern.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Five Candle Principles**: (1) Wick length reveals strength/weakness, (2) No wick = strong sentiment, (3) Narrow body = weak sentiment, (4) Same candle different meaning by trend position, (5) Volume validates price always
- **Shooting Star**: Price action = weakness. Volume grades it: low = minor pause, avg = moderate reversal, ultra-high = insider selling climax.
- **Hammer**: Price action = strength. Volume grades it: low = minor bounce, avg = moderate reversal, ultra-high = insider buying climax.
- **Long Legged Doji**: Price action = indecision. UNIQUE: CAN have anomaly on low volume (stop-hunting/manipulation). Needs min avg volume to be valid.
- **Hanging Man**: Hammer at top of bull trend = BEARISH. First sign of selling pressure.
- **Wide Spread Candle**: Strong sentiment. High vol = genuine; low vol = warning/trap.
- **Narrow Spread Candle**: Weak sentiment. Low vol = normal; HIGH vol = anomaly (absorption).
- **Stopping Volume**: Deep lower wicks + high vol in downtrend. Multiple candles needed to apply brakes.
- **Topping Out Volume**: Deep upper wicks + high vol in uptrend. Precursor to selling climax.

## Key Concepts
- Multiple identical candles in same area exponentially increase signal strength
- Volume also helps HOLD positions in trends — low vol pullbacks are staying signals
- News releases produce long-legged dojis — volume reveals manipulation
- Three premier candles are NEVER anomalous in price action; volume always validates strength

## Anti-patterns
- Assuming a single hammer/shooting star is a reversal — it's just a warning
- Trading the hanging man as strength at trend top
- Interpreting long-legged doji on low volume as indecision (it's manipulation)
- Getting excited by wide spread candles without checking volume

## Key Takeaways
- Three premier candles + volume = complete entry/management/exit system
- Stopping volume and topping out volume bracket trend extremes
- Same candle = different meaning depending on trend location
- Volume validates everything — without it, candle reading is incomplete
