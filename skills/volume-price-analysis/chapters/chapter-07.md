# Chapter 7: Support And Resistance Explained

## Core Idea
Support and resistance zones are the breeding grounds for trends. Combined with VPA, they reveal when trends are about to start, pause, or reverse. These zones are densely populated with weak/strong traders — volume validates breakouts.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Congestion Zones**: ~70% of market time. Created by three forces: pending news, climax phases, and old price areas with trapped traders.
- **Ceiling & Floor**: Analogy of house floors/ceilings. Resistance becomes support on breakout and vice versa.
- **Isolated Pivots**: 3-bar reversal patterns defining congestion boundaries. Pivot high = ceiling, pivot low = floor.
- **Congestion Entrance**: First pivot high + first pivot low define the zone.
- **Breakout Rules**: (1) Need "clear water" above ceiling, (2) Must be validated by above-average volume, (3) Low vol breakout = fakeout/trap.
- **Trapped Traders**: Weak traders buy at tops, sell at bottoms — creating the dense populations that make S/R zones sticky.

## Key Concepts
- Pivot high: centre candle has higher high AND higher low than neighbours
- Pivot low: centre candle has lower low AND lower high than neighbours
- Breakout with volume = continuation/reversal signal; breakout without volume = trap
- Ceilings/floors are "rubber bands," not rods of steel — some give is normal
- Once broken, ceiling becomes support; floor becomes resistance
- Old congestion zones remain on charts forever — market "remembers" them
- Multiple time frames: congestion on 5min < 15min < hourly in significance

## Anti-patterns
- Treating S/R lines as exact price levels — they are zones
- Entering on first touch of ceiling/floor without waiting for volume confirmation
- Ignoring old congestion zones when they are revisited

## Key Takeaways
- Congestion zones are where trends are born — they are OPPORTUNITIES, not frustrations
- Pivots define congestion; volume validates breakouts
- S/R combined with VPA gives a complete framework for entries, exits, and stop placement
- What was resistance becomes support — the house analogy makes this intuitive
