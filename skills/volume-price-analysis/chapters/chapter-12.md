# Chapter 12: Volume And Price — The Next Generation

## Core Idea
New developments extend traditional VPA: Equivolume charts replace time with volume on the X-axis; Candlevolume combines equivolume boxes with candle wicks; Delta Volume measures bid-ask volume differential. The core VPA principles remain valid across all these innovations.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Equivolume Charts**: Richard Arms (1994). X-axis = volume, Y-axis = price. Boxes instead of candles. Width = volume, height = price range. Colour = direction (black up, red down).
  - Tall narrow box = wide price spread + low volume = anomaly
  - Short wide box = narrow price spread + high volume = anomaly
  - Square box = proportional effort/result = normal
  - "Power box" = high volume + wide price = genuine breakout
- **Candlevolume**: Hybrid equivolume + candles. Variable-width candles with wicks. Combines volume visibility with traditional candle reading.
- **Delta Volume**: Volume at ask minus volume at bid. Positive delta = buying pressure; negative delta = selling pressure. Each bar shows net direction of order flow.
- **Cumulative Delta Volume**: Sum of delta across bars. Shows persistent buying/selling pressure trend.

## Key Concepts
- Equivolume removes time from price axis — Coulling cautions this loses the Cause & Effect (time) dimension
- Candlevolume preserves wicks (sentiment reversals) while adding volume-width visibility
- Delta requires bid/ask data — most applicable to futures and equities with open exchanges
- These are emerging tools, not replacements for core VPA methodology

## Anti-patterns
- Replacing VPA with equivolume — they are complementary, not substitutes
- Ignoring the time dimension — Wyckoff's Cause & Effect relies on time
- Assuming delta volume is available or reliable in all markets (forex lacks true bid/ask volume)

## Key Takeaways
- VPA continues to evolve — equivolume, candlevolume, delta are promising extensions
- Core principles (effort vs result, cause & effect) remain the foundation
- Candlevolume best preserves traditional VPA analysis while adding volume visibility
- Delta and cumulative delta offer a new dimension for order-flow analysis in suitable markets
