# Chapter 9: Volume At Price (VAP)

## Core Idea
VAP dissects a volume bar to show WHERE volume concentrated within the price range. It reveals the density of buying/selling at each price level, turning invisible S/R zones into a visible histogram on the Y-axis.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **VAP Histogram**: Volume distribution on Y-axis, showing concentration per price level
- **VAP vs VPA**: VPA = outside view of candle (total volume); VAP = inside view (volume per price level)
- **Visual S/R**: VAP makes congestion zones visible instantly — dense volume = strong S/R
- **Volume Density Benchmarking**: Compare VAP density across congestion zones to gauge significance
- **Colour Coding**: Blue/red ratio in VAP bar shows balance of up vs down candles at that level

## Key Concepts
- VAP is a SUPPORTING technique to VPA, not a replacement
- VAP reveals which price levels had concentrated buying (bottom of range) vs selling (top of range)
- Longer congestion + denser VAP = stronger support/resistance
- VAP profiles change dynamically as new price action occurs
- Market makers/insiders cannot hide volume concentration at key levels
- Any good charting platform (NinjaTrader, etc.) includes VAP as standard

## Key Concepts (continued)
- A VAP spike in a narrow price range signals potential reversal (absorption/demand meeting supply)
- VAP helps place stops: below dense volume support, above dense volume resistance
- VAP can validate breakouts: breakout from dense VAP zone on high vol = genuine

## Anti-patterns
- Using VAP INSTEAD of VPA — they complement each other
- Ignoring VAP when trading breakouts from old congestion zones
- Treating VAP levels as hard price barriers rather than density zones

## Key Takeaways
- VAP makes invisible S/R visible — it paints the market's memory on the chart
- Dense VAP regions = high-probability reversal/breakout zones
- VAP is a visual confirmation tool for VPA analysis
- Combining VPA (candle-level) with VAP (price-level) gives a 3D view of the market
