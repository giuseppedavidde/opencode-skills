# Chapter 2: Why Volume

## Core Idea
Volume is the ONLY leading indicator. Combined with price, it creates an explosive analytical tool. The "Parable of Uncle Joe" — a wholesaler who manipulates supply, demand, and news to maximise profit — mirrors exactly how market makers operate. Volume reveals their true activity.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Uncle Joe Parable**: Wholesaler creates fear/greed via rumour to manipulate inventory
- **Market Maker Mechanics**: Specialists who see both sides of the book, set prices, and manage inventory
- **Tick Volume as Proxy**: In spot forex, tick data is ~90% representative of actual volume
- **Volume is Relative**: Only meaningful when compared to recent history on the SAME feed
- **Auction Analogy**: Empty room = low volume / low price move; packed room = high volume / fast price action

## Key Concepts
- All markets are manipulated (stocks: specialists; forex: central banks; futures: large operators)
- Volume reveals the truth behind price action
- Consistency of volume feed > absolute accuracy
- Volume without price is meaningless; price without volume is incomplete
- Time is a key component — same volume in less time = more pressure
- Four elements of price: open, high, low, close — electronic trading has changed their significance
- Buying/selling is always from the wholesaler (insider) perspective in this book

## Anti-patterns
- Obsessing over volume feed quality — relative comparison on same feed is sufficient
- Thinking tick volume in forex is useless — it works in practice
- Ignoring the market maker structure — understanding it is essential to VPA

## Key Takeaways
- Volume is your window into insider activity
- The Uncle Joe story is a complete model of market manipulation
- Even imperfect volume (tick data) is enormously valuable
- All volume analysis is relative — compare bar to bar, not to an absolute standard
