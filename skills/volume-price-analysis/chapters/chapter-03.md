# Chapter 3: The Right Price

## Core Idea
Price is the second component of VPA. While volume reveals activity, price reveals the battle between buyers and sellers. Candle anatomy — especially wicks and spread — tells the story of market sentiment. Volume then validates or invalidates that story.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Candle Anatomy**: Open, high, low, close, upper/lower wicks, spread
- **Wick Significance**: Reveals complete reversal in market sentiment during the session
- **Spread Significance**: Wide = strong sentiment; narrow = weak sentiment
- **Price Action vs VPA**: PAT (Price Action Trading) tells half the story; volume completes it
- **Sine Wave Price Model**: Price oscillates as buyers/sellers battle; candle reveals the outcome
- **VAP Introduction**: Volume At Price — "inside the candle" volume distribution

## Key Concepts
- Lower wick = sellers overwhelmed by buyers = potential strength; Upper wick = buyers overwhelmed by sellers = potential weakness
- Zero spread (open = close) is AS significant as a large spread
- Wicks + volume = one of the most powerful signals in VPA
- Electronic trading has reduced significance of opens/closes (24hr markets)
- Time frame matters: same candle pattern on daily chart > 5-minute chart
- Candle colour (bullish/bearish) matters only for direction, not strength

## Anti-patterns
- Trading price action alone without volume validation
- Ignoring wicks — they reveal true intra-session sentiment shifts
- Treating all candles equally regardless of where they appear in the trend

## Key Takeaways
- Wicks reveal the REAL story of each trading session
- A candle's meaning changes completely depending on trend position
- Spread shows sentiment strength; wicks show sentiment reversal
- Volume is the only way to know whether the price action is genuine
