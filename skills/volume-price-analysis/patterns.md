# Volume Price Analysis — Patterns Reference

## 1. Shooting Star (Weakness)

**When to use**: After an extended bullish move or during consolidation at resistance. Watch for it at potential tops.

**How to identify**: Long upper wick (at least 2x body), small body at lower end, close near open. Volume bar beneath.

**Volume context**:
| Volume | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Low | Minor pause, not significant |
| Average | Moderate weakness, potential pullback |
| Ultra-high | Insiders selling heavily — major reversal likely |

**Trade-offs**: Single shooting star = warning only. Wait for confirmation (2nd shooting star or subsequent down candle). Can appear in downtrends as continuation weakness.

## 2. Hammer (Strength)

**When to use**: After an extended bearish move or during consolidation at support. Watch for it at potential bottoms.

**How to identify**: Long lower wick (at least 2x body), small body at upper end, close near open. Volume bar beneath.

**Volume context**:
| Volume | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Low | Minor bounce, not significant |
| Average | Moderate strength, potential pullback |
| Ultra-high | Insiders buying heavily — major reversal likely |

**Trade-offs**: Single hammer = warning only. Market may pause, pull back, then continue lower. Requires confirmation. At trend top, hammer becomes HANGING MAN (bearish).

## 3. Long Legged Doji (Indecision / Manipulation)

**When to use**: During news releases, at potential reversal points, or when market is whipsawing.

**How to identify**: Open = close (or very close), long upper AND lower wicks. Resembles a cross.

**Volume context**:
| Volume | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Low | ANOMALY — stop-hunting / manipulation. NOT a valid reversal signal |
| Average | Legitimate indecision, potential reversal |
| High | Strong indecision — major battle between buyers and sellers |

**Trade-offs**: UNIQUE among premier candles — can have anomaly on low volume. Always check volume before interpreting.

## 4. Stopping Volume (Brakes Applied)

**When to use**: During a sharp downtrend/waterfall. Look for the market slowing its descent.

**How to identify**: Sequence of 2-3 candles with deep lower wicks + high/rising volume. Each candle's body may narrow. Ends with hammer.

**How to trade**: Wait for the full sequence and confirmation (low-volume test or hammer). Do NOT enter on first stopping volume candle.

**Trade-offs**: Takes time to develop (market is like an oil tanker). Premature entry leads to being stopped out. High probability setup when completed.

## 5. Topping Out Volume (Market Tiring)

**When to use**: During an extended uptrend. Look for momentum slowing at the top.

**How to identify**: Sequence of 2-3 candles with deep upper wicks + high/rising volume. Spreads narrow. Ends with shooting star.

**How to trade**: Wait for shooting star confirmation. Do NOT short on first topping volume candle.

**Trade-offs**: Can take several candles. The final shooting star is the confirmation trigger.

## 6. Low Volume Test (Insider All-Clear)

**When to use**: After accumulation phase (test of supply) or after distribution phase (test of demand).

**How to identify**: Narrow spread candle (body can be bullish or bearish) with deep wick, accompanied by LOW volume. Price moves back toward the congestion zone, then recovers.

**How to trade**: ENTRY signal. Low volume = no residual supply/demand. Insiders are ready to move.

**Trade-offs**: High volume test = FAILED test = more consolidation needed. Do NOT enter on a failed test. This is one of the highest-probability VPA signals.

## 7. Wide Spread + High Volume (Genuine Move)

**When to use**: On breakouts from congestion or during trend development.

**How to identify**: Candle with wide body (closes near high/low), small or no wicks, accompanied by above-average volume.

**How to trade**: This validates the move. Enter in the direction of the breakout.

**Trade-offs**: If volume is LOW with a wide spread = TRAP. Always check the volume bar.

## 8. Narrow Spread + High Volume (Absorption)

**When to use**: After extended trend, at potential reversal points.

**How to identify**: Narrow body, high volume. Price barely moved despite massive effort.

**How to trade**: WARNING signal. In uptrend = potential top (selling into buying). In downtrend = potential bottom (buying into selling).

**Trade-offs**: Can be the first signal of a climax. Look for confirmation on subsequent candles.

## 9. Congestion Breakout (Trend Spawn)

**When to use**: After extended sideways price action (congestion zone).

**How to identify**: Price closes clearly above ceiling (bullish) or below floor (bearish) with above-average rising volume.

**How to trade**: Enter on the breakout bar or on the first pullback that holds the breakout level on low volume.

**Trade-offs**: Low volume breakout = fakeout/trap. Need "clear water" above/below — not just a wick. Use VAP to assess density of the congestion zone for gauging breakout significance.

## 10. Climax (Buying / Selling)

**When to use**: At extreme trend exhaustion points.

**How to identify**: Repeated ultra-high volume bars with narrow spreads and deep wicks. Buying climax = deep lower wicks. Selling climax = deep upper wicks.

**How to trade**: Do NOT trade against the climax. Wait for it to complete. Look for the low-volume test that follows. That test is the entry trigger.

**Trade-offs**: Climaxes can span multiple candles (2-5+). Patience required. The fireworks display marks the END of the trend, not the reversal point.

## 11. Congestion Patterns

| Pattern | Bias | Entry | Stop |
|---------|------|-------|------|
| Falling Triangle | Bearish | Below floor on volume | Above last pivot high |
| Rising Triangle | Bullish | Above ceiling on volume | Below last pivot low |
| Pennant | Unknown | Breakout direction | Opposite side of pennant |
| Triple Top | Bearish/reversal | Below support or above resistance | Opposite side of zone |
| Triple Bottom | Bullish/reversal | Above resistance or below support | Opposite side of zone |
