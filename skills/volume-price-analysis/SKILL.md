# A Complete Guide To Volume Price Analysis
**Author**: Anna Coulling | **Chapters**: 12 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. Validation vs Anomaly
The ONLY two states in VPA. Volume either **validates** price (confirms continuation) or reveals an **anomaly** (signals potential reversal). Every candle, every sequence, every trend is analysed through this binary lens.

### 2. Wyckoff's Three Laws
- **Law of Supply & Demand**: When demand > supply, prices rise; when supply > demand, prices fall.
- **Law of Cause & Effect**: The extent of any price move (effect) is proportional to the accumulation/distribution period (cause). Longer consolidation → bigger breakout.
- **Law of Effort vs Result**: Volume (effort) and price range (result) must be in harmony. High volume + narrow spread = anomaly (weakness/strength). Low volume + wide spread = anomaly (trap/manipulation).

### 3. The Market Cycle (Accumulation → Distribution)
Markets cycle through: **Accumulation** (insiders buy at wholesale, warehouse fills) → **Markup** (steady rise, confidence builds) → **Distribution** (insiders sell at retail, warehouse empties) → **Markdown** (fast decline, panic selling). This cycle repeats across ALL time frames.

### 4. Three-Step VPA Process
1. **Micro**: Analyse each candle individually for validation/anomaly
2. **Macro**: Analyse candle clusters for minor trend signals
3. **Global**: Full chart context — S/R, trend lines, longer-term structure

### 5. Volume Confirms Price Direction
- Rising prices + rising volume = **genuine bullish move**
- Falling prices + rising volume = **genuine bearish move**
- Rising prices + falling volume = **weakness / potential reversal**
- Falling prices + falling volume = **selling exhaustion / potential reversal**

### 6. Volume is the ONLY Leading Indicator
Price alone is lagging. Volume reveals insider activity, manipulative moves, and whether smart money is joining the move. Without volume, all price action is ambiguous.

### 7. All Volume is Relative
Absolute volume values are irrelevant. Only the **relative comparison** to recent bars matters. A consistent feed (even imperfect tick volume in forex) is sufficient — consistency > accuracy.

### 8. Multiple Time Frame Analysis
Three time frames: **fast** (entry signals), **primary** (trade execution), **slow** (dominant trend bias). Ripple effect: signals appear first on fast, propagate to slow. Trends aligned across TFs = lower risk.

### 9. Candles + Volume = Complete Picture
Three premier candles: **Shooting Star** (weakness), **Hammer** (strength), **Long Legged Doji** (indecision). Volume grades the signal strength: low vol = minor, avg = moderate, ultra-high = climax.

### 10. News is Validated by Volume
Ignore news headlines. Watch volume reaction: strong volume surge = insiders validate the news. Low volume on big news = trap/manipulation.

## Topic Index
- **Accumulation**: Phase where insiders fill warehouses. Schematic in Ch5. Marked by repeated testing, low-volume tests after selling climax.
- **Anomaly**: Core VPA signal. Volume and price disagree. Wide spread + low vol = trap. Narrow spread + high vol = absorption.
- **Breakout**: Must be validated by above-average volume. Low volume breakout = fakeout. Clear water above/below congestion needed.
- **Buying Climax**: Firework display at bottom of bear trend. Insiders buying, public panic selling. Ultra-high volume, narrow spreads.
- **Candles**: Shooting Star (weakness), Hammer (strength), Long Legged Doji (indecision). Volume grades signal amplitude.
- **Cause & Effect**: Wyckoff's 2nd law. Consolidation duration = trend extent. Longer congestion = larger breakout.
- **Congestion**: ~70% of market time. Spawning ground for trends. Defined by pivot highs/lows. Volume reveals significance.
- **Delta Volume**: Bid-ask volume differential. Cumulative delta sums over time. Emerging VPA technique.
- **Distribution**: Insiders sell to public at retail prices. Follows markup phase. Precedes markdown.
- **Effort vs Result**: Wyckoff's 3rd law. Volume should match price range. Discrepancy = anomaly.
- **Equivolume**: Price-volume boxes replacing candles. X-axis = volume, Y-axis = price. Time removed.
- **Forex Volume**: Tick volume as proxy (~90% representative). Consistency matters more than absolute accuracy.
- **Insiders**: Market makers, specialists, large operators. See both sides of order flow. Cannot hide volume.
- **Manipulation**: Creating fear/greed via media/news. Not conspiracy — structural market feature.
- **Multi-TF**: 3-tier (fast/primary/slow). Ripple propagation. Dominant bias from slowest TF.
- **News**: Volume validates news. High vol = genuine reaction. Low vol = manipulation/stop-hunting.
- **Pivots**: 3-bar reversal patterns defining congestion boundaries. Dynamic trend markers.
- **Selling Climax**: Fireworks at top of bull trend. Insiders selling, public buying greedily. Ultra-high volume.
- **Stopping Volume**: Candles with deep lower wicks + high vol in downtrend. Brakes applied by insiders.
- **Support/Resistance**: Congestion zones = dense trader populations. Floors become ceilings and vice versa.
- **Testing**: Low-volume retests after accumulation/distribution confirm readiness. High-volume = failed test, more work needed.
- **Tick Charts**: Activity-based candles. Speed of formation = volume proxy. Preferred by professionals.
- **Topping Out Volume**: Deep upper wicks + high vol in uptrend. Precursor to selling climax.
- **Trap**: Fake breakout on low volume. Insiders testing sentiment before reversing.
- **VAP**: Volume At Price. Histogram on Y-axis showing volume density per price level. Visual S/R.
- **Validation**: Volume agrees with price direction and range. Normal market behaviour.
- **Warehouse**: Core analogy. Insiders as wholesalers buying at wholesale (low) and selling at retail (high).
