# Volume Price Analysis — Cheatsheet

## Volume-Price Anomaly Table

| Price Action | Volume | Interpretation | Action |
|-------------|--------|---------------|--------|
| Wide spread up | High | VALID — genuine bullish | Hold / buy |
| Wide spread up | Low | ANOMALY — trap up / test | Caution / exit longs |
| Wide spread down | High | VALID — genuine bearish | Hold / short |
| Wide spread down | Low | ANOMALY — trap down / test | Caution / exit shorts |
| Narrow spread up | Low | VALID — weak sentiment | Ignore / neutral |
| Narrow spread up | High | ANOMALY — absorption (selling into strength) | Warning — potential top |
| Narrow spread down | Low | VALID — weak sentiment | Ignore / neutral |
| Narrow spread down | High | ANOMALY — absorption (buying into weakness) | Warning — potential bottom |

## Candle → Bias Quick Reference

| Candle | Trend Position | Bias | Volume Needed |
|--------|---------------|------|--------------|
| Shooting Star | After uptrend | Bearish | High = strong, Low = minor |
| Hammer | After downtrend | Bullish | High = strong, Low = minor |
| Hanging Man | Top of uptrend | Bearish | Above average |
| Long Legged Doji | Any direction | Indecision | Avg+ valid; LOW = manipulation |
| Wide Spread | Breakout/trend | Directional | Must match spread size |
| Narrow Spread | Trend | Neutral | HIGH = anomaly/absorption |

## Entry Rules (Highest Probability)

1. **Low Volume Test after Climax**: Wait for climax to complete. Enter when low-volume test appears.
2. **Breakout from Congestion**: Wait for clear close outside zone. Volume must be above average AND rising.
3. **Shooting Star at Resistance + High Volume**: Wait for confirmation (next bar lower). Enter short.
4. **Hammer at Support + High Volume**: Wait for confirmation (next bar higher). Enter long.
5. **Stopping Volume Sequence**: Wait for all candles + hammer confirmation. Enter long.

## Exit Rules

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Wide spread candle + LOW volume in your direction | Exit immediately (trap) |
| Narrow spread + HIGH volume against your position | Exit — absorption signals exhaustion |
| Two consecutive anomalies against your position | Exit — trend weakening |
| Climax in your direction | Start taking profits; trail stop |
| Low volume test after your entry | HOLD — confirms position |

## Volume Scale Guide

| Term | Definition | Action |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Ultra-high | 3x+ above average | Climax / major signal |
| High | 2x above average | Strong signal, pay attention |
| Above average | Above recent bars | Noticeable, may be significant |
| Average | Middle of recent range | Neutral |
| Low | Below average | Weak signal, ignore unless test |
| Ultra-low | Near zero | No interest / manipulation |

## Multi-TF Checklist

- [ ] Fast TF (entry chart): what is the candle-by-candle VPA saying?
- [ ] Primary TF (trade chart): trend direction, volume validation
- [ ] Slow TF (bias chart): dominant trend — trade WITH it for lower risk
- [ ] All three aligned? → higher conviction trade
- [ ] Fast + Primary aligned but Slow against? → counter-trend trade (higher risk, shorter hold)
- [ ] Check VAP for volume density at S/R levels
- [ ] Check congestion zones ahead for natural targets/stops

## Key Principles (Memorise)

1. Volume ALWAYS tells the truth — price can lie
2. Effort vs Result must be in harmony
3. Cause (time in congestion) determines Effect (trend extent)
4. All volume is relative — same feed, compare to recent bars
5. First anomaly = alarm bell, not trade signal — wait for confirmation
6. The market NEVER reverses on a dime — be patient
7. Trading is an ART, not a science — software cannot do VPA
8. News is validated by volume, not the headline
9. Insiders cannot hide volume — follow them
10. Three premier candles + volume = complete trading system
