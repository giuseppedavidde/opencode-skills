# Volume Price Analysis — Glossary

## A
- **Accumulation**: Phase where insiders buy at wholesale prices, filling warehouses. Characterised by sideways congestion, repeated shakeouts via bad news, and high volume at lows.
- **Anomaly**: Core VPA signal. Volume and price disagree. Two types: (a) wide spread + low volume = trap/manipulation, (b) narrow spread + high volume = absorption/exhaustion.

## B
- **Buying Climax**: Firework display at bottom of bear trend. Insiders buy aggressively, public panic-sells. Ultra-high volume, narrow spreads, deep lower wicks. Signals imminent markup.

## C
- **Candle Volume Charts**: Hybrid equivolume + candlesticks. Variable-width candles reflecting volume, with wicks preserved.
- **Cause & Effect**: Wyckoff's 2nd law. The duration/extent of accumulation/distribution (cause) determines the size of the subsequent trend (effect).
- **Congestion Zone**: Sideways price region (~70% of market time). Spawning ground for trends. Defined by pivot highs/lows.
- **Cumulative Delta**: Running total of delta volume across bars. Shows persistent buying/selling pressure.

## D
- **Dark Pool**: Private exchange for large block trades. Hides volume temporarily.
- **Delta Volume**: Difference between volume traded at ask (buying) and bid (selling). Positive/negative bars show net order flow direction.
- **Distribution**: Phase where insiders sell at retail prices, emptying warehouses. Follows markup. Precedes markdown.
- **Doji, Long Legged**: Candle with open=close and long upper+lower wicks. Signifies indecision. On low volume = manipulation/stop-hunting.

## E
- **Effort vs Result**: Wyckoff's 3rd law. Volume (effort) must match price range (result). Discrepancy = anomaly.
- **Equivolume**: Chart type where X-axis = volume, Y-axis = price. Boxes replace candles. Width reflects volume.

## F
- **Falling Triangle**: Congestion pattern with lower highs + flat floor. Bearish bias — likely downside breakout.

## H
- **Hammer**: Candle with long lower wick, small body at top. Sign of strength — sellers rejected. Volume grades significance.
- **Hanging Man**: Hammer-like candle at top of bull trend. BEARISH — first sign of selling pressure.

## I
- **Insiders**: Market makers, specialists, large operators. See both sides of order flow. Cannot hide volume. Primary actors in the accumulation/distribution cycle.

## M
- **Market Manipulation**: Using news/media to create fear/greed in retail traders. Not conspiracy — structural feature of all markets.
- **Multi-TF Analysis**: Three time frames (fast/primary/slow). Fast for entry, primary for execution, slow for dominant bias.

## P
- **Pennant**: Congestion pattern with converging lower highs and higher lows. Coiled spring — explosive breakout, direction unknown until it happens.
- **Pivot High**: 3-bar pattern where centre bar has higher high AND higher low than neighbours. Defines ceiling.
- **Pivot Low**: 3-bar pattern where centre bar has lower low AND lower high than neighbours. Defines floor.

## R
- **Rising Triangle**: Congestion pattern with higher lows + flat ceiling. Bullish bias — likely upside breakout.

## S
- **Selling Climax**: Firework display at top of bull trend. Insiders sell aggressively, public buys greedily. Ultra-high volume, deep upper wicks. Signals imminent markdown.
- **Shooting Star**: Candle with long upper wick, small body at bottom. Sign of weakness — buyers rejected. Volume grades significance.
- **Stopping Volume**: Multiple candles with deep lower wicks + high volume in downtrend. Insiders applying brakes. Precursor to buying climax.
- **Support/Resistance**: Horizontal price zones from past congestion. Floors become ceilings on breakdown; ceilings become floors on breakout.

## T
- **Testing**: Low-volume retest after accumulation/distribution to confirm readiness. High volume = failed test, more work needed.
- **Tick Volume**: Proxy for actual volume in forex. ~90% representative. Consistency > absolute accuracy.
- **Topping Out Volume**: Multiple candles with deep upper wicks + high volume in uptrend. Precursor to selling climax.
- **Trap**: Fake breakout on low volume. Insiders testing sentiment before reversing.
- **Triple Bottom**: Three tests of same support level. Reversal or breakout opportunity.
- **Triple Top**: Three tests of same resistance level. Reversal or breakout opportunity.

## V
- **Validation**: Volume agrees with price direction and range. Normal market behaviour.
- **VAP (Volume At Price)**: Histogram on Y-axis showing volume density per price level. Visual S/R.
- **VPA (Volume Price Analysis)**: Analysis of volume bars relative to price bars, looking for validation or anomaly.

## W
- **Warehouse**: Core analogy. Insiders as wholesalers: buy at wholesale (low), fill warehouse, sell at retail (high), empty warehouse, repeat.
- **Wyckoff's Laws**: Three laws: Supply & Demand, Cause & Effect, Effort vs Result. Foundation of all VPA.
