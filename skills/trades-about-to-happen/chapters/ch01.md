# Chapter 1: Where to Find Trades

## Core Idea
Trades congregate around the edges of trading ranges, not in the middle. Like fishing—go where the fish live.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Six action areas**: springs, upthrusts, absorption, tests of breakouts/breakdowns, tests of high-volume vertical areas, interactions with trend lines/channels
- **Trading range dynamics**: rectangular swing ranges or coiling apexes; boundaries repeatedly tested
- **Where to Find Trades diagram** (Figure 1.1): central organizing model for the entire book

## Key Concepts
- Trade frequency is highest at range boundaries, not in the middle
- After breakouts/breakdowns, price often retests the boundary
- Lines make the story visible—without them, price action is a tangle
- Trading ranges exist on all timeframes (minutes to years)

## Key Takeaways
The first step is drawing the trading ranges. Focus on the dynamics of supply/demand at the edges. The "where" precedes the "how."
