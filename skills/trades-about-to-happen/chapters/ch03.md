# Chapter 3: The Story of the Lines

## Core Idea
Lines interconnected tell a narrative. The confluence of multiple lines at one price area marks where trades are about to happen.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Axis lines**: support becomes resistance and vice versa (Edwards & Magee concept)
- **Ice lines**: support lines at the bottom of a trading range (Bob Evans metaphor)
- **Confluence**: multiple line types converging—increases turning point probability
- **Apex / coiling**: converging trend lines narrowing to a point of equilibrium
- **Incomplete apex**: price tightening before a breakout/breakdown
- **Crisscross areas**: where earlier struggles occurred that may offer support/reaction

## Key Concepts
- The longer the timeframe, the bigger the reward from apex resolutions
- Gradual advances indicate "better quality buying" (Neill)
- Slow accumulation = weak hands to strong hands
- Terminal shakeout = end of a prolonged trading range resolved with a final washout
- The story of the lines integrates with price range, close position, and volume

## Key Takeaways
Read long-term charts to understand position within the historical framework. Zoom into daily charts for timing. Lines alone suffice as a guide, but volume adds conviction.
