# Chapter 11: Point & Figure and Renko

## Core Idea
P&F and Renko charts filter noise, reveal lines of congestion (preparation), and provide price projections. Renko is the "consummate tape reading medium."

## Frameworks Introduced
- **P&F box size + reversal ratio selection**: 1:1, 1:2, 1:3 ratios; adjusting for maximum clarity
- **Congestion line counts**: count columns in a congestion area, multiply by box size, add to low (for up) or subtract from high (for down)
- **Phased P&F projections**: conservative to maximum targets using different congestion widths
- **Renko with volume + time**: brick contains volume and duration; wave volume plotted at turning points
- **Composite operator**: Wyckoff's term for insiders/pools manipulating price to accumulate/distribute
- **Vertical format Renko**: bricks unfold vertically like P&F for better congestion visibility

## Key Concepts
- Wyckoff's workhorse was the 1-point P&F chart
- Preparation (congestion width) determines price projection magnitude
- Smaller box sizes + intraday data produce tighter patterns
- Renko bricks naturally filter noise; they "offer peace of mind"
- Brick time + volume reveal effort vs. reward in a noise-free format
- P&F/Renko/wave charts converge on the same reading of market structure

## Key Takeaways
The modern Renko chart with volume and time per brick surpasses Wyckoff's original tape-reading chart. P&F projections are guidelines, not guarantees. No trade should be established solely because of a P&F count.
