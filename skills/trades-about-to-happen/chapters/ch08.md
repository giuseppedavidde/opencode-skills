# Chapter 8: Chart Studies

## Core Idea
Integrated case studies combining lines, bar reading, volume, springs, upthrusts, and absorption. "This is how I teach traders."

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Spring on a pullback to test breakout**: the favorite setup—high-volume breakout, correction into the vertical area, spring of support line
- **Tick difference indicator**: net up-volume minus net down-volume histogram
- **Cumulative net volume trend line**: like on-balance volume but based on up/down volume difference
- **True range as volume proxy**: wide range = heavy activity, especially in forex
- **Crisscross areas**: where earlier high-volume struggles occurred that later offer support/resistance

## Key Concepts
- Openings relative to previous day's high/low/close reveal buyer/seller intent
- Net up-volume surges on narrow-range days = short covering + weak longs
- Selling climax + shortening of thrust + demand resurfacing = buy setup
- True range works as volume proxy for yield indices and forex
- Price/volume ambiguity is normal—not every setup is perfect

## Key Takeaways
The chart studies demonstrate real-time decision making. Uncertainty is part of the game. Kenko: "The most precious thing in life is its uncertainty."
