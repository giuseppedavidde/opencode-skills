# Chapter 2: Drawing Lines

## Core Idea
Lines frame price movement and reveal the struggle between buyers and sellers. Drawing them correctly is the prerequisite for reading the story.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Support/Resistance lines**: horizontal boundaries of trading ranges
- **Axis lines**: lines that alternate as support and resistance
- **Trend lines (demand/supply lines)**: dynamic support/resistance
- **Trend channels**: parallel demand line + supply line
- **Reverse trend lines/channels**: for steep trends that won't fit normal channels
- **Converging lines / wedge patterns**: converging trend + reverse trend lines

## Key Concepts
- Lines are drawn from the perspective of the last day on the chart
- Touch points add validity to a trend line
- False moves on either side of a trading range are visible only with lines
- Beyond a reverse trend line/channel often marks a trend reversal
- Broadened channels (second parallel) work when one channel isn't enough

## Key Takeaways
Lines don't guarantee anything—they focus attention. Be open-minded and willing to redraw. No single "legal" way exists; whatever works to tell the story is correct.
