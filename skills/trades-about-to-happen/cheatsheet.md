# Cheatsheet

## Cluster → Bias Mapping
| Cluster Location | Volume | Bias |
|-----------------|--------|------|
| At resistance | Expanding | Absorption → breakout |
| At resistance | Contracting | Potential upthrust |
| At support | Expanding (down) | Spring possible |
| At support | Contracting | Exhaustion → bounce |
| Mid-range | Low | Dead center—no edge |
| After trend | Climactic | Exhaustion → reversal |

## Tape-Reading Cues
| Wave Pattern | Reading | Action |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| 3+ waves up, SOT on last, vol declining | Demand tired | Go short |
| 3+ waves down, SOT on last, vol declining | Supply exhausted | Go long |
| Big effort, no reward (E/R−) | Hidden supply/demand | Prepare reversal |
| Easy move, small effort (E/R+) | Trend strong | Stay with trend |
| Down-wave duration > up-wave duration | Bears gaining | Short bias |
| Up-wave duration > down-wave duration | Bulls gaining | Long bias |
| Wave volume spike + narrow range | Climactic | Take profits |
| Heavy down-volume at support | Possible spring | Watch for reversal |

## Entry / Exit Rules
| Setup | Entry Trigger | Stop | Initial Target |
|-------|-------------|------|---------------|
| Spring | Reversal into range + confirmation | Below spring low | Prior resistance |
| Upthrust | Reversal below resistance + follow-through | Above upthrust high | Prior support |
| Secondary test | Narrow range + low vol at test level | Below test low | Breakout beyond |
| Breakout pullback | Low vol pullback to vertical area | Below base | P&F projection |
| Absorption | Close above resistance after rising supports | Below absorption area | Measured move |
| SOT intraday | Reversal after 3-4 impulses | Beyond final extreme | Opposite SOT level |
| Apex breakout | Close outside apex + vol expansion | Inside apex | Apex width projected |

## Key Quantities (Crabel)
| Pattern | Definition | Use |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| NR7 | Range narrower than previous 6 days | Impending expansion |
| 2Bar NR | Narrowest 2-day range in 20 days | Trend day expected |
| 3Bar NR | Narrowest 3-day range in 20 days | 2-5 day swing |
| ID/NR4 | Inside day + narrowest in 4 days | Highest probability |
| Hinge | Any narrow range (Wyckoff) | Precursor to swing |

## Weis's "Top 5 Mental Checks"
1. What is the position within the trading range?
2. Is this a spring, upthrust, or absorption?
3. Is the effort (volume) in proportion to the reward (progress)?
4. Are lines telling a confluence story?
5. Has the moment of recognition arrived—or am I forcing the trade?
