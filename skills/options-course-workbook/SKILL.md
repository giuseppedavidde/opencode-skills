# The Options Course Workbook
**Author**: George A. Fontanills | **Chapters**: 19 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Three Pillars of Options Trading
Every trade must account for **duration** (time to expiration), **direction** (bullish/bearish/neutral), and **magnitude** (volatility/expected move size). The interrelation of these three provides the edge needed for consistent profitability.

### Risk-First Framework
Define risk BEFORE entering every trade. Risk is the **primary input**, not an afterthought. The three possible resolutions of any options contract: (1) expire worthless, (2) be exercised by the owner, (3) be assigned to the writer.

### The Reward/Risk Ratio
The best trades offer **limited risk + unlimited reward**. The worst trades offer unlimited risk with limited reward (naked shorts). Every trade must be evaluated on: potential risk, potential reward, probability of success, time horizon.

### Delta Neutral Philosophy
Remove directional bias to profit from **magnitude of movement** alone. Combine long/short positions so total delta = 0. Adjustments bring the trade back to neutral while locking in profits.

### Volatility as Opportunity
IV is like elastic—stretching to extremes but reverting to the mean. **Buy when IV is low** (long straddles/strangles), **sell when IV is high** (credit spreads, short options). A volatility crush destroys directional winners; always check IV before entry.
