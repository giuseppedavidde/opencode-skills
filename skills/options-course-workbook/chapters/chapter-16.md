# Chapter 16: Mastering the Market

## Core Idea
Successful trading is **90% psychology and risk management, 10% strategy**. Define your risk tolerance, use a trading journal, and follow preset exit points to remove emotion from trading decisions.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Elements of a Good Investment**: Low risk, favorable risk profile, high potential return, meets time constraints and risk tolerance, understandable, within capital constraints
- **Reward/Risk Ratio**: Must account for potential risk, potential reward, probability of success, and time to return
- **Exit Strategies**: (1) Time exits (close by a certain date), (2) Profit exits (take profit at target), (3) Loss exits (stop-loss)
- **Risk Tolerance Factors**: Available investment capital (primary), knowledge level, time availability

## Key Concepts
- Fear and greed drive markets — **stick to your plan**, not your emotions
- Risk graphs reveal the full P/L profile before entry
- Use only capital you can afford to lose — emotional trading results from risking money you need
- Keep a **trading journal** recording both trade details and emotional state
- Investment objectives: interest income, tax-free securities, capital gains
- Diversification reduces portfolio risk
- Reliable knowledge and research beat analyst tips and guru advice

## Key Takeaways
1. Define **risk tolerance, time commitment, and goals** before you start trading
2. A trading journal is the single best tool for improving performance
3. Preset exit strategies remove emotion — honor them regardless of fear or greed
4. The reward/risk ratio isn't enough — also factor in **probability of success** and **time horizon**
