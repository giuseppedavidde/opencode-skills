# Options Course Workbook — Glossary

## A
- **Adjustment**: Modifying an open position by buying/selling instruments to alter risk/reward or return to delta neutral
- **American-style option**: Can be exercised at any time before expiration (stock options)
- **Arbitrage**: Risk-free profit from price discrepancies between related instruments
- **Ask**: The lowest price a seller will accept; retail traders buy at the ask
- **Assignment**: Notification to an option writer requiring fulfillment of the contract (deliver stock for calls, buy stock for puts)
- **At-the-money (ATM)**: Option whose strike price equals (or nearly equals) the current underlying price
- **Automatic exercise**: Clearing firm auto-exercises ITM options at expiration

## B
- **Bear call spread**: Sell lower-strike call + buy higher-strike call; credit, limited risk, bearish
- **Bear put spread**: Buy higher-strike put + sell lower-strike put; debit, limited risk, bearish
- **Bid**: The highest price a buyer will pay; retail traders sell at the bid
- **Bid-ask spread**: Difference between bid and ask prices
- **Black-Scholes**: Option pricing model using price, strike, time, interest rate, volatility
- **Breakeven**: Price at which a position shows neither profit nor loss
- **Bull call spread**: Buy lower-strike call + sell higher-strike call; debit, limited risk, bullish
- **Bull put spread**: Sell higher-strike put + buy lower-strike put; credit, limited risk, bullish
- **Butterfly**: Buy 1 ITM + sell 2 ATM + buy 1 OTM (same expiration); profits in range-bound markets

## C
- **Calendar spread**: Buy long-term option + sell short-term option (same strike); benefits from time decay
- **Call option**: Right to buy the underlying at a specified price within a specified time
- **Collar**: Covered call + protective put; limits both risk and reward on a stock position
- **Condor**: Like butterfly but with 4 consecutive strikes; wider profit zone
- **Contrarian**: Trading against the majority view; betting the crowd is wrong at extremes
- **Covered call**: Own stock + sell call; limited profit, downside protection = premium received
- **Covered put**: Short stock + sell put; bearish, limited profit
- **Credit spread**: The sold option is worth more than the bought option; net premium received

## D
- **Debit spread**: The bought option costs more than the sold option; net premium paid
- **Delta**: Rate of option price change per $1 move in underlying (0 to ±100). Also = probability of ITM at expiration
- **Delta neutral**: Position with total delta ≈ 0; profits from magnitude, not direction
- **Derivative**: Financial instrument whose value derives from an underlying asset (options, futures)
- **Diagonal spread**: Long + short options with different strikes AND different expirations
- **Directional trade**: Requires a specific market direction to profit

## E
- **European-style option**: Can be exercised only on the expiration date (most index options)
- **Exchange**: Marketplace for trading securities (NYSE, CBOE, ISE)
- **Exercise**: To implement the right to buy (call) or sell (put) the underlying
- **Expiration date**: Last day an option is valid; stock options expire Saturday after 3rd Friday
- **Extrinsic value**: Option price minus intrinsic value; also called time value
- **ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)**: Index-tracking fund tradeable like a stock; many are optionable

## F
- **Fill**: The price at which an order is executed
- **Futures**: Contract obligating delivery/receipt of a commodity or financial instrument at a future date

## G
- **Gamma**: Rate of delta change per $1 move in underlying; highest for ATM options

## H
- **Hedger**: Uses options/futures to protect existing positions from adverse price movement
- **Historical volatility (HV)**: Past price fluctuation measured by standard deviation (typically 21–23 days)

## I
- **Implied volatility (IV)**: Expected future volatility derived from option market prices
- **In-the-money (ITM)**: Call when underlying > strike; Put when underlying < strike
- **Intrinsic value**: The amount an option is ITM; zero for ATM/OTM options
- **Iron butterfly**: Bear call spread + bull put spread; credit, limited risk, range-bound profit

## L
- **LEAPS**: Long-term equity anticipation securities; options with expirations up to 3 years
- **Leg in**: Entering each side of a spread separately
- **Leverage**: Controlling a larger position with less capital
- **Limit order**: Order to buy/sell at a specified price or better
- **Liquidity**: Ease of converting an asset to cash; high volume = high liquidity
- **Long**: Buying a security; going long a call is bullish, going long a put is bearish

## M
- **Margin**: Cash deposit required to secure a trade; also, borrowing from broker to buy securities
- **Margin call**: Demand to deposit additional funds when equity drops below maintenance level
- **Market maker**: Dealer who provides liquidity by taking the opposite side of trades
- **Market order**: Immediate fill at current market price
- **Moneyness**: Relationship between strike price and underlying price (ITM/ATM/OTM)

## N
- **Naked option**: Selling an option without an offsetting position (unlimited risk)
- **Nondirectional**: Strategy that profits regardless of direction (straddles, strangles)

## O
- **Open interest**: Total number of outstanding options contracts
- **Option chain**: Table of all available option strikes, expirations, premiums for a given underlying
- **Out-of-the-money (OTM)**: Call when underlying < strike; Put when underlying > strike

## P
- **Position delta**: Sum of all positive and negative deltas in a hedged position
- **Premium**: The price paid by the option buyer to the option seller
- **Put option**: Right to sell the underlying at a specified price within a specified time
- **Put/call ratio**: Puts traded ÷ calls traded; contrarian sentiment indicator

## R
- **Ratio spread**: Uneven number of bought and sold options (all calls or all puts)
- **Risk graph**: Graphical representation of profit/loss across a range of underlying prices
- **Root symbol**: The underlying security identifier in an option symbol

## S
- **Short**: Selling a security; going short a call is bearish, going short a put is bullish
- **Skew**: Different implied volatility levels across different strikes and/or expirations
- **Slippage**: Difference between expected and actual transaction costs
- **Specialist**: Exchange member who maintains orderly markets in assigned stocks
- **Straddle**: ATM call + ATM put; profits from large moves in either direction
- **Strangle**: OTM call + OTM put; cheaper than straddle but needs larger move
- **Strike price**: The price at which the underlying can be bought (call) or sold (put)

## T
- **Theta**: Daily time decay; negative for long options, positive for short options
- **Time decay**: Loss of option value as expiration approaches; accelerates in last 30 days
- **Time value**: Portion of premium attributable to time remaining (extrinsic value)

## V
- **Variable delta**: Delta that changes as the underlying moves (options); contrast with fixed delta (stock)
- **Vega**: Option price change per 1% change in implied volatility
- **Vertical spread**: One bought + one sold option, same type, same expiration, different strike
- **VIX**: CBOE Volatility Index; measures SPX implied volatility; contrarian sentiment indicator
- **Volatility**: Magnitude of price fluctuation over a given period
- **Volatility crush**: Sharp IV drop after a known event (earnings, FDA decision)
- **Volume**: Number of shares or contracts traded in a given period

## W
- **Wasting asset**: An investment that loses value over time (options due to theta)
