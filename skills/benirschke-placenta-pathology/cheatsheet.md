## Gross lesion differential
| Lesion | Appearance | Microscopy |
|--|--|--|
| Infarct | Firm, granular, pale; rim | Coagulative necrosis; any preterm, >5% at term |
| Intervillous thrombus | Laminated, tan or red; basal | Pushes villi aside; no infarction |
| Perivillous fibrin | White plaque | MPFD if >50% of one slide |
| Retroplacental hematoma | Dark adherent clot | Compressed, infarcted villi |
| Breus' mole | Raised chorionic surface | Subchorionic thrombohematoma; 1:1200 |
| Chorangioma | Solid nodule | Hemangioma; >4 cm giant |

## Amsterdam criteria
| Category | Lesions |
|--|--|
| MVM | Hypoplasia <10th centile, infarcts, DVH, accelerated maturation, decidual arteriopathy |
| FVM | Thrombi, avascular villi, VSK, intimal fibrin cushions, stem-vessel obliteration |
| VUE | Lymphocytic villitis; low grade <10 villi/focus, high grade >10 |
| MIR | Subchorionitis, chorionitis, chorioamnionitis, necrotizing |
| FIR | Phlebitis, arteritis, necrotizing funisitis |
| Other | Chorangiosis, delayed villous maturation, CIV, MPFD/MFI |

High-grade FVM: >1 avascular focus (≥45 over 3 sections or ≥15/section), or ≥2 thrombi.

## Sampling
| Specimen | Minimum |
|--|--|
| Villous tissue | 3 pieces, fetal and maternal surfaces |
| Umbilical cord | 2 sections |
| Membrane roll | 1–2 sections |
| Slides | 4: three villous, one cord/roll |

## Prose
- Weigh the trimmed disc; measure three dimensions; describe insertion, coiling, lesions.
- MVM is maternal, FVM fetal. Weight alone is not a diagnosis.
- Grade each lesion and give percentage involved.
- Preterm infarcts always significant; term infarcts >5% need documentation.
