# Glossary — Benirschke's Pathology of the Human Placenta

Alphabetical. Numbers are the thresholds used in the book.

**Abruptio placentae** — Premature separation after 20 weeks; 0.5–1.0% of births; 11-fold recurrence.

**Accelerated villous maturation** — Hypermature small villi with syncytial knots >33%; ≥30% of a full-thickness section.

**Accreta spectrum** — Accreta, increta, percreta; 1/533 births; previa plus prior cesarean 11–61%.

**Acute atherosis** — Spiral-artery foam cells, fibrinoid necrosis, medial degeneration; preeclampsia and FGR.

**Amsterdam criteria** — Consensus definitions for MVM, FVM, VUE and maternal/fetal inflammatory responses.

**Avascular villi** — Hyalinized, vessel-free villi with viable trophoblast; direct evidence of fetal thrombosis.

**Basal plate** — Maternal placental surface: decidua basalis, Nitabuch's fibrinoid, spiral-artery openings.

**Battledore placenta** — Marginal cord insertion less than 1 cm from the edge; about 7% at term.

**Bilobed placenta** — Two discs joined by membranes; 0.3–0.9%.

**Breus' mole** — Massive subchorionic thrombohematoma; 1:1200 placentas; monosomy X association.

**Chorangioma (chorioangioma)** — Placental hemangioma; >4 cm is "giant" and risks hydrops and thrombocytopenia.

**Chorangiomatosis** — Diffuse stem-villous capillary proliferation; 0.55%; often with chorangioma.

**Chorangiosis (chorangiotic change)** — More than 10 capillaries in more than 10 terminal villi in ≥10 fields at 10×; 5.5–7%.

**Chorioamnionitis** — Acute neutrophil inflammation of chorion and amnion; maternal and fetal stages separate.

**Chorionic plate** — Fetal placental surface bearing umbilical vessel branches, covered by amnion.

**Chronic histiocytic intervillositis** — Intervillous histiocytes with fibrinoid and agglutinated villi; recurrent loss.

**Chronic villitis of unknown etiology** — Lymphocytic villitis in 5–15% of term placentas; recurrence 10–17%; high-grade >10 villi.

**Circummarginate placenta** — Inward membrane insertion without a plicated ring; 1.8–25.5%.

**Circumvallate placenta** — Inward membrane insertion with circumferential fibrin ring; 0.62–5.25%.

**Complete hydatidiform mole** — Androgenetic diploid, p57-negative, no fetus; persistent GTD 8.9–20%.

**Cotyledon** — One maternal-fetal circulatory unit with one artery and one vein.

**Decidual arteriopathy** — Deficient remodeling, fibrinoid necrosis, atherosis, mural hypertrophy, thrombosis; best in membrane-roll decidua.

**Delayed villous maturation** — Uniform immaturity after 34 weeks; ≥30% of a full-thickness section.

**Distal villous hypoplasia** — Sparse terminal villi with wide intervillous spaces; >30% of distal villi; FGR before 32 weeks.

**Erythroblastosis fetalis** — Hydropic anemic fetus with nucleated red cells; pale, enlarged, immature placenta.

**Fetal vascular malperfusion** — Thrombi, avascular villi, stromal-vascular karyorrhexis, intimal fibrin cushions; segmental or global.

**Fibrinoid necrosis** — Waxy, cell-free vessel-wall replacement in decidual arteriopathy.

**Hofbauer cells** — Fetal villous macrophages, 10–35 µm, CD14/CD68-positive, vacuolated.

**Hydrops fetalis** — Two or more fetal fluid collections; immune or non-immune; often placentomegaly.

**Hypercoiled cord** — More than 3 coils per 10 cm or >90th centile; 6–21%; FVM and demise.

**Hypocoiled cord** — Less than 1 coil per 10 cm or <10th centile; kinking and nuchal cord.

**Infarct** — Coagulative villous necrosis; any preterm, or >5% of term non-peripheral parenchyma.

**Interhemal membrane** — Maternal-fetal barrier, 2–3 µm at vasculosyncytial membranes after 32 weeks.

**Intervillous thrombosis** — Laminated intervillous clot displacing villi; not infarction.

**Langhans cells** — Villous cytotrophoblast, the proliferating stem-cell layer beneath syncytiotrophoblast.

**Marginal insertion** — Cord less than 1 cm from the edge; 6.89% of 194,365 deliveries.

**Massive perivillous fibrinoid deposition** — Perivillous fibrinoid >50% of one slide; small firm placenta; recurrence.

**Maternal floor infarction** — Basal perivillous fibrinoid with a thick yellow floor; recurrent.

**Maternal vascular malperfusion** — Hypoplasia <10th centile, infarcts, DVH, accelerated maturation, decidual arteriopathy.

**Molar pregnancy** — Complete, partial, invasive or metastatic mole; diagnosed by morphology, ploidy, p57.

**Nitabuch's stria** — Fibrinoid layer at the basal plate; deficient in accreta.

**Partial hydatidiform mole** — Diandric triploid, p57-positive, fetus often present; persistent GTD 0.2–4%.

**Perivillous fibrin deposition** — Fibrin around villi; normal in small amounts, pathologic when diffuse.

**Placenta membranacea** — Thin diffuse placenta covering the chorionic sac; bleeding, retention.

**Placenta previa** — Implantation over or near the internal os; main accreta risk factor.

**Placental mesenchymal dysplasia** — Cystic edematous stem villi, normal trophoblast; about 1/4000; Beckwith-Wiedemann.

**Placental weight** — 470 g at 40 weeks, 22 cm diameter, 2.5 cm thick; <10th centile is hypoplasia.

**Retained products of conception** — Placental tissue left after delivery or abortion; hemorrhage, infection, polyp.

**Retroplacental hematoma** — Blood between basal plate and uterus; compresses and infarcts villi.

**Single umbilical artery** — 0.63% of deliveries, 1.92% of autopsies, 3.9% of twins; left absent in 73%.

**Spiral artery remodeling** — Trophoblast conversion to low-resistance vessels; myometrial invasion by 15 weeks.

**Succenturiate lobe (accessory lobe)** — Separate lobe on membranes only; 5–6% of placentas; one-third infarcted.

**Syncytial knots** — Nuclear aggregates; increased >20% before 34 weeks, >30% of 100 villi at term.

**Transplacental hemorrhage** — Fetal blood in maternal circulation; >50 mL large; 7.5% hydropic.

**Twin-twin transfusion syndrome** — Deep unbalanced anastomoses in monochorionic placenta; Quintero I–IV; cerebral palsy 22%.

**Umbilical cord coiling index** — Coils divided by length; normal 0.2 ± 0.1/cm.

**Vasa previa** — Membranous vessels over the internal os; 1 in 50 velamentous; mortality 58–73%.

**Velamentous insertion** — Cord inserts on membranes; 1.11% of 194,365 singleton deliveries.

**Villous maturation** — Terminal-villous pattern for gestational age; DVH, delayed or accelerated.

**Villous stromal-vascular karyorrhexis** — Karyorrhexis, red-cell extravasation, septation after fetal thrombosis; foci ≤10 or >10 villi.
