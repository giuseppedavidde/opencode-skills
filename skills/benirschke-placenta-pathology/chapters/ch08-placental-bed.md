# Chapter 8: The Placental Bed

## Core Idea
The placental bed is the area of uterine wall underlying the placenta and the site of the most complex maternal-fetal cellular interactions. The decidua is a prerequisite for controlled invasion: without it, as in tubal pregnancy, invasion is excessive. Extravillous trophoblast arises from cytotrophoblast cell columns, migrates as interstitial and endovascular populations, and remodels spiral arteries as far as the inner third of the myometrium. Invasion depth is greatest centrally and least peripherally, so the bed is not uniform.

## Frameworks Introduced

**Regional decidua.** Basalis beneath the implantation site, capsularis over the superficial pole, parietalis for the rest of the wall. Capsularis and parietalis fuse at 12–14 weeks.

**Extravillous subsets.** Interstitial cells migrate through stroma. Endovascular cells invade spiral arteries and migrate down the lumen; they express CD56 and are loosely linked by desmosomes, forming intercellular channels. Multinucleated giant cells mark the extreme range of interstitial invasion.

**Extravillous immunophenotype.** Cytokeratin positive, HLA-G positive, c-erbB-2 positive, EGF receptor negative, E-cadherin negative. Villous cytotrophoblast is HLA-G negative.

**Proliferation markers.** Tritiated thymidine and BrdU label S phase only and are restricted to the proximal one to three layers. Ki-67/MIB-1 labels all phases except G0 and G1 and is positive in two to six layers. PCNA has a 20-hour half-life and may label cells that already left the cycle.

## Key Concepts
- First trimester: about 70% of leukocytes are uterine natural killer (uNK) cells, making up about 30% of stromal cells. Macrophages are about 20% of leukocytes; T cells are sparse and B cells few.
- By term, uNK cells are only about 40% of CD45-positive cells and have lost their granules.
- Decidualization begins on day 23 of the cycle, around the spiral arteries. Decidual cells secrete laminin, type IV collagen, fibronectin, and heparan sulfate proteoglycans.
- Decidua basalis measures 5–6 mm at 4 weeks and about 1 mm by 15–20 weeks.
- The cytotrophoblastic shell is four to five cells thick. Failure of shell development allows premature onset of the maternal circulation and miscarriage from oxidative stress.
- Interstitial trophoblast reaches the inner third of the myometrium in normal pregnancy. At ectopic sites such as the fallopian tube, invasion is unrestricted and reaches the serosa.
- Placental bed giant cells contain two to tens of euchromatic nuclei. Human invasive extravillous trophoblast is diploid to tetraploid.
- Maternal KIR AA with fetal HLA-C2 gives strong inhibitory signalling to uNK cells and is associated with miscarriage, growth restriction, and pre-eclampsia.
- MMP-2 predominates early; a switch to MMP-9 occurs toward the end of the first trimester. TIMP-1 secretion rises between weeks 6 and 11.
- Converted spiral arteries are re-endothelialized by maternal cells, not trophoblast.
- Invasion is greatest centrally and minimal peripherally, where spiral arteries show little remodelling.
- Shallow invasion is associated with early pregnancy loss and pre-eclampsia; excessive invasion produces placental retention and the accreta spectrum.

## Anti-patterns
- Equating decidual necrosis with infection; it has no clear clinical significance.
- Diagnosing extravillous trophoblast on morphology alone; use cytokeratin with HLA-G and loss of EGFR and E-cadherin.
- Treating Ki-67 or PCNA positivity in endovascular trophoblast as proof of proliferation.
- Assuming invasion is uniform across the bed.
- Assuming converted spiral artery endothelium is trophoblastic.
- Interpreting macrophage counts in pre-eclampsia from mixed sample types; Redline found no change in a larger study.

## Key Takeaways
1. Sample the placental bed centrally; peripheral biopsies underestimate invasion.
2. Identify extravillous trophoblast with cytokeratin, HLA-G, and loss of EGFR and E-cadherin.
3. Expect invasion to stop at the inner third of the myometrium; deeper invasion suggests accreta spectrum.
4. Prefer Ki-67 to PCNA if a proliferation marker is needed.
5. Treat uNK and KIR data as risk modifiers, not diagnostic tests.
6. Treat shell deficiency as a mechanism of first-trimester miscarriage.
