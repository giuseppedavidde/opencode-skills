# Glossary: Crypto Technical Analysis Terms

## Core Trading Terms
- **Technical Analysis (TA)**: Using historical price patterns to predict future movements
- **Fundamental Analysis (FA)**: Assessing intrinsic value through valuation techniques
- **Hype Analysis**: Trading based on social trends, sentiment, and momentum
- **Support**: Price level where buying pressure is concentrated; downtrend pauses
- **Resistance**: Price level where selling pressure is concentrated; uptrend pauses
- **Trend**: Direction of price movement (uptrend, downtrend, sideways)
- **Breakout**: Price moves above resistance or below support
- **Reversal**: Change in price direction (uptrend → downtrend or vice versa)
- **Continuation Pattern**: Signals existing trend will continue
- **False Signal / Head Fake**: Breakout that reverses immediately, trapping traders

## Indicator Terms
- **Leading Indicator**: Predicts future price movement (fast, less accurate)
- **Lagging Indicator**: Confirms movement after it starts (slow, more accurate)
- **Oscillator**: Indicator varying within upper/lower bounds (0-100 typical)
- **Overbought**: Oscillator near upper limit; asset likely overvalued, sell signal
- **Oversold**: Oscillator near lower limit; asset likely undervalued, buy signal
- **Divergence**: Price and oscillator move in opposite directions; strong signal
- **Moving Average (MA)**: Smoothed price line over specified period
- **SMA** (Simple Moving Average): Equal weight to all periods
- **EMA** (Exponential Moving Average): Greater weight to recent periods
- **RSI** (Relative Strength Index): 0-100 momentum oscillator; 70/30 thresholds
- **MACD** (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): 12/26 EMA difference + 9-day signal
- **Stochastic Oscillator**: Leading momentum; 80/20 thresholds
- **Bollinger Bands**: Price envelope showing overbought/oversold via standard deviation
- **Ichimoku Cloud**: Five-line combined trend/momentum/S/R indicator
- **Parabolic SAR**: Dots indicating trend direction and reversals
- **Fibonacci Retracement**: S/R levels at 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%

## Charting Terms
- **Candlestick**: OHLC display with real body (open/close) and wicks (high/low)
- **Real Body**: Difference between open and close price
- **Wick/Shadow**: Line showing high/low of period
- **Bullish Candle**: Close > open (typically green)
- **Bearish Candle**: Close < open (typically red)
- **Renko Chart**: Bricks showing only price movement; ignores time
- **Heikin-Ashi**: Smoothed candlestick variant averaging two periods
- **Point & Figure**: X (rising) and O (falling) columns; ignores time

## Pattern Terms
- **Head and Shoulders**: Three-peak bearish reversal pattern (~85% accuracy)
- **Inverse H&S**: Three-trough bullish reversal pattern
- **Cup and Handle**: Bullish continuation; U-shaped cup + brief pullback handle
- **Engulfing**: Second candle body fully covers first; bullish or bearish reversal
- **Doji**: Open/close nearly equal; signals indecision/reversal
- **Hammer**: Short body + long lower wick at downtrend bottom; bullish reversal
- **Shooting Star**: Small lower body + long upper wick at uptrend top; bearish
- **Morning Star**: Three-candle bullish reversal (red → short → green)
- **Evening Star**: Three-candle bearish reversal (green → short → red)
- **Pennant**: Symmetrical triangle after sharp move; continuation
- **Flag**: Parallel channel after sharp move; continuation

## Crypto-Specific Terms
- **Altcoin**: Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin
- **Market Cap**: Price × Circulating Supply; total market value
- **Circulating Supply**: Number of publicly available coins
- **Maximum Supply**: Maximum coins that can ever exist
- **Halving**: Mining rewards cut in half (every 4 years for Bitcoin)
- **Burning**: Coins permanently removed from circulation (eater address)
- **Deflationary Asset**: Supply decreases over time; each unit increases in value
- **Inflationary Asset**: Supply increases over time; each unit decreases in value
- **On-Chain Metrics**: Blockchain data (active addresses, hash rate, transaction volume)
- **Fear & Greed Index**: 0-100 sentiment gauge for crypto market
- **Big-Brother Technique**: New coin resembling successful project with minor positive changes
- **HFT** (High-Frequency Trading): Algorithmic trading at massive scale/speed
- **Slippage**: Difference between expected and actual trade price
- **Stop-Loss**: Automatic sell order at predetermined price to limit losses
- **Trailing Stop-Loss**: Stop-loss that follows price upward
- **Long**: Betting price will go up (buy)
- **Short**: Betting price will go down (sell)

## Psychological Terms
- **Sunk Cost Fallacy**: Continuing because time/money already invested
- **Confirmation Bias**: Seeking evidence supporting existing beliefs
- **Anchoring Bias**: Initial information skews all subsequent judgment
- **Availability Bias**: Overweighting easily recalled information
- **Martingale Bias**: Doubling down on losing investments
- **Hot-Hand Fallacy**: Streaks predict future outcomes
- **Endowment Effect**: Overvaluing assets you own
- **Outcome Bias**: Judging by result, not process
