# Chapter 11: Investing & Psychology

## Core Idea
Money decisions are deeply emotional. Psychology affects both individual trading decisions and overall market movements (bull = greed/euphoria, bear = anxiety/fear). Understanding biases is as important as understanding indicators.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Trading Rules System**: Online rules (max trade size) + Offline rules (stop after 3 wins/losses)
- **Biases Awareness Framework**: Identify → Acknowledge → Counteract via strategy
- **Stop-Loss Discipline**: Three consecutive wins or losses = stop trading for the day

## Key Concepts
- **When to Stop Trading**: After 3 consecutive wins (emotional high) or 3 losses (emotional low); impaired decision-making
- **Have a Mantra**: "I am calm, focused, and confident"; reset emotional state
- **Take Breaks**: Walk, exercise, meditate—reset before making decisions
- **Establish Rules**: Rule-based trading is the single most important habit
- **Trade Size**: Never change position size based on feelings; only based on risk analysis
- **Trading Journal**: Log entry/exit, asset, notes—provides perspective and data for improvement
- **10 Biases/Fallacies**:
  - **Intuition**: Experienced traders develop valid intuition; beginners confuse it with greed/fear
  - **Sunk Cost**: Continuing because time/money already invested; cut losses
  - **Social Proof**: "Everyone's buying it"—hype distorts risk perception
  - **Availability**: Overweighting information that comes easily to mind
  - **Martingale**: Doubling down on losers; mathematically flawed in trading
  - **Hot-Hand**: Streaks don't predict future outcomes
  - **Anchoring**: First impression anchors all subsequent judgment
  - **Confirmation**: Seek evidence supporting existing beliefs
  - **Conservatism**: Under-revising beliefs when new data contradicts
  - **Outcome**: Judging by result, not process
  - **Endowment Effect**: Overvaluing assets you own
  - **Framing**: Comparing relative vs absolute data
  - **Loss Aversion**: Fear of loss causes more loss

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Crypto's 24/7 nature amplifies emotional trading (constant price checks)
- Social media echo chambers (Telegram, Discord, Twitter) intensify confirmation bias
- Round-number anchoring is extremely strong in crypto ($50K, $100K BTC)
- Sunk cost fallacy is deadly in crypto bear markets (holding to zero)
- Fear & Greed cycles are more extreme than in traditional markets

## Key Takeaways
- Strong strategy + self-awareness + experience = best defense against biases
- Stop after 3 consecutive wins or losses—it's a rule, not a suggestion
- A trading journal is your best tool for improvement
- Awareness alone doesn't prevent biases; rule-based systems do
