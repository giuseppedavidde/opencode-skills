# Chapter 10: Oscillators

## Core Idea
Oscillators vary within defined upper/lower bounds to identify overbought (sell) and oversold (buy) conditions. 15 oscillators are covered, ranked by popularity, with RSI, MACD, and Stochastic as the essential three.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Momentum Oscillation**: All oscillators measure momentum relative to historical ranges
- **Divergence**: Price moves opposite to oscillator—strongest signal type (bullish divergence = price down, oscillator up)
- **Centerline Crossovers**: Above zero = bullish momentum; below zero = bearish
- **Signal Line Crossovers**: Oscillator crossing its smoothed signal line = trade signal

## Key Concepts
- **RSI** (Relative Strength Index): 0-100 range; >70 overbought (sell), <30 oversold (buy); crypto may need adjusted thresholds (40/60)
- **MACD**: 12/26 EMA difference + 9-day signal line + histogram; crossover signals; most popular oscillator
- **Stochastic**: 0-100; >80 overbought, <20 oversold; leading indicator (predicts before price moves)
- **TSI** (True Strength Index): Momentum + signal line crossovers + centerline; divergence is key signal
- **MFI** (Money Flow Index): Volume-weighted RSI; >80 overbought, <20 oversold; some use 90/10 to reduce false signals
- **CCI** (Commodity Channel Index): Unbounded; >+100 overbought, <-100 oversold; lagging
- **Klinger Oscillator**: Volume-force vs EMAs; identifies long-term money flow
- **PPO**: Percentage-based MACD variant; comparable across different-priced assets
- **PVO**: Volume-based PPO; measures volume above/below average
- **Chaikin Oscillator**: ADL momentum; centerline crossovers = buy/sell
- **SMIEO**: TSI-based histogram; confirm trend strength
- **DPO**: Removes short-term trends to reveal cycles
- **CMO**: -100 to +100; >50 overbought, <-50 oversold
- **UO** (Ultimate Oscillator): Three-period weighted momentum; reduces false signals
- **AO** (Awesome Oscillator): Histogram; zero-line crossovers = direction; divergence = reversal

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Crypto volatility means standard RSI 30/70 thresholds may need adjustment (try 25/75 or 40/60)
- MACD is widely used on all crypto timeframes; works best on 1H-4H
- Stochastic's 80/20 works better for crypto than RSI's 30/70
- MFI is underused in crypto but valuable—volume + price gives clearer signals in manipulated markets
- Divergence signals are more reliable in crypto than simple overbought/oversold readings

## Key Takeaways
- RSI + MACD + Stochastic is the essential oscillator trio for crypto
- Divergence is the strongest single signal across all oscillators
- Adjust overbought/oversold thresholds to match crypto's higher volatility
- Combine one leading (Stochastic) + one lagging (MACD) for best results
