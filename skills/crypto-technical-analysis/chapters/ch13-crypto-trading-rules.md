# Chapter 13: Crypto Trading Rules

## Core Idea
Five essential rules that save money and preserve emotional stability. These are philosophical principles, not technical strategies—they govern how you approach the market.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Cyclical Market Framework**: Bitcoin operates in 4-year cycles (halving → bull run → crash → accumulation); nothing lasts forever
- **Process > Outcome**: Judge decisions by the process, not the result
- **Rule-Based Trading**: Predefined rules remove emotional decision-making

## Key Concepts
- **Nothing Lasts Forever**: Bull runs end in crashes; crashes lead to new bull runs. Bitcoin cycle pattern: massive uptrend, crash to higher lows. Historical: 2017 (15x), 2019 (3x), 2020-2021 (10x+). Holding through crashes historically pays off.
- **No Woulda, Shoulda, Coulda**: After a bad trade, spend a few minutes learning, then move on. Don't dwell on what you would/should/could have done.
- **Don't Be Emotional**: The market doesn't care how you feel. Emotions are the antithesis of technical trading. Losses are learning opportunities.
- **Diversify**: Recommended split: BTC, ETH/alternatives, altcoins, cash. Example allocation: 35/25/30/10. Diversification counters crypto's extreme risk.
- **Prices Don't Matter**: Supply is arbitrary (1T vs 10M coins). Market cap matters, not price. Fractional shares make price irrelevant. Don't buy because it's "cheap."

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Bitcoin's 4-year halving cycle is unique to crypto and fundamental to trading strategy
- Crypto diversification is different from stocks—some traders go all-in on 2-3 top-50 coins
- Price anchoring (thinking $0.50 is "cheap" and $50,000 is "expensive") is the most common beginner mistake
- Emotional trading is amplified by 24/7 access, social media, and extreme volatility
- Tax implications: short-term (held <1 year) vs long-term gains; crypto treated as property by IRS

## Key Takeaways
- Nothing lasts forever: bull and bear markets are both temporary
- Don't be emotional: stick to your strategy regardless of market conditions
- Diversify across BTC, ETH, and altcoins (never 100% in one asset)
- Price is meaningless without market cap context
- Learn from losses quickly, then move on without regret
