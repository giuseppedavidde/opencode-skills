# Chapter 7: Chart Patterns

## Core Idea
Chart patterns are recognizable formations that predict future price action. They operate on wider timeframes than candlestick patterns and form from general price action shapes.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Pattern Classification**: All patterns are Bullish, Bearish, Continuation, or Reversal
- **Essential 8**: Triangles | Rectangles | Head & Shoulders | Double/Triple Top/Bottom | Doji | Morning/Evening Star | Abandoned Baby | Two Black Gapping
- **Breakout Confirmation**: Wait for volume spike + closes beyond trendline to avoid head fakes

## Key Concepts
- **Triangles**: Ascending (bullish), Descending (bearish), Symmetrical (either direction); continuation patterns
- **Price Channel**: Parallel trendlines forming S/R; breakouts signal direction
- **Rounding Bottom** (Cup & Handle): U-shaped reversal; handle = brief pullback before breakout; weeks/months to form
- **Pennant & Flag**: Post-jump consolidation; triangle (pennant) or parallel lines (flag); continue pre-formation direction
- **Head & Shoulders**: Three peaks—middle highest; 85% accuracy for bearish reversals
- **Inverse H&S**: Bullish reversal; breakout above neckline
- **Double/Triple Top/Bottom**: M/W formations; 75-80% accuracy; triple patterns more reliable
- **Bump & Run**: Three phases (lead-in, bump, rollover); signals reversals after excessive moves

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Patterns form faster in crypto due to 24/7 trading and higher volatility
- Head & Shoulders is common at Bitcoin cycle tops
- Cup & Handle patterns will become more common as crypto matures
- Volume confirmation is extra important—crypto has more false breakouts than stocks
- Pattern reliability may differ: crypto less efficient = more pronounced trends but also more fakeouts

## Key Takeaways
- Triangles are the most common crypto pattern; ascending = bullish, descending = bearish
- Head & Shoulders (~85% accuracy) is the most reliable pattern
- Always wait for breakout confirmation—don't trade anticipation in crypto
- Wider timeframe patterns (cup & handle) are more reliable in crypto's noise
