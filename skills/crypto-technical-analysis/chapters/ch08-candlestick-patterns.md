# Chapter 8: Candlestick Patterns

## Core Idea
Candlestick patterns are short-term formations (1-5 candles) specific to candlestick charts. They rely on body and wick geometry to predict immediate price movement. 50 of the most popular patterns are covered.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Single-Candle Patterns**: Hammer, Inverse Hammer, Hanging Man, Shooting Star, Doji (5 types), Marubozu, Spinning Top
- **Two-Candle Patterns**: Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Piercing Line, Tweezer Bottom/Top, Harami, Harami Cross, Dark Cloud Cover
- **Three-Candle Patterns**: Morning/Evening Star, Three White Soldiers, Three Black Crows, Abandoned Baby, Doji Star, Upside/Downside Tasuki Gap, Stick Sandwich
- **Five-Candle Patterns**: Rising/Falling Three Methods, Three Line Strike

## Key Concepts
- **Hammer**: Short body + long lower wick at downtrend bottom; bullish reversal
- **Hanging Man**: Same shape as hammer but at uptrend top; bearish reversal
- **Engulfing**: Second candle body completely covers first; Bullish (red→green) or Bearish (green→red)
- **Doji Family**: Open/close nearly equal; Standard, Long-Legged, Dragonfly (bullish), Gravestone (bearish), Four-Price
- **Morning Star**: Three-candle bullish reversal (long red → short body → long green)
- **Evening Star**: Three-candle bearish reversal (long green → short body → long red)
- **Marubozu**: No wicks; strong continuation signal
- **Harami**: Small candle within previous large candle; "pregnant" pattern; reversal signal
- **Abandoned Baby**: Rare three-bar reversal with doji gap; very reliable
- **Three White Soldiers**: Three consecutive green candles; end of downtrend

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Rapid 24/7 movement means candlestick patterns form more frequently but with more false signals
- Dojis are especially common in crypto due to frequent indecision/consolidation phases
- Engulfing patterns work well on higher timeframes (4H+) in crypto; lower timeframes are noisy
- Three White Soldiers and Three Black Crows are more reliable in crypto than single-candle patterns
- Volume confirmation critical—crypto wicks can be manipulated on low-volume candles

## Key Takeaways
- Hammer + Morning Star = strong bullish reversal confirmation
- Doji at S/R = potential reversal; combine with next candle for confirmation
- Engulfing patterns require full body coverage (wicks don't count)
- Don't trade patterns alone—use oscillators for confirmation
- Higher timeframes = more reliable patterns in crypto
