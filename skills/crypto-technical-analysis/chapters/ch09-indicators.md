# Chapter 9: Indicators

## Core Idea
Indicators are mathematical calculations applied to price/volume data. This chapter covers major non-oscillator indicators: Ichimoku Cloud, Bollinger Bands, Parabolic SAR, Gann tools, Pitchfork, Fibonacci, and Moving Averages.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Ichimoku Cloud**: Five-line system (Tenkan-Sen, Kijun-Sen, Senkou A/B, Chikou Span) combining momentum, trend, S/R into one indicator
- **Bollinger Band Width (BBW)**: Difference between upper/lower bands ÷ middle band; single-line overbought/oversold gauge
- **Fibonacci Tool Suite**: Retracement (6 levels), Fans (angle lines), Arcs (half-circles), Wedge, Channel, Circles, Time Zones
- **Gann Theory**: Market cycles + geometry + time → price prediction

## Key Concepts
- **Ichimoku Signals**: Price above cloud = bullish; Tenkan crosses Kijun above cloud = strong buy; Chikou crossing price = signal
- **Bollinger Bands**: ~90% of price action between bands; hugging upper = overbought, hugging lower = oversold; breakouts beyond bands = major moves
- **Parabolic SAR**: Dots below price = bullish, above = bearish; dots crossing price = trend reversal; works best in trending (not choppy) markets
- **Gann Fan**: 9 angles from 1:8 (82.5°) to 8:1 (7.5°); central 1:1 = 45° main S/R; other angles = lesser S/R
- **Andrews' Pitchfork**: Three points → median line + two outer lines = trend channels; Schiff variant adjusts origin point
- **Fibonacci Retracement**: Draw between low→high (or high→low); S/R at 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%
- **Moving Averages**: SMA vs EMA; crossovers signal momentum changes; common periods: 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Ichimoku Cloud particularly useful in crypto for combining multiple indicators into one view
- Fibonacci levels are self-fulfilling in crypto—many traders watch the same levels
- Parabolic SAR works well in crypto trends but fails in consolidation (common in crypto)
- Bollinger Bands need wider settings for crypto due to higher volatility (2.5+ std dev)
- Moving average crossovers (50/200) are widely watched in crypto for golden/death crosses
- Gann tools are less commonly used in crypto but valuable for longer-term cycle analysis

## Key Takeaways
- Ichimoku Cloud provides a complete TA picture in one indicator
- Fibonacci retracement levels are the most widely-used crypto S/R tool (61.8% key)
- Bollinger Bands + RSI = strong overbought/oversold combo for crypto
- MA crossovers (50/200) signal major trend changes; reliable in crypto's cyclical markets
