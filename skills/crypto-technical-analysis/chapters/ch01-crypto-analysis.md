# Chapter 1: Crypto Analysis

## Core Idea
Crypto analysis is the foundation for all trading decisions. The chapter debunks three major myths (easy money, risk-free trading, 100% accuracy) and establishes that crypto requires effort, carries extreme risk matched to reward, and that TA is probabilistic—nothing is ever certain.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Two Types of Crypto Analysis**: Technical (historical patterns) vs Fundamental (intrinsic value)
- **Risk-Return Tradeoff**: Higher potential reward = proportionally higher risk; crypto sits at the extreme end
- **Myth-Busting Framework**: Identify common misconceptions, examine underlying assumptions, replace with probability-based thinking

## Key Concepts
- **Easy Money Myth**: Current bull runs don't predict future performance; principles > luck
- **Risk-Free Myth**: Risk-return tradeoff means crypto is extremely risky despite social media narratives
- **100% Accurate Myth**: TA is probability-based (60-80% accuracy is excellent); no strategy works all the time
- **Learning from losses**: Being wrong is fine; historical profitability over time is what matters

## Crypto-Specific Considerations
- Crypto market is less efficient than stocks, creating more TA opportunities but also more noise
- Social media amplifies risk perception distortion (everyone seems to be making money)
- The extreme volatility means even 60% accurate strategies can be highly profitable due to large moves
- Newer market means less historical data for backtesting; patterns may be less reliable than in equities

## Key Takeaways
- TA is a bet on probability, not certainty
- Market conditions change; past success doesn't guarantee future results
- Risk management is more important than prediction accuracy
