# Crypto Trading Patterns Reference

## Chart Patterns

### Ascending Triangle
- **When to use**: During an uptrend; horizontal resistance + rising support
- **How**: Buy when 2 bars close above resistance line; stop-loss at or just below breakout line
- **Trade-offs**: ~75% reliable; false breakouts common in crypto; volume confirmation needed
- **Crypto notes**: Forms frequently on 4H-1D timeframes; works well with RSI divergence

### Descending Triangle
- **When to use**: During a downtrend; horizontal support + falling resistance
- **How**: Short when 2 bars close below support; stop-loss at or just above breakout line
- **Trade-offs**: ~75% reliable; can be a consolidation before continuation
- **Crypto notes**: Common in crypto bear markets; riskier in low-volume alts

### Symmetrical Triangle
- **When to use**: During any trend; shrinking price range with lower highs/higher lows
- **How**: Wait for breakout direction confirmed by volume spike + 2 closes beyond trendline
- **Trade-offs**: No directional bias; tends to continue pre-formation trend; ~70% reliability
- **Crypto notes**: Most common triangle in crypto; 24/7 noise creates many fakeouts

### Head and Shoulders
- **When to use**: After a prolonged uptrend; 3 peaks with middle highest
- **How**: Enter at neckline breakout; stop-loss above right shoulder; target = neckline - head height
- **Trade-offs**: ~85% accuracy (highest of all patterns); takes weeks/months to form
- **Crypto notes**: Very reliable at Bitcoin cycle tops; inverse H&S reliable at bottoms

### Cup and Handle
- **When to use**: After consolidation; U-shaped cup followed by downward handle
- **How**: Buy on handle breakout above cup rim; stop-loss below handle low
- **Trade-offs**: Weeks to months to form; V-shaped bottoms are less reliable than U-shaped
- **Crypto notes**: Becoming more common as crypto matures; works on weekly timeframes

### Double Top / Double Bottom
- **When to use**: Two failed breakouts at same price level (M for top, W for bottom)
- **How**: Enter on confirmation (break below valley for double top, above peak for double bottom)
- **Trade-offs**: ~75-80% accurate; triple patterns more reliable than double
- **Crypto notes**: Most common pattern in crypto; frequently appears at macro S/R levels

### Pennant / Flag
- **When to use**: After a sharp vertical move (flagpole); consolidation phase
- **How**: Buy on continuation break in direction of initial move; stop-loss at consolidation low
- **Trade-offs**: High reliability for continuation; short duration (1-20 bars)
- **Crypto notes**: Excellent for capturing crypto momentum moves; tight stop-losses possible

### Wedge (Falling / Rising)
- **When to use**: Falling wedge = potential bullish breakout; rising wedge = potential bearish breakout
- **How**: Falling: buy on upward breakout above upper trendline. Rising: short on downward breakdown
- **Trade-offs**: Falling wedge more reliable than rising wedge; moderate accuracy
- **Crypto notes**: Falling wedge breakout is a reliable buy signal in crypto uptrends

## Candlestick Patterns

### Bullish Engulfing
- **When to use**: After a downtrend; red candle followed by larger green candle covering red body
- **How**: Enter on next candle if confirmed; stop-loss below engulfing candle low
- **Trade-offs**: Strong signal when at support; less reliable in sideways markets
- **Crypto notes**: Most reliable on 4H+ timeframes; combine with RSI divergence for best results

### Bearish Engulfing
- **When to use**: After an uptrend; green candle followed by larger red candle covering green body
- **How**: Short on confirmation; stop-loss above engulfing candle high
- **Trade-offs**: Uncommon but reliable; deeper red candle = stronger signal
- **Crypto notes**: Excellent for catching crypto pullbacks; false signals in strong uptrends

### Morning Star
- **When to use**: At bottom of downtrend; long red → short body → long green
- **How**: Enter when third candle closes above midpoint of first candle; stop-loss below star low
- **Trade-offs**: Very reliable three-candle reversal; the doji variant is even stronger
- **Crypto notes**: One of the most reliable reversal signals in crypto; works across all timeframes

### Evening Star
- **When to use**: At top of uptrend; long green → short body → long red
- **How**: Short when third candle closes below midpoint of first candle; stop-loss above star high
- **Trade-offs**: Reliable bearish reversal; gaps enhance signal strength
- **Crypto notes**: Rare but powerful in crypto; best on daily timeframe

### Hammer
- **When to use**: At bottom of downtrend; short body + long lower wick (2:1 wick-to-body minimum)
- **How**: Enter on next bullish candle; stop-loss below hammer low
- **Trade-offs**: Single-candle = less reliable; needs confirmation; inverse hammer is bullish variant
- **Crypto notes**: Very common in crypto; many false signals; always wait for next-candle confirmation

### Shooting Star
- **When to use**: At top of uptrend; small lower body + long upper wick (2:1 wick-to-body minimum)
- **How**: Short on next bearish candle; stop-loss above shooting star high
- **Trade-offs**: Bearish counterpart to hammer; reliable at resistance
- **Crypto notes**: Frequently marks local tops in crypto; combine with overbought RSI

### Doji (all types)
- **When to use**: At trend extremes; open and close nearly equal
- **How**: Wait for next candle direction for confirmation; do not trade dojis alone
- **Trade-offs**: Signal of indecision, not direction; dragonfly = bullish, gravestone = bearish
- **Crypto notes**: Extremely common in crypto consolidation; long-legged doji = major indecision

### Three White Soldiers
- **When to use**: After downtrend; three consecutive green candles with small wicks
- **How**: Enter on third candle close or next open; stop-loss below lowest soldier low
- **Trade-offs**: Very strong bullish reversal; more reliable than single-candle patterns
- **Crypto notes**: Reliable end-of-downtrend signal in crypto; can also occur as continuation

### Three Black Crows
- **When to use**: After uptrend; three consecutive red candles with small wicks
- **How**: Short on third candle close; stop-loss above highest crow high
- **Trade-offs**: Strong bearish reversal; indicates sustained selling pressure
- **Crypto notes**: Reliable top signal in crypto; often precedes significant corrections

### Abandoned Baby
- **When to use**: At trend extremes; candle → doji gap → opposite-direction candle
- **How**: Enter on third candle; stop-loss beyond the doji gap
- **Trade-offs**: Rare pattern but very reliable for short-term reversals
- **Crypto notes**: Rare in crypto but highly effective when it appears; best on daily timeframe
