**Authors**: Alan John, Jon Law | **Pages**: 525 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### The Key Three (Premises of TA)
1. **History Tends to Repeat Itself** — Investor psychology and self-predicting patterns drive recurring price behavior
2. **The Market Discounts Everything** — Prices reflect all available information (Efficient Market Hypothesis); crypto is less efficient, creating opportunity
3. **Prices Move in Trends** — Trends are more likely to continue than reverse

### Crypto-Specific Analysis Trinity
- **Technical Analysis** — Short-term entry/exit via patterns, indicators, oscillators
- **Fundamental Analysis** — Long-term value via on-chain metrics, project metrics, sentiment, utility, relative value
- **Hype Analysis** — Trend/social-momentum trading; riskiest form specific to crypto

### Indicator Function Framework (Alert → Predict → Confirm)
1. **Alert** — An indicator signals a potential setup
2. **Predict** — A second indicator forecasts direction
3. **Confirm** — A third indicator validates before entry

### Risk-Return Tradeoff in Crypto
Crypto sits at the highest end of the volatility spectrum. Higher reward potential carries proportionally higher risk. Use stop-losses, trailing stops, and position sizing rigidly.
