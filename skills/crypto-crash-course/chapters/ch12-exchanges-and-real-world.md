# Chapter 12: Exchanges & Real-World Use

## Core Idea
Exchanges are the primary gateway for buying, selling, and trading cryptocurrencies. Choosing the right exchange requires evaluating currency support, interface, fees, and trustworthiness. Cryptocurrencies are also increasingly accepted by online retailers and available via crypto debit cards for real-world spending.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Exchange Due Diligence Checklist**: Country currency support → Interface usability → Payment method compatibility → Reviews/trust → Fee structure → Security features
- **Security Triad for Exchange Accounts**: Strong password + 2FA + multiple exchange accounts

## Key Concepts
### Exchange Selection
- Find exchanges supporting your local currency and preferred payment method
- Read reviews for trustworthiness and hidden issues
- **Must-do**: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) — combines "what you know" (password) with "what you have" (phone/authenticator)
- **Must-do**: Use the strongest possible password (mixed case, numbers, unique)
- **Backup plan**: Sign up for at least two exchanges — if one fails, you're not locked out

### Selling Through Exchanges
- You sell TO the exchange, not to an individual buyer
- Compare offers across exchanges for the best sell price
- Exchanges make a profit on the spread — shop around

### Real-World Use
- Growing merchant adoption: Overstock.com (first major), Expedia, Newegg, Microsoft, Shopify, Dish
- Payment process: QR code scans → automatic price conversion → authorize on phone
- Crypto debit cards (e.g., CryptoPay): Spend crypto anywhere Visa is accepted; pulls from wallet directly

## Anti-patterns
- Using the same password across exchanges — a single breach compromises all accounts
- Skipping 2FA — the single most effective security measure
- Keeping all funds on exchange wallets — transfer to cold storage after every trade
- Ignoring fee differences — small percentage differences compound significantly

## Key Takeaways
- Choose exchanges carefully based on currency, fees, and security reputation
- Always enable 2FA and use unique strong passwords
- Maintain accounts on at least two exchanges for redundancy
- Transfer profits to cold storage immediately
- Crypto is increasingly spendable through merchants and debit cards
