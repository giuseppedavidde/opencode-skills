# Chapter 10: Altcoins With Potential & Picking Winners

## Core Idea
Beyond the major cryptocurrencies, dozens of altcoins target specific niches — from joke coins that gained real value (Dogecoin) to serious infrastructure projects (EOS, Cardano, NEO). Picking winners requires a systematic due diligence framework, not hype-chasing.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Winner Selection Framework**: Team → Purpose → Progress → Price
- **Niche Opportunity Matrix**: Each altcoin serves a specific sector — payments (Dogecoin), data (IOTA), lending (SALT), energy (Power Ledger), identity (Civic), entertainment (TRON)

## Key Concepts
- **Dogecoin**: Started as a joke (Shiba Inu meme); surprisingly persistent as a payment coin and popular entry point for beginners
- **IOTA**: Selling and providing data through a feeless network
- **SALT**: Blockchain lending platform — deposit crypto, receive fiat loans
- **EOS**: Ethereum competitor promising zero-fee transactions
- **Cardano**: First peer-reviewed blockchain; home for dapps and smart contracts
- **NEO**: Chinese-origin; potential to dominate if China restricts foreign coins
- **Power Ledger**: Decentralized renewable energy trading; sell excess solar to neighbors
- **TRON**: Entertainment distribution network; purchase content with TRONIX tokens

### How to Pick a Winner
1. **Team**: Who designed it? What have they built before? Who backs them?
2. **Purpose**: Does it solve a real problem, or is it a solution looking for one?
3. **Progress**: Is there a working beta/testnet? Planned features that attract users?
4. **Price**: Is the coin fairly valued relative to its stage? Avoid hyped-up valuations.

## Anti-patterns
- Investing in "joke coins" expecting them to all perform like Dogecoin
- Buying during ICO hype at inflated prices — wait for reasonable valuation
- Ignoring country-specific regulatory risk (especially Chinese coins)
- Assuming more features = better investment — simplicity often wins

## Key Takeaways
- Altcoins fill real niches: energy trading, identity, data storage, entertainment
- Always evaluate team, problem, progress, and price before investing
- The best time to invest is before a project gains mainstream attention, not after
