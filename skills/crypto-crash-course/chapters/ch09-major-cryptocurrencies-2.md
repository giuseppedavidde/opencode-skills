# Chapter 9: Major Cryptocurrencies (Part 2)

## Core Idea
A second wave of cryptocurrencies offers specialized features: Golem creates a decentralized supercomputer, Factom secures business records, Dash introduces masternodes for governance/features, OmiseGO scales blockchain via Plasma, Zcash uses zero-knowledge proofs for privacy, Bitcoin Cash increases block size for payments, and Steem rewards social media content.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Dual-Token Models**: Some networks (Factom, Steem) use separate tokens for access/utility vs. investment/staking
- **Governance Innovation**: Dash's masternode system creates a formal incentive structure for network maintenance

## Key Concepts
### Golem
- Decentralized supercomputer: rent out your computer's idle power or borrow others'
- Use cases: CGI rendering, ML/DNA analysis, AI, NLP
- Task templates + reputation system for both providers and requesters

### Factom
- Unalterable record-keeping for business, government, and legal data
- Two tokens: Factoids (traded, decentralize network) + Entry Credits (non-transferable, pay for data entries)
- Applications: medical records, driver's licenses, supply chain, legal data

### Dash (Digital Cash)
- Forked from Litecoin; introduced Masternodes (1000 Dash collateral)
- PrivateSend: coin mixing for untraceable transactions
- InstantSend: near-instant transaction confirmation
- Masternodes earn dividends for providing network services

### OmiseGO
- Built on Ethereum; aims to be the largest peer-to-peer exchange
- Plasma Protocol: blockchains on top of blockchains for unlimited scalability
- Goal: millions of transactions per second via child chains

### Zcash
- First cryptocurrency with zero-knowledge cryptography (zk-SNARKs)
- Shielded transactions conceal sender, recipient, and amount
- Selective disclosure feature for regulatory compliance and auditing

### Bitcoin Cash (BCash)
- Hard fork from Bitcoin in 2017 over block size dispute
- 8MB blocks (vs. Bitcoin's 1MB) for more transactions per block
- Designed for everyday electronic cash, not store of value

### Steem
- Blockchain-based social media platform rewarding content creation
- Three earning methods: posting, curating (upvoting), direct purchase
- Rewards split between Steem Power and Steem Dollars

## Anti-patterns
- Investing in a project without understanding its tokenomics (dual-token models)
- Ignoring governance structure — masternode requirements affect decentralization
- Confusing hard forks with the original chain

## Key Takeaways
- Specialized blockchains serve distinct niches: computation, records, governance, privacy, payments, content
- Dual-token and masternode models introduce new incentive structures
- Scalability solutions like Plasma address blockchain congestion
