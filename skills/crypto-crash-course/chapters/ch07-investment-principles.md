# Chapter 7: Investment Principles & Risk Management

## Core Idea
Cryptocurrency investing requires the same discipline as any other market — but amplified due to extreme volatility. The core principles are: resist emotional trading, think long-term, research relentlessly, protect your assets, and mind the fees.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Conservative Allocation Principle**: Only invest what you can afford to lose. Cryptocurrency is a high-risk, high-potential-return asset class.
- **Long-Term Horizon Strategy**: Short-term volatility is noise. The general multi-year trend of established cryptocurrencies is upward.
- **Research Pyramid**: Product → Team → Market Demand → Code Quality → Community → Competitive Landscape
- **Security First Protocol**: Hot wallet for active trading → immediately move profits to cold storage

## Key Concepts
- **Resist temptation**: Fear of missing out (FOMO) and panic selling are the biggest threats to new investors
- **Diversification**: Don't put everything in Bitcoin alone — spread across multiple promising cryptocurrencies
- **Deep research**: Evaluate the product's real-world use case, the development team's pedigree, and whether genuine demand exists
- **Asset protection**: No system is "unhackable." Use software wallets with strong keys; move coins offline to hardware wallets immediately
- **Fee awareness**: Even small exchange fees compound over time. Compare fee structures before committing to any platform
- **Regulatory monitoring**: Government decisions can devastate investments overnight. Stay informed on policy changes.

## Anti-patterns
- Investing based on hype or celebrity endorsement without personal research
- Keeping all coins on an exchange (not your keys, not your coins)
- Ignoring fees — they can erase profits in volatile markets
- Panic selling during dips — the opposite of the long-term strategy

## Key Takeaways
- Treat crypto as a long-term allocation, not a get-rich-quick scheme
- Research the project, not just the price
- Use cold storage for the bulk of holdings; only keep trading capital on exchanges
- Diversify across multiple currencies and never invest more than you can lose
