## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

1. **Digital Gold Framework** — Bitcoin is positioned as "digital gold": a fixed-supply (21M cap) store of value resistant to inflation and government manipulation. Other coins serve complementary roles (e.g., Litecoin as "silver" for daily transactions).

2. **Decentralization Trust Model** — Trust is shifted from centralized institutions (banks, governments) to mathematical consensus (proof-of-work, blockchain verification by distributed nodes). No single party can manipulate the system.

3. **Volatility-As-Opportunity Principle** — Extreme price swings are not bugs but features for informed investors. Long-term hodling smooths volatility; short-term trading exploits it for profit.

4. **Portfolio Diversification for Beginners** — Never invest more than you can afford to lose. Diversify across multiple cryptocurrencies (not just Bitcoin). Use cold storage for long-term holdings, hot wallets only for active trading.

5. **Research-First Investment Approach** — Before investing: evaluate the team's pedigree, assess genuine market demand, review the whitepaper, check code quality, look for community support and transparency. 96% of ICOs fail.

6. **Security Layering** — Use strong unique passwords + 2FA on exchanges. Move profits immediately to cold storage. Use multiple addresses for anonymity. Never share private keys.
