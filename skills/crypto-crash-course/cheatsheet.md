# Cheatsheet — Quick Reference for Beginners

## Security Checklist

- [ ] **Enable 2FA on every exchange account** — combines password + phone/authenticator
- [ ] **Use unique, strong passwords** — mixed case, numbers, special chars; never reuse
- [ ] **Transfer profits to cold storage immediately** — hot wallet = spending, cold = savings
- [ ] **Never share your private keys** — they are the keys to your money, period
- [ ] **Use a new address for each transaction** — preserves privacy and prevents transaction linking
- [ ] **Sign up for at least 2 exchanges** — redundancy if one goes down
- [ ] **Keep only trading capital on exchanges** — move the rest offline
- [ ] **Beware of "too good to be true" promises** — 99% are scams
- [ ] **Verify wallet reputation** before depositing funds — check history and reviews
- [ ] **Store paper wallets securely** — a single piece of paper is a single point of failure

## Exchange Comparison

| Criteria | What to Check |
|----------|--------------|
| Currency support | Does it support your country's fiat currency? |
| Payment methods | Credit/debit card, bank transfer, PayPal? |
| Interface | Intuitive for beginners? Mobile app available? |
| Fees | Trading fee, withdrawal fee, deposit fee — compare all |
| Security | 2FA required? Cold storage for user funds? History of hacks? |
| Liquidity | High volume = tighter spreads = better prices |
| Reviews | Check recent user experiences on forums |

## Buying Guide — Step by Step

1. **Choose an exchange** matching your currency, payment method, and security needs
2. **Create and verify your account** — submit ID for KYC compliance
3. **Enable 2FA** before depositing any funds
4. **Deposit fiat currency** via bank transfer, card, or other supported method
5. **Place your first buy order** — start with a small amount to learn the process
6. **Transfer coins to your hot wallet** — don't leave them on the exchange
7. **Move the majority to cold storage** — keep only trading funds hot
8. **Set up a second exchange** as backup before you need it

## Investment Quick Rules

- **Never invest more than you can afford to lose** — crypto is high-risk
- **Diversify** across at least 3-5 different cryptocurrencies
- **Think in years, not days** — short-term volatility is noise
- **Research before buying** — team, purpose, progress, price
- **Ignore FOMO** — there will always be another opportunity
- **Monitor regulatory news** — government decisions can move markets
- **Stay humble** — even experts can't predict crypto prices reliably

## Red Flags — When to Walk Away

- Promises of guaranteed returns or "double your money"
- Anonymous or unverifiable development team
- Mining described as the primary feature (not a feature, a mechanism)
- Developers avoid questions on forums (BitcoinTalk.org)
- No working product or beta — just a whitepaper
- Vague goals with no clear milestones or timeline
- Hard sell pressure — "buy now or miss out forever"
