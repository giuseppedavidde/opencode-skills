# Patterns & Techniques

## Cooking Method Selection
**When to use**: choosing how to cook each Smartfood to maximize nutrient retention.
**How**: 
1. Identify the dominant smart molecule in the ingredient
2. Select method per the Cooking Science Rules table (SKILL.md)
3. When in doubt: steam or brief stew with minimal water
4. Never fry antocianine-rich vegetables
5. Cook carotenoid-rich vegetables with EVOO

## Smartfood Meal Assembly
**When to use**: building a daily meal plan.
**How**:
1. Visual formula: ½ vegetable plate, ¼ whole grains, ¼ protein
2. Include ≥1 Longevity Smartfood per meal
3. Include ≥1 Protective Smartfood per meal
4. Breakfast is mandatory (include fruit, whole grains, nuts)
5. Last meal ≥3 hours before sleep

## Curcumina Activation
**When to use**: cooking with curcuma to maximize absorption.
**How**:
1. Always add freshly ground black pepper (piperina)
2. Dissolve in fat (EVOO) before adding to dish
3. Add to cooking water for grains/legumes, or sprinkle on fish/veg
4. Buy small quantities, store in cool dark place
5. Replace if bitter (old/heat-exposed curcuma)

## Non-Heme Iron Absorption
**When to use**: vegetable-based meals that need iron bioavailability.
**How**:
1. Add vitamin C source: lemon juice, fresh peperoncino, orange zest
2. Finish meal with vitamin-C-rich fruit (agrumi, fragole, kiwi)
3. Pair with lactic acid (yogurt + pistacchi or dark chocolate)
4. Cook legumes thoroughly (heat improves non-heme iron availability)

## Fasting-Mimicking Day Protocol
**When to use**: one-day fasting-mimicking for healthy adults (max 1x/week).
**How**:
1. Allowed: non-starchy vegetables (80g+ raw, 200g+ cooked), EVOO (1 tbsp/meal), seeds (1 tbsp/meal), herbs, spices, lemon
2. Excluded: any protein source (meat, fish, eggs, dairy, legumes, nuts), any carb source (grains, pasta, bread, fruit, sugar, potatoes)
3. One fasting-mimicking meal can replace a regular lunch or dinner
4. Never replicate within 7 days
5. Contraindicated for children, elderly, pregnant, ill, eating disorder history

## Fiber Achievement
**When to use**: reaching 25-30g daily fiber target.
**How**:
1. Vegetables and fresh fruit covering half of each meal
2. Whole grains at least once daily
3. One portion (30g) nuts daily
4. Legumes at least 3x/week
5. Prefer whole vegetables over juices/smoothies (fibers destroyed by blending)
