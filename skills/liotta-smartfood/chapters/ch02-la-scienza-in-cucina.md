# Chapter 2: La scienza in cucina

## Core Idea
The chemical fate of food molecules during cooking, preservation, and freezing — and how to maximize nutrient bioavailability through proper techniques and pairings.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Molecular Destinies Framework
Every cooking method alters nutrient structure differently. Key rules:
- **Antocianine**: steam > raw > brief stew > microwave > boil > fry. Keep peel on purple potatoes (−70% without peel)
- **Capsaicina**: heat-stable, can be added anytime; bread removes from mouth (not water)
- **Carotenoidi**: cooked > raw (bioavailability increases with heat + oil). Good in canned/frozen
- **Curcumina**: must pair with piperina (black pepper) + fat for absorption. Avoid old/heat-exposed spice
- **Ferro non-eme**: cooking + vitamin C (lemon/pepper) improves absorption
- **Fisetina**: concentrated in apple peel — always wash and eat with skin
- **Fibers**: 25-30g/day from veggies, whole grains, legumes, nuts. Destroyed by juicing/blending

### Cooking Method Recommendations
| Method | Best for | Avoid for |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Raw | Radicchio, peppers (vitamin C), berries | Carotenoid-rich veg |
| Steam | Most vegetables, preserves antocianine | — |
| Boil | Legumes (improves non-heme iron), carrots | Antocianine-rich veg (−55%) |
| Pan-sear/stew | Melanzane (increases availability) | — |
| Grill | — | Melanzane (−50% antocianine) |
| Fry | — | Most nutrients (−62% antocianine) |
| Microwave | Patate viola (8 min preserves antocianine) | — |

## Key Concepts
- **Biodisponibilità**: fraction of nutrients that pass from food into the body
- **Fibre idrosolubili**: form gel, slow sugar/fat digestion, feed microbiome
- **Fibre insolubili**: absorb water, increase stool mass, carry toxins
- **Vegetable besciamella**: replace butter+flour+milk with EVOO+whole flour+vegetable milk

## Key Takeaways
1. Steam or brief stew to preserve antocianine; never fry purple/red vegetables
2. Always cook carotenoid-rich veg with a drizzle of oil
3. Pair curcuma with black pepper; buy small quantities, store cool
4. Pair iron vegetable sources with lemon juice or fresh pepper
5. Eat apple peel (fisetina), wash thoroughly
6. Get 25-30g fiber daily; avoid replacing whole vegetables with juices
