# Chapter 6: I pasti e i piatti che mimano il digiuno

## Core Idea
Fasting-mimicking meals developed by IEO's SmartFood team — low-calorie vegetable dishes whose smart molecules may activate longevity genes (Sirt) the same way caloric restriction does, without actual deprivation.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Fasting-Mimicking Protocol (IEO)
- **Frequency**: max 1 day/week; not more than 1 day in a 7-day span
- **Target**: healthy adults only
- **Not for**: children, elderly, pregnant women, those with illness or eating disorder history

### Allowed Foods
- Non-starchy vegetables (80g+ raw leafy, 200g+ cooked): all greens, cruciferous, peppers, zucchini, etc.
- EVOO: 1 tbsp per meal
- Oil seeds: max 1 tbsp per meal (or ½ tbsp + ½ tbsp EVOO)
- Herbs and spices: unlimited (especially Longevity: curcuma, peperoncino)
- Lemon juice and zest

### Excluded Foods
- Animal proteins (meat, fish, eggs, dairy)
- Legumes, nuts
- Simple carbs (sugar, fruit, honey)
- Complex carbs (pasta, rice, bread, grains, potatoes)
- Any protein source that would trigger Tor

## Featured Fasting-Mimicking Recipes
- Crema di asparagi alla curcuma
- Insalatona con vinaigrette alla senape
- Grande grigliata vegetariana
- Minestrone invernale + minestrone tiepido d'estate
- Carpaccio di cavolfiore marinato al ginepro
- Bietole, capperi e olive taggiasche
- Ratatouille profumata di basilico
- Sapori d'autunno (carpaccio di funghi e semi)
- Listarelle di cavolo rosso con asparagi marinati
- Bastoncini di zucchine e melanzane alla menta
- Pinzimonio di verdure con salsa allo zenzero
- Passato di carciofi e porri

## Key Concepts
- **Sirt activation**: during fasting, Sirt genes orchestrate energy redistribution → repair over growth
- **Tor inhibition**: protein intake stimulates Tor (aging gene); fasting-mimicking meals are protein-free
- **Insulin pathway**: carbohydrate intake triggers insulin → aging genes; these meals are carb-free
- **Satiety from fat**: lipids signal fullness to the brain via gut-brain axis; EVOO + seeds provide this
- **Spice satiety**: spicy compounds (capsaicin) may further suppress appetite
- **Mood effects**: fasting periods often correlate with increased attention, calmness, euphoria (adaptation mechanism)

## Anti-patterns
- **Self-prescribed prolonged fasting**: dangerous without medical supervision
- **Repeating fasting days**: max 1/week as precaution
- **Applying protocol to vulnerable groups**: children, elderly, pregnant, ill, eating disorder history

## Key Takeaways
1. Fasting-mimicking = vegetables + EVOO/seeds + herbs/spices + lemon — nothing else
2. Max one day per week; never for vulnerable individuals
3. The protocol is experimental (based on limited human research) — an opportunity, not a prescription
4. These dishes also work as light, flavorful everyday side dishes
5. Longevity spices (curcuma, peperoncino) may amplify the genetic effects
