# Chapter 3: Ricette in famiglia

## Core Idea
Everyday recipes that combine 30 Smartfoods in Mediterranean tradition — accessible ingredients, practical techniques, no exotic requirements. All recipes IEO-certified.

## Recipe Categories
- **Insalate**: agrodolce with finocchi/cipolle/arance rosse, lattuga/radicchio/uva/pera/melagrana, panzanella integrale
- **Zuppe e minestre**: crema di zucca alla cannella, minestra legumi/orzo al curry, zuppa farro/fagioli di soia, zuppa lenticchie/spinaci alla curcuma, zuppa cicerchie/castagne, zuppa ceci/gamberetti
- **Primi**: risotto giallo agli asparagi, trofie pesto leggero, fusilli pesto radicchio/noci, spaghetti salsa di pomodoro, orecchiette broccoli, pennette carciofi/capperi, orzotto piccante, trenette pesto trapanese, linguine champignon/tofu
- **Piatti unici**: sformatini cous cous, pomodori ripieni di quinoa, pizza margherita, sandwich verdure/farinata, piadina vegetariana/noci
- **Secondi**: salmone al vapore/riso basmati, uova in camicia/asparagi/anacardi, dentice patate viola al cartoccio, hamburger borlotti/senape, polpettine sarde al sugo, straccetti pollo/carote, orata all'acqua pazza, arrosto tacchino/prugne, filetti branzino/nocciole, sgombri al vapore, filetti merluzzo
- **Salse**: paté di capperi, guacamole, tzatziki, hummus, paté di melanzane, crema fagioli cannellini, salsina vegetale piccante

## Key Concepts
- **Symbols**: each recipe tagged for vegetarian, vegan, and common intolerances (gluten-free, lactose-free)
- **Meal graph**: visual representation of Smartfood composition per dish
- **No red meat**: the diet discourages red meat; recipes focus on fish, white meat, eggs, legumes

## Key Takeaways
1. Use the 30 Smartfoods as your pantry staples — all available at supermarket
2. Adapt Mediterranean classics: swap refined flour for whole grain, butter for EVOO
3. Batch-cook legumes and whole grains for quick Smartfood meals
4. Every recipe is scalable; adjust portions using the visual formula
