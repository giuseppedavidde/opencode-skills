# Chapter 5: I dolci (meno dolci)

## Core Idea
Desserts made with Smartfood ingredients — lower in sugar, higher in nutritional value, without sacrificing pleasure. The philosophy: sweetness should come from fruit and smart ingredients, not refined sugar.

## Featured Recipes
- Fragole e ciliegie in un abbraccio di cioccolato e pistacchio
- Piccole gelatine di tè verde con fiori e frutta
- Focaccine di castagne con crema di cachi
- Biscotti alle mandorle
- Crostata alla marmellata di lamponi
- Torta di mele (with peel-on apples for fisetina)
- Brownie con cacao, nocciole e fagioli neri
- Crêpes con frutti di bosco e latte di soia
- Porridge con mele e uvetta

## Key Concepts
- **Sugar awareness**: know how many teaspoons of sugar per serving
- **Smart substitutions**: replace white flour with whole-grain, butter with EVOO, milk with vegetable alternatives
- **Legumes in desserts**: black beans in brownies add fiber and nutrients without altering taste
- **Fruit as sweetener**: rely on dried fruit, ripe bananas, dates to reduce added sugar

## Key Takeaways
1. Smartfood desserts use fruit and smart ingredients as primary sweeteners
2. Cocoa ≥70% is a Smartfood (quercetina) — use it in place of milk chocolate
3. Legumes (black beans, chickpea flour) can replace part of the flour in baked goods
4. Even Smartfood desserts are treats — portion control still applies
