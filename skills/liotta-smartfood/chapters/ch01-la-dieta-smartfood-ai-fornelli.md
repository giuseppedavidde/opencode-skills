# Chapter 1: La Dieta Smartfood ai fornelli

## Core Idea
Introduction to the Smartfood diet: the first diet in Europe with IEO (European Institute of Oncology) certification, based on nutrigenomics research. 30 scientifically-selected foods that modulate gene expression to combat aging, overweight, and chronic diseases.

## Frameworks Introduced

### The 30 Smartfood System
- **20 Longevity Smartfood**: foods with molecules that activate Sirt (longevity genes) and inhibit p66/Tor (aging genes)
- **10 Protective Smartfood**: macronutrient/mineral/vitamin sources that prevent chronic disease
- **7 smart molecules**: antocianine, capsaicina, curcumina, epigallocatechingallato (EGCG), fisetina, quercetina, resveratrolo

### Visual Meal Formula
- ½ vegetables, ¼ whole grains, ¼ protein source
- Breakfast is essential (Smartfood-compliant)
- Meal timing: no late-night eating

### Nutrigenomics Principle
- Abundant meals → gerontogenes active → aging
- Fasting → longevity genes active → repair
- Smartfood molecules mimic fasting without deprivation

## Key Concepts
- **p66**: aging gene sensitive to sugar intake
- **Tor**: aging gene sensitive to protein intake
- **Sirt (Sirt1-7)**: longevity genes that regulate metabolism and repair
- **Caloric restriction**: 30-40% reduction, proven to extend lifespan across species
- **Mimicry hypothesis**: eating Smartfoods produces fasting-like genetic effects

## Anti-patterns
- **Punishment diets**: deprivation-based regimes are unsustainable; Smartfood prioritizes pleasure + science
- **All-or-nothing perfection**: the diet recommends "in medio stat virtus" — balance, not extremism
- **Ignoring breakfast**: skipping meals disrupts metabolic gene regulation

## Key Takeaways
1. Build meals around the 30 Smartfoods, prioritizing Longevity varieties
2. Apply the visual formula: ½ veg, ¼ whole grains, ¼ protein
3. Favor cooking methods that preserve smart molecules (steam over boil/fry)
4. Pair curcumina with pepper (piperina) and fat for absorption
5. Pair iron-rich vegetables with vitamin C (lemon, pepper) for absorption
