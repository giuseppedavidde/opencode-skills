# Glossary

**Antocianine** — Polyphenolic pigments (red-to-blue) in 11 Longevity foods. Smart molecule that inhibits aging genes, slows carbohydrate absorption, protects cardiovascular system. Best preserved by steaming. (Ch 1, 2)

**Biodisponibilità** — Fraction of a nutrient that passes from food into the body's circulation. Increased by cooking for carotenoids, by vitamin C for non-heme iron. (Ch 2)

**Capsaicina** — Molecule in peperoncino/paprika that creates burning sensation. Heat-stable, inhibits aging genes. (Ch 1, 2)

**Carotenoidi** — 600+ organic compounds (betacarotene, licopene, luteina). Prevent arterial oxidation, reduce cancer risk. Bioavailability increases with cooking + oil. (Ch 2)

**Curcumina** — Smart molecule in curcuma. Anti-inflammatory, silences Tor gene. Needs piperina (black pepper) + fat for absorption. (Ch 1, 2)

**Epigallocatechingallato (EGCG)** — Smart molecule in tè verde/nero. Mimics fasting effects on longevity genes. Requires 5+ min infusion. (Ch 1)

**Fisetina** — Flavonoid in cachi, fragole, mele. Acts on Sirt gene. Concentrated in apple peel. (Ch 1)

**Gerontogeni** — Aging genes (p66, Tor) that activate after abundant meals, driving energy production, fat storage, free radical damage. (Ch 1)

**Longevity Smartfood** — 20 foods with molecules that activate Sirt and inhibit gerontogenes. (Ch 1)

**Mima-digiuno (fasting-mimicking)** — Meals using only vegetables, EVOO, seeds, herbs, spices — no protein or carbs — to trigger Sirt activation. (Ch 6)

**Nutrigenomica** — Discipline studying how nutrients interact with DNA to influence gene expression and health outcomes. (Ch 1)

**p66** — Gerontogene sensitive to sugar intake. Drives aging when activated by carbohydrate-rich meals. (Ch 1)

**Piperina** — Molecule in black pepper that dramatically increases curcumina absorption. (Ch 2)

**Protective Smartfood** — 10 foods/categories (garlic, whole grains, herbs, nuts, fruit, legumes, EVOO, seed oils, seeds, vegetables) that provide protective macro/micronutrients. (Ch 1)

**Quercetina** — Smart molecule in capperi, cipolle, cioccolato fondente, lattuga, asparagi. (Ch 1)

**Resveratrolo** — Smart molecule in uva (especially black). (Ch 1)

**Sirt (Sirt1-7)** — Longevity gene family activated by fasting/restriction. Drives cellular repair, anti-inflammation, lifespan extension. (Ch 1)

**Smartfood** — 30 scientifically-selected foods (20 Longevity + 10 Protective) certified by IEO for their health benefits. (Ch 1)

**Tor** — Gerontogene sensitive to protein intake. Drives aging when activated. (Ch 1)
