## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### The 30 Smartfood System
**20 Longevity Smartfood** — foods containing molecules that activate longevity genes (Sirt) and inhibit aging genes (p66, Tor). Organized by smart molecule:
- **Antocianine** (11 foods): arance rosse, cavoli rossi, ciliegie, fragole, frutti di bosco, melanzane, mele rosse, patate viola, prugne nere, radicchio rosso, uva nera
- **Quercetina** (6 foods): asparagi, capperi, cioccolato fondente ≥70%, cipolle, lattuga
- **Fisetina** (3 foods): cachi, fragole, mele
- **Individual molecules**: curcuma (curcumina), peperoncino/paprika (capsaicina), tè verde/nero (epigallocatechingallato), uva (resveratrolo)

**10 Protective Smartfood** — aglio, cereali integrali, erbe aromatiche, frutta a guscio, frutta fresca, legumi, olio EVO, oli di semi, semi oleosi, verdura

### Nutrigenomics Genetic Switch
- After abundant meals → gerontogenes (p66, Tor) activate → energy production, fat storage, free radicals, aging
- After fasting/restriction → longevity genes (Sirt) activate → repair, anti-inflammation, lifespan extension
- **Mimicry**: 7 smart molecules (antocianine, capsaicina, curcumina, EGCG, fisetina, quercetina, resveratrolo) bind to same pathways as fasting

### Cooking Science Rules
| Molecule | Best Method | Avoid | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|-------|
| Antocianine | Steam, brief stewing | Frying (−62%), boiling (−55%) | Keep peel on potatoes (−70% loss without) |
| Capsaicina | Any | — | Heat-stable; bread removes from mouth |
| Carotenoidi | Cooked + oil | — | Bioavailability increases with heat |
| Curcumina | With pepper + fat | Old/heat-exposed spice | Buy small quantities, store cool |
| Ferro non-eme | Cooked + vitamin C | Prolonged boiling | Lemon/pepper improves absorption |

### Fasting-Mimicking Protocol (IEO)
Max 1 day/week, healthy adults only:
- ✅ Non-starchy vegetables, EVOO (1 tbsp), oil seeds (1 tbsp), herbs, spices, lemon
- ❌ Animal proteins, legumes, nuts, grains, fruit, sugar
- ⚠️ Not for children, elderly, pregnant, ill, or history of eating disorders
