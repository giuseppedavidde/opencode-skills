# Cheatsheet

## Hypertensive disorders
| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| Chronic hypertension | ≥140/90 before 20 weeks, or pre-existing |
| Severe chronic hypertension | SBP ≥160 or DBP ≥110 mmHg |
| Gestational hypertension | ≥140/90 after 20 weeks, twice 6 h apart, no proteinuria or severe features |
| Pre-eclampsia | ≥140/90 after 20 weeks plus proteinuria ≥300 mg/24 h, ratio ≥0.3 or dipstick >1+ |
| Severe features | ≥160/110 twice >4 h; platelets <100,000/mm³; AST/ALT ≥2×; creatinine ≥1.1 mg/dL; pulmonary oedema; cerebral symptoms |
| Superimposed pre-eclampsia | New proteinuria, or sudden BP rise or drug escalation, in chronic hypertension |
| HELLP | LDH >600 IU/L or bilirubin ≥1.2 mg/dL; AST/ALT ≥2×; platelets <100,000/mm³ |
| Eclampsia | Grand mal seizures with pre-eclampsia or HELLP |

Acute severe BP: labetalol 20 mg IV then 40/80/80 mg every 10 min (max 220 mg); hydralazine 5–10 mg IV/IM every 20 min; nifedipine 10–20 mg orally.

## Glucose screening and diagnosis
| Test | Cutoffs (mg/dL) |
|---|---|
| 50 g GCT, 1 h | 130, 135 or 140; the book uses 135 |
| 100 g GTT — NDDG | 105 / 190 / 165 / 145 |
| 100 g GTT — Carpenter–Coustan | 95 / 180 / 155 / 140 |
| 75 g GTT — IADPSG | 92 / 180 / 153 |
| Diagnosis | Two abnormal values on the 100 g test; one on the 75 g test |
| GDM targets | Fasting <95; 2-h ≤120; 1-h ≤140 |
| Pregestational preconception | HbA1c <6% |

## Thyroid ranges in pregnancy
| Period | TSH upper reference (97.5th centile) | Treatment target |

| 6–13 weeks | 3.1–5.1 mU/L | 0.5–2.5 mU/L |
| 14–27 weeks | 2.7–4.1 mU/L | 0.5–2.5 mU/L |

## Anticoagulant dosing by indication
| Indication | Antepartum | Postpartum |
|---|---|---|
| APS with fetal loss, no thrombosis | Aspirin plus prophylactic LMWH or UFH | LMWH plus aspirin |
| APS with prior VTE | Therapeutic LMWH | Warfarin, lifelong |
| Prior VTE, low-risk thrombophilia | Prophylactic LMWH | Prophylactic or intermediate |
| High-risk thrombophilia with prior VTE | Therapeutic LMWH | Therapeutic or prophylactic |
| Acute VTE | Therapeutic LMWH, or UFH ≥5 days then LMWH | ≥6 weeks; total ≥6 months |

Enoxaparin: prophylactic 40 mg SC daily (APS chapter 30–40 mg every 12 h), intermediate 40 mg every 12 h, therapeutic 1 mg/kg every 12 h. Anti-Xa: 0.2–0.4 U/mL prophylactic, 0.5–1.2 U/mL therapeutic.

## Infection screening schedule
| Time | Tests |
|---|---|
| First prenatal visit | HIV (opt-out), HBsAg, syphilis, rubella IgG, varicella IgG, urine culture |
| First visit, if risk | Chlamydia and gonorrhoea if age <25 or risk factors; HCV antibody |
| 24–28 weeks | GDM screen; repeat HIV, HBsAg and syphilis if high risk |
| 28 weeks | Anti-D 300 µg if Rh(D)-negative and unsensitised |
| 36 0/7–37 6/7 weeks | GBS anovaginal culture |
| Postpartum | Rubella and varicella vaccine if non-immune; HBV vaccine if non-immune |

## Antepartum testing intervals
| Condition | Start | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Chronic or gestational hypertension | 32 weeks | Weekly NST |
| Pre-eclampsia | At diagnosis | Twice-weekly NST |
| Pregestational diabetes | 32 weeks, twice weekly at 36 | NST |
| Fetal growth restriction | At diagnosis | Twice-weekly NST/MVP |
| Oligohydramnios, MVP <2 cm | 32 weeks | Weekly NST/MVP |
| Polyhydramnios, MVP >8 cm | 28 weeks | Weekly NST/MVP |
| Prior unexplained fetal death | 1 week before the prior loss | Weekly NST |
| Postdates | 40 weeks weekly, 41 weeks twice weekly | NST |

Read the table first, then confirm the detail in the chapter. Aspirin for pre-eclampsia prevention is 81 mg daily, from 12–28 weeks and optimally before 16 weeks. Magnesium sulfate is the drug of choice for eclampsia. When a value falls between categories, use the higher category.
