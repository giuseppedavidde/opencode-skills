# Contrarian Trading Patterns & Setups

## Pattern 1: Extreme Bearish Put/Cell Squeeze (Squeeze Play I)
**When to use**: CBOE equity-only put/call ratio EMA5-21 oscillator crosses from above zero to below zero AND price closes above previous day's high.
**How**: Enter long on next open. Exit when EMA21-50 crosses from above to below zero.
**Trade-offs**: Best for equity indices (S&P 500, DJIA, NASDAQ 100). Excellent win rate (68%+). Average win 2.3x average loss. Short side weaker — consider long-only variant. Works on bond futures with dollar-weighted normalized put volume.

## Pattern 2: Individual Stock Sentiment Squeeze (Squeeze Play II)
**When to use**: Individual stock equity put/call ratio EMA50-100 oscillator's 10-day high exceeds +5% AND close > 3-day high.
**How**: Enter long on next open. Use 10% max stop + 50% trailing stop with 9-day delay. Or use LEAPS with time stops (T+30/60/90).
**Trade-offs**: Requires liquid options (large caps only). Non-optimized results beat buy/hold inconsistently. Best in bull markets for long trades. Short side degrades past T+30. Merck-style outlier stocks can produce 800%+ returns.

## Pattern 3: Tsunami Sentiment Wave (LEAPS Required)
**When to use**: EMA21-50 oscillator exceeds +10% (extreme bearishness) with no existing short position.
**How**: Buy ITM LEAPS call on next open. No price trigger, no stop-loss. Exit at T+30, T+60, or T+90.
**Trade-offs**: Long-only is strongest. LEAPS structurally required due to open trade volatility. T+60 is the best time frame (72.9% annualized). Fewer trades but larger wins.

## Pattern 4: VIX/VXO Implied Volatility Reversal
**When to use**: VIX implied volatility EMA21-50 oscillator 10-day high > +5% (fear extreme) AND close > 5-day high.
**How**: Enter long S&P 500 futures/ETF. Exit at T+30 using time stop.
**Trade-offs**: More fear = stronger signal. Works on equity indices but weaker than put/call ratio Squeeze Play. VIX > VXO. EMA1-10 5% threshold is a viable fast variant.

## Pattern 5: Public Short Sales Capitulation
**When to use**: Normalized Public Short Sales Ratio (NPSR) EMA4-8 oscillator at extreme high (excessive public shorting).
**How**: Enter long on the equity index (DJIA, S&P 500, NASDAQ 100, Russell 2000). Proprietary entry rules deliver 3-4.75 win/loss ratios.
**Trade-offs**: 2-week data delay does not negate signal. Largest absolute profits of any indicator in the book. Multi-market diversification smooths drawdowns.

## Pattern 6: Advisory Opinion Exhaustion
**When to use**: Investors Intelligence % bears EMA1-4W weekly oscillator > +5% AND weekly close > previous week high.
**How**: Enter long S&P 500. Exit when oscillator crosses zero and close < previous week low.
**Trade-offs**: Weakest of the sentiment indicators (opinion vs action). Market Vane Bullish Consensus (reversed) is stronger. Best for medium-term moves.

## Pattern 7: Bear News Flow Extremes
**When to use**: BNI4-8EMA oscillator > +5% (fear extreme in news) AND price close > previous week high.
**How**: Enter long S&P 500. Exit at week 2-4 time stops. Fear-based news has stronger predictive power than greed-based news.
**Trade-offs**: Week 1 exits are premature. Week 4 combined long/short produced 943 points, one of the book's best results. The quantified news flow approach is novel but requires proprietary data.

## Pattern 8: OEX Smart Money Confirmation
**When to use**: Use alongside any of the above patterns. When OEX put/call ratio EMA10-250 oscillator moves in the same direction as equity-only (confirmation), the signal is stronger.
**How**: Do NOT trade against OEX extremes. If OEX is bullish at a market top, the top is less certain. If OEX is bearish at a market bottom, the bottom may not hold.
**Trade-offs**: The OEX/equity-only divergence is itself information. Use OEX as a filter, not a primary signal.

## Pattern 9: LEAPS Long-Only Composite
**When to use**: Combine Squeeze Play II and TSW signals, taking only long trades across multiple stocks with diversified time frames.
**How**: Allocate equal capital ($10K per stock) across T+30, T+60, T+90 subsystems. Use ITM LEAPS exclusively.
**Trade-offs**: Eliminates short-side weakness. 40-51% annualized returns. Requires sufficient capital for multi-stock, multi-time-frame deployment.
