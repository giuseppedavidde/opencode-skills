# Chapter 10: Squeeze Play II and LEAPS Surrogates

## Core Idea
Replace stock positions with in-the-money LEAPS (Long-term Equity AnticiPation Securities) to eliminate stop-loss whipsaw and capture large sentiment swings. LEAPS provide defined risk with unlimited upside and 90% leverage.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Time Stops Instead of Price Stops**: Exit at T+30, T+60, T+90 days from entry. No maximum loss stop, no trailing stop. Defined risk = LEAPS premium paid.
- **Triple-Exit Subsystem**: Three time frames create three separate trading systems, each with $10,000 capital per stock.
- **LEAPS as Surrogates**: ITM LEAPS have minimal time premium, act as stock proxies with 10-15% capital requirement.

## Key Concepts
- **Non-LEAPS Underperforms**: $135,033 total profit vs $290,377 buy/hold — stops exit too early.
- **LEAPS Transforms Results**: $560,754 total profit vs $290,377 buy/hold — 51% annualized vs 27%.
- **Long-Only LEAPS Best**: Eliminate short trades, take only long T+30/60/90: $437,384 profit (40% annualized).
- **No Stop-Loss Advantages**: Traditional stops exit positions before the sentiment wave completes; LEAPS ride through volatility.

## Anti-patterns
- Using stop-losses with LEAPS — the entire point is defined risk without price-based exits.
- Trading OTM or ATM LEAPS — ITM LEAPS minimize time decay and delta uncertainty.
- Taking short trades with LEAPS — put LEAPS underperform; short-side time frames degrade past T+30.

## Key Takeaways
- LEAPS solve the stop-loss problem for sentiment-based systems.
- Long-only LEAPS Squeeze Play II is a strong standalone approach.
- Time stops (T+30/60/90) outperform price stops for capturing sentiment reversals.
