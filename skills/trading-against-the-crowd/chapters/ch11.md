# Chapter 11: The Tsunami Sentiment Wave Trading System

## Core Idea
The Tsunami Sentiment Wave (TSW) system targets only the most extreme sentiment readings (EMA21-50 > +10% or < -10%), capturing the biggest waves for long rides. Fewer trades, larger moves, no triggers or stops.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **TSW Setup**: EMA21-50 oscillator (medium speed). Entry only at +10% threshold (bottoms) or -10% (tops). No price trigger, no stop-loss. Pure sentiment extreme approach.
- **LEAPS Required**: The wide threshold + no stops means large adverse moves are possible; LEAPS limit risk to premium paid.
- **No Signal Override**: If in a long position, ignore all short signals until time stop exits. Prevents whipsaw from counter-trend signals.

- **LEAPS Performance**: $494,664 total vs $298,597 buy/hold (50% annualized).

- **Merck Star Performance**: T+60 LEAPS on MRK: $157,634 profit, 15-1 win/loss ratio, zero closed drawdown.
- **Comparison to Squeeze Play II**: TSW is slightly weaker overall but excellent in T+30 and T+60 frames.

## Anti-patterns
- Using TSW without LEAPS — the open trade drawdowns are too large for direct stock trading.
- Taking every signal — the 10% threshold should be strict; relaxing it degrades performance.
- Combining long and short signals simultaneously — the no-override rule prevents competing positions.

## Key Takeaways
- TSW targets only the most extreme sentiment conditions, producing fewer but larger winning trades.
- LEAPS are structurally required for this approach due to wide thresholds and no stops.
- Long trades significantly outperform short trades — a long-only TSW with LEAPS is a strong strategy.
