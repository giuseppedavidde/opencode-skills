# Chapter 19: The Fourth Estate Crowd

## Core Idea
News coverage amplifies crowd psychology through "attention cascades." By quantifying bearish and bullish news flow intensity, Summa creates a proprietary sentiment indicator that successfully predicts short-term market reversals.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Bear News Intensity Index (BNI4-8EMA)**: Normalized ratio of bearish news stories to total Wall Street news flow. Processed through 4/8-week EMA oscillator.
- **Squeeze Play II for News**: EMA4-8 oscillator with +5%/-5% thresholds. Time stops at weeks 1-4. Price trigger: close > prev week high / < prev week low.
- **Positive Feedback Loop Theory**: More bullish news → more buyers → higher prices → more bullish news. Symmetrical for bearish news, amplified by fear.

## Key Concepts
- **Week 2 Exit Best**: 586 points longs-only (vs 314 BH). 2.45 win/loss ratio. Reward/risk = 100.
- **Weeks 3-4 Also Strong**: 641 and 659 points respectively with excellent profiles.
- **Combined Long/Short Weeks 4**: 943 points vs 314 BH — one of the best systems in the book. 80.68% annualized average across all time frames.
- **Fear > Greed**: Bear news flow (fear) has stronger predictive power than bull news flow (greed). Short-side tests outperformed long-side.
- **Base Terms**: Key words scanned: optimism/pessimism, hopes/worries, soar/plunge, upbeat/panic, bullish/bearish.

## Anti-patterns
- Exiting too quickly (week 1) — longs-only week 1 produced only 82 points. Need at least 2 weeks for news sentiment to play out.
- Assuming bullish news flow has equal predictive power — bearish extremes are more reliable.
- News trading requires quantitative discipline — subjective reading of headlines is not systematic.

## Key Takeaways
- Quantitative news flow analysis is a novel, effective sentiment indicator.
- Bear news intensity (fear) outperforms bull news intensity (greed).
- The combined long/short week-4 exit system is one of the best performers in the book.
