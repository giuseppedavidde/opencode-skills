# Chapter 7: From Statistical Tests to Sentiment Trading System

## Core Idea
Statistical testing confirms equity-only put/call ratios predict reversals at extremes. This chapter converts those findings into Squeeze Play I — a mechanical system using custom sentiment oscillators without price triggers.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Squeeze Play I (Sentiment-Only)**: Entry when EMA5-21 crosses zero (from above = long, from below = short). Exit when EMA21-50 crosses zero.
- **Three Oscillator Speeds**: EMA5-21 (fast), EMA21-50 (medium), EMA50-250 (slow) tested for comparative performance.
- **System Robustness via Optimization**: Run optimization across parameters to check if profits are concentrated or broad.

## Key Concepts
- **Sentiment-Only Is Premature**: Squeeze Play I without triggers generates only 312.7 S&P points vs 406.8 buy/hold — not viable.
- **Faster Oscillators Outperform**: EMA5-21 > EMA21-50 > EMA50-250 in net profit and drawdown.
- **Sentiment Waves Leading, Not Coincident**: The oscillator crosses zero after sentiment has peaked/valleyed — this lag is why price triggers are needed.

## Anti-patterns
- Expecting sentiment-only systems to time entries precisely — they catch the reversal zone but enter too early.
- Using slow oscillators for swing trading — EMA50-250 had -127.5 point loss with -669 open drawdown.

## Key Takeaways
- Sentiment oscillators alone are insufficient for a trading system.
- EMA5-21 is the best entry speed; EMA21-50 for exits.
- The exit strategy is the Achilles heel — Squeeze Play I's simple zero-cross exit is a starting point, not a final solution.
