# Volume Profile — Cheatsheet

## Profile Shapes — Quick Identification
| Shape | Market State | Buyer/Seller Balance | Best Trade |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|------------|
| D-Profile | Balanced (range) | Buyers = Sellers | Short at top HVN, Long at bottom HVN |
| P-Profile | Uptrend | Buyers > Sellers | Long on pullback to POC |
| b-Profile | Downtrend | Sellers > Buyers | Short on pullback to POC |
| Thin Profile | Strong trend | One side dominates | Long/Short at volume clusters in trend |

## Level Significance Hierarchy
1. VPOC (highest volume single price) — strongest single level
2. Value Area High/Low (VAH, VAL) — edges of fair value
3. Volume Clusters (HVN) — wide accumulation zones
4. Volume Clusters within thin trends — continuation magnets
5. Low Volume Nodes (LVN) — price will gap through, weakest level

## Entry/Exit Rules
| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Price touches heaviest volume level | Enter immediately (limit order, no confirmation) |
| Level gets tested 0-3 pips away with 8+ pip reaction | Discard level (already tested) |
| Price ignores level, hits SL | Take reversal trade from same level |
| Price near weak high/low | Skip entry — price will shoot through |
| Failed auction near entry | Skip entry — magnet pulls price toward it |
| Price reaches 70-80% of PT | Move SL to reaction point (Neutral approach) |
| Price rotates at entry without rejection | Exit at BE |

## Fixed SL/PT Sizing (Intraday)
1. Load 500 daily candles with ATR(200)
2. Read ATR value on daily chart
3. Multiply by 10,000 → daily volatility in pips
4. Intraday SL/PT = 10-20% of daily volatility

## Stop-Loss Management Approaches
| Approach | Rule | Best For |
|----------|------|----------|
| Aggressive | Never move SL | Asian session (asleep) |
| Neutral | Move SL to reaction at 70-80% PT | EU/US sessions (preferred) |
| Conservative | Move SL to BE at 70-80% PT | Counter-trend trades |

## Macro News — Red News Categories
| Type | Examples | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| Weak Red | Oil Inventories, Unemployment Claims, Consumer Confidence | Can hold or re-enter 1-5 min after |
| Standard Red | CPI, GDP, NFP, Unemployment Rate | Never hold through. Re-enter after volatility calms |
| Monster Red | Rate Decisions, FOMC, Monetary Policy | Avoid entirely. Skip 1-2 sessions |
