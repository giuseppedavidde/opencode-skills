# Volume Profile — Trading Patterns & Setups

## Setup #1: Volume Accumulation
**When to use**: After price breaks out of a sideways rotation area. The breakout direction reveals whether buyers or sellers accumulated.
**How**: Apply Flexible VP to the rotation area → mark the heaviest volume level (wide bar) → place limit order at this level on pullback → enter in breakout direction.
**Trade-offs**: Highly reliable due to institutional defense. Requires patience — price may take days to return. Rotation must be clearly identifiable.
**Long Entry**: Rotation → strong uptrend → enter long at heaviest volume level.
**Short Entry**: Rotation → strong sell-off → enter short at heaviest volume level.

## Setup #2: Trend Setup
**When to use**: Strong trend with visible volume clusters where price paused within the trend.
**How**: Identify the strongest volume cluster within the trend → mark the heaviest volume bar → when price retraces, enter in trend direction at that level.
**Trade-offs**: Works well in trending markets. Counter-trend entries from this setup are risky. Confluence with Support-becoming-Resistance produces the strongest S/R zones.
**Long Entry**: Uptrend with volume cluster → long at cluster's heaviest volume.
**Short Entry**: Downtrend with volume cluster → short at cluster's heaviest volume.

## Setup #3: Rejection Setup
**When to use**: A clear aggressive rejection of higher or lower prices.
**How**: Identify strong rejection area → apply Flexible VP to the rejection → mark the heaviest volume within the rejection → enter on return.
**Trade-offs**: Most difficult setup to master. Hard to gauge "strong enough" rejection. Volume distribution within rejection can be ambiguous. Requires practice.
**Long Entry**: Rejection of lower prices (selling → buying reversal) → long at heaviest volume.
**Short Entry**: Rejection of higher prices (buying → selling reversal) → short at heaviest volume.

## Reversal Trades
**When to use**: After a volume-based level is completely ignored and SL is hit. Price shows zero reaction at the level.
**How**: Immediately change bias. Enter the opposite direction from the exact same level. Use smaller positions initially until comfortable.
**Trade-offs**: Psychological difficulty — switching bias is hard. Must be decisive. Price must NOT have reacted at all (even 1-2 pip reaction invalidates the reversal).

## Support Becoming Resistance (PA Confirmation)
**When to use**: To confirm any volume-based level. Look for a breached strong support or resistance.
**How**: Identify strong S/R → wait for breach + price action above/below → price returns to breached level → enter.
**Trade-offs**: Works standalone or as confluence. Works on all timeframes. Multiple reactions = stronger level.

## Open-Drive
**When to use**: One-sided move from a tight channel or session start.
**How**: Mark the open of the first aggressive candle → wait for pullback → enter from that level.
**Trade-offs**: The open-drive candle open is strong S/R. Works after sideways action or at session opens (Asian, EU, US).
