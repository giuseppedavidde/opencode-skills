# VOLUME PROFILE: The insiders guide to trading
**Author**: Trader Dale | **Chapters**: 6 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### VPOC (Volume Point of Control)
The single price level with the highest traded volume in a given period. Acts as the strongest reference point — the price institutions value most. Price tends to return to VPOC (magnet effect). Use VPOC as: primary entry zone, profit target, or invalidation level.

### Value Area (VA)
The price range where ~70% of total volume occurred. Defined by VAH (Value Area High) and VAL (Value Area Low). Price within VA = fair price zone. Price outside VA = potential mean reversion opportunity.

### HVN (High Volume Node)
Price levels with heavy volume (wide profile). Represents institutional accumulation/distribution zones. Acts as strong support/resistance. Price stops, rotates, consolidates at HVNs.

### LVN (Low Volume Node)
Price levels with minimal volume (thin profile). Represents gaps/inefficiency. Price moves through LVNs rapidly. Acts as weak support/resistance — expects breakage.

### TPO (Time Price Opportunity)
Each letter/time-slot represents price acceptance at a level. TPO count = time spent at price. Wide TPO clusters = acceptance. Narrow TPO = rejection/fast move.

### D-Profile (Balanced)
Bell-shaped distribution. Buyers and sellers in temporary equilibrium. Institutional accumulation happening. Trade: short at high-volume near top, long at high-volume near bottom. PT at POC.

### P-Profile (Bullish)
Aggressive buyers, weak sellers. Uptrend context. Key areas: POC (support on pullback), volume cluster in thin area (aggressive buyers' defense zone).

### b-Profile (Bearish)
Aggressive sellers, weak buyers. Downtrend context. Key areas: POC (resistance on pullback), volume cluster in thin area (aggressive sellers' defense zone).

### Thin Profile (Trending)
Strong one-sided movement. Few volume clusters. Key: volume clusters in uptrend = support, volume clusters in downtrend = resistance.

### Failed Auction
Market imperfection where no single "winner" emerged at a swing point. Acts as a magnet — price will test and pass through it. Use to extend PT or avoid entries.
