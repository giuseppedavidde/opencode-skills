# Chapter 4: Trading Style, Instruments & Macro News

## Core Idea
The same VP setups work on ALL timeframes. Choose a style that fits your lifestyle, trade instruments with low correlation and low costs, and never trade through significant macro news.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Intraday vs Swing vs Long-term**: Same logic, different SL/PT sizing, different timeframe for analysis
- **Instrument Selection**: Liquidity, spread costs, correlation, volatility, behavior with VP levels
- **Macro News Handling**: 10 rules — avoid prediction, track releases, quit before, re-enter after
- **News Classification**: Weak red (hold possible), Standard red (never hold), Monster red (avoid entirely)

## Key Concepts
- **Intraday**: 30-min charts, 10-20% of daily ATR for SL/PT (e.g., 10-12 pips for EUR/USD)
- **Swing**: 4H/Daily charts, 50-400% of daily ATR for SL/PT. Counter-trend trades acceptable
- **Long-term**: Weekly/Monthly charts. Watch swap rates (negative swap hurts over months)
- **Best intraday pairs**: EUR/USD (#1), AUD/USD, USD/CAD, USD/JPY
- **Avoid GBP pairs**: Too volatile, spike past S/R levels
- **Risk**: Trade max 4 pairs. Correlation = hidden risk. Use rules like "max 2 USD positions"
- **News Strategy**: Step 1: check calendar. Step 2: quit 2-5 min before. Step 3: wait for volatility to calm. Step 4: re-enter or take reversal
- **FX Squawk**: Fastest real-time news notification service
- **Economic News Impact Tool**: Check historical volatility of any macro event
- **Indirect effects**: Oil → CAD, China → AUD, World danger → JPY, Dairy → NZD

### Preferred Instruments by Style
- **Intraday**: EUR/USD (#1 for VP reactions), AUD/USD (slow, precise), USD/CAD (oil-linked), USD/JPY (precise but spikey)
- **EUR/USD characteristics**: Highest liquidity, cheapest, average volatility, "tells" you when something significant is happening
- **AUD/USD**: Slow, less volatile, commodity-linked (China), good for patient traders
- **USD/JPY**: Volatile, precise VP reactions, safe haven, capable of big moves
- **USD/CAD**: Oil-linked, sometimes imprecise reactions (shoots past then turns)
- **GBP/USD**: Avoid — too volatile, unpredictable VP reactions, spike moves
- **Swing/Long-term**: Any pair OK. Keep 0.5-4× daily ATR for SL/PT. Negative swap fees hurt over months.
- **Crypto**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple only — best liquidity, lowest slippage.

### Detailed News Protocol
1. Check Forex Factory every morning → mark all red news times
2. For Monster red news: stop trading 1-2 hours before → resume 1-2 sessions later
3. For Standard red news: quit trades 2-5 min before → wait for volatility to calm → re-enter if level intact
4. For Weak red news: can hold through, or re-enter 1-5 min after release
5. Use FX Squawk for real-time unexpected news alerts
6. During news, reduce to minimal position size (or use limit orders at minimal size)
7. After strong post-news trend: take Reversal trades in the new trend direction

### Analysis Routine (Intraday)
1. **Macro Calendar Check** (1 min): Open Forex Factory, mark all red news times.
2. **Big Picture** (2 min): Check 240-min or Daily chart. Identify overall trend. Strong uptrend = long only. Strong downtrend = short only. Rotation = both directions.
3. **Price Action Scan** (5 min): On 30-min chart, mark all: sideways price action areas, aggressive initiations, strong rejections, strong/weak highs/lows, failed auctions.
4. **Volume Profile Analysis** (5-10 min): Apply Flexible VP to all significant PA areas. Identify which of the 3 setups applies. Mark heaviest volume levels.
5. **Confirmation Check** (2 min): Do any PA strategies confirm the volume levels (support/resistance swap, open-drive, AB=CD, session open)?
6. **Level Finalization** (2 min): Check for weak highs/lows or failed auctions near levels. Discard any level compromised by these.
7. **Execution**: Set limit orders at levels. Monitor. Execute all triggered trades.

### Analysis Routine (Swing/Long-term)
- Same as intraday but: skip macro calendar step, skip overall trend step (swing levels can overcome trends).
- Use 240-min or Daily charts for swing, Weekly/Monthly for long-term.
- Confirmation strategies: support/resistance swap, open-drive, AB=CD (skip session open and daily/weekly high-low).

## Key Takeaways
- Choose 1-2 instruments initially, master them, then expand
- Never trade Monster red news (Rate Decisions, FOMC, NFP)
- After significant news, take reversal trades in the new trend direction
- Use ATR to size SL/PT for your chosen timeframe and instrument
- Beware indirect news effects: Oil→CAD, China→AUD, World danger→JPY, Dairy→NZD
- Systematic daily routine = consistent results. Never skip steps.
