# Volume Profile — Glossary of Terms

**AB = CD** — Harmonic pattern where leg AB equals leg CD in pip distance. Entry at D, measured with Fibonacci (0% at A, 100% at B → place 0% at C, 100% at D).

**Aggressive Initiation** — Strong one-sided price movement after sideways accumulation. Shows institutional direction bias.

**Aggressive Market Participants** — Institutions using market orders to enter/exit large positions, driving price.

**Alternative SL** — Exit only on candle close past SL level. Prevents SL hunts. Use with Catastrophic SL at 150% of normal SL.

**ATR (Average True Range)** — Measures average daily volatility. Set to 200 period. Used to determine fixed SL/PT values.

**b-Profile** — Bearish profile shape: aggressive sellers, weak buyers. Seen in downtrends. POC = resistance on pullback.

**Break-even (BE)** — Entry price level. Moving SL to BE when price reaches 70-80% of PT (Conservative approach).

**Catastrophic SL** — 150% of normal SL. Hard exit point for Alternative SL method.

**Correlation** — Degree two instruments move together. High correlation = hidden risk amplification.

**Cycle of Doom and Despair** — Constant strategy switching without mastering any. Primary reason retail traders fail.

**D-Profile** — Balanced bell-shaped profile. Accumulation zone. Top/bottom edges = S/R, POC near middle = PT target.

**Daily Open** — Where the new trading day opens at NY close (5:00 PM ET). Acts as S/R.

**Failed Auction** — Market imperfection where no single winner emerged at swing extreme. Acts as a magnet — price will test through it.

**Fixed PT/SL** — Same pip value for all trades, determined by ATR (e.g., 10 pip PT, 12 pip SL).

**Flexible Volume Profile** — Custom indicator allowing volume analysis on any selected chart area.

**Good Losing Trade** — Analysis was correct, execution flawless, but trade lost. Statistically normal. Stick to strategy.

**Good Winning Trade** — Proper analysis + flawless execution = profit. The ideal.

**HVN (High Volume Node)** — Wide profile area with heavy volume. Strong S/R. Institutional accumulation.

**LVN (Low Volume Node)** — Thin profile area with minimal volume. Weak S/R, price moves through quickly.

**Market Order** — Order executed immediately at best available price. Used by aggressive participants.

**Neutral SL Management** — Move SL to reaction point when price reaches 70-80% of PT.

**Open-Drive** — One-sided aggressive move from sideways channel or session open. Open candle becomes S/R.

**P-Profile** — Bullish profile shape: aggressive buyers, weak sellers. Seen in uptrends. POC = support on pullback.

**POC (Point of Control)** — Price level with highest traded volume. Strongest reference point in any profile.

**Reversal Trade** — After SL is hit with no reaction, enter opposite direction from same level.

**RRR (Risk Reward Ratio)** — Potential gain relative to risk. Dale prefers ~1:1.

**Session Open** — Asian, European, US session starts. Opening price becomes S/R.

**Sideways Price Action** — Rotation/consolidation area. Institutions accumulate positions here.

**Strong High/Low** — Swing point with visible aggressive rejection (long tail, fast reversal). Strong S/R.

**Strong Rejection** — Sudden aggressive reversal. One side clashes with stronger counter-party. Level becomes defended S/R.

**Support Becoming Resistance** — When a support level is breached, it becomes resistance (and vice versa).

**Thin Profile** — Trend profile. Fast one-sided movement with minimal volume accumulation.

**TPO (Time Price Opportunity)** — Each letter represents price acceptance at a level over a time period.

**Value Area (VA)** — Price range containing ~70% of total volume. VAH = top, VAL = bottom.

**Volume Accumulation Setup** — Setup #1. Trade from heaviest volume level within sideways area after a breakout.

**Volume Cluster** — Localized high-volume area within a trend profile. Entry/continuation zone.

**Volume Profile** — Histogram showing volume at each price level (vs. volume over time).

**Weak High/Low** — Swing point with slow, reluctant turn, no aggression. Price will shoot through it.
