# Wyckoff 2.0 Trading Patterns

## Accumulation Patterns

### Spring (Shakeout at Range Lows)
- **When to use**: Price is near the lower boundary of a potential accumulation range after Phase A/B development.
- **How**: Price briefly breaks below range lows (capturing stop-losses), reverses immediately with a wide-range bullish candle (SOSbar) and high volume. Wait for LPS (test of the Spring area) as conservative entry, or aggressive entry on the SOSbar breakout.
- **Confirmation**: SOSbar must follow the Spring. Volume should be relatively high on the reversal. Decreasing volume during the range supports accumulation.
- **Trade-offs**: Aggressive entry (at Spring) risks a failed Spring (turning into distribution). Conservative entry (at LPS) risks missing the move if the price doesn't pull back.
- **Volume Profile confluence**: Look for LVN at the range lows (rejection zone). VPOC below price supports buyer control.

### LPS (Last Point of Support)
- **When to use**: After a Spring has occurred and the initial SOS is visible. The price is pulling back toward the Spring area or mid-range.
- **How**: Wait for price to approach the Spring low or a mid-range HVN. Look for a new SOSbar (or order flow buy initiative) at the zone. Enter with buy stop above the trigger candle.
- **Confirmation**: Weaker volume on the pullback (effort/result harmony). Weis wave smaller on the corrective wave than the impulsive wave.
- **Trade-offs**: Higher probability than Spring entry but may not develop — the price may rally directly from the Spring.

### Back Up (BUEC / Test After Breakout)
- **When to use**: After the bullish breakout of the accumulation range, price returns to test the broken Creek.
- **How**: Identify the Creek (structure top + VAH confluence). Wait for price to pull back to this zone. Look for SOSbar or buy initiative at the zone. Enter buy stop above.
- **Confirmation**: Volume on the pullback should be low relative to the breakout. The pullback should not re-enter the range. Weekly VWAP confluence adds strength.
- **Trade-offs**: Most reliable entry but furthest from the start of the move — shorter risk/reward.

## Distribution Patterns

### Upthrust (Shakeout at Range Highs)
- **When to use**: Price is near the upper boundary of a potential distribution range after Phase A/B development.
- **How**: Price briefly breaks above range highs, reverses immediately with a wide-range bearish candle (SOWbar) and high volume. Wait for LPSY (test of the Upthrust area) as conservative entry.
- **Confirmation**: SOWbar must follow the Upthrust. High/unusual volume during range development supports distribution.
- **Trade-offs**: Aggressive entry risks a failed Upthrust (turning into accumulation). Conservative entry may not develop.
- **Volume Profile confluence**: Look for LVN at the range highs. VPOC above price supports seller control.

### LPSY (Last Point of Supply)
- **When to use**: After an Upthrust with initial SOW visible. Price is pulling back toward the Upthrust level.
- **How**: Wait for price to rally to the Upthrust zone. Look for SOWbar (or order flow sell initiative) at the zone. Enter with sell stop below.
- **Confirmation**: Low volume on the corrective rally. Bearish Weis waves predominant.
- **Trade-offs**: More reliable than Upthrust entry but may not offer a pullback.

### Test After Bearish Breakout
- **When to use**: After bearish breakout below Ice, price returns to test the broken structure.
- **How**: Identify the Ice (structure bottom + VAL confluence). Wait for price to pull back up to this zone. Look for SOWbar or sell initiative. Enter sell stop below.
- **Confirmation**: Low-volume pullback that does not re-enter the range.
- **Trade-offs**: Most reliable distribution entry.

## Continuation Patterns

### VPOC Migration — Continuation Protocol
- **When to use**: Session VPOC migrates in the direction of the trend.
- **How**: After VPOC migration, price should continue in the trend direction with speed. If price moves immediately, enter on a minor pullback to the new VPOC level.
- **Confirmation**: Time consumption is minimal — continuation should be nearly immediate.
- **Trade-offs**: Requires speed in execution. The pullback may be very shallow.

### VPOC Migration — Reversal Protocol
- **When to use**: VPOC migrates but price does NOT continue in that direction.
- **How**: Price consumes time at the new level without follow-through → change of character → look for reversal at the broken VA extreme or VPOC.
- **Confirmation**: Time consumption (excessive sideways) + impulsive move in the opposite direction.
- **Trade-offs**: Reversal trades have longer preparation time but may be V-turn (no clear entry).

### Control + Test (Order Flow Continuation)
- **When to use**: Missed the original reversal pattern. Trend is already established.
- **How**: Identify the control zone (imbalances + high volume + wide range candle from the impulsive move). Wait for price to test back to this level. Look for new initiative in the trend direction at the zone.
- **Confirmation**: Clean test with low-volume pullback to the control level. New initiative candle closes in favor of the trend.
- **Trade-offs**: Allows joining a trend after the initial move but requires precise level identification.

## Volume Profile Patterns

### Reversal at VA Extremes
- **When to use**: Price reaches VAH or VAL within a range context.
- **How**: At VAH, look for SOWbar or bearish initiative. At VAL, look for SOSbar or bullish initiative. Enter with stop beyond the extreme.
- **Confirmation**: Rejection candle (wick or close against the imbalance). Follow-through in the opposite direction.
- **Trade-offs**: Simple and objective. Works best when VAH/VAL align with structure levels.

### Continuation at VA Extreme (Test)
- **When to use**: Price has left the VA and returns to test VAH (bullish) or VAL (bearish).
- **How**: If rejected at the VA extreme → continuation trade in the breakout direction. If it re-enters the VA → activate reversion trade.
- **Confirmation**: The test candle should show low volume (lack of interest to re-enter). Immediate move away from the VA.
- **Trade-offs**: Clear binary outcome — either continuation or reversion.

### Failed Reversal
- **When to use**: Price re-enters the VA (activating reversion) but is strongly rejected at VPOC.
- **How**: If VPOC holds and price recovers the VA extreme → cancel reversion, activate continuation. If VPOC breaks → reversion still active.
- **Confirmation**: Strong reaction at VPOC (wide range, high volume). Subsequent move must confirm.
- **Trade-offs**: High complexity — requires active management at the VPOC level.
