# Wyckoff 2.0 Cheatsheet

## Volume-Price Anomaly Table

| Price Action | Volume | Interpretation | Action |
|-------------|--------|----------------|--------|
| Rising, wide range | High | Healthy trend, buying initiative | Hold long / add on pullback |
| Rising, narrow range | High | Effort/result divergence — supply appearing | Prepare to exit / tighten stops |
| Rising, wide range | Low | Weak rally — no institutional support | Do NOT buy / look for short |
| Falling, wide range | High | Healthy downtrend, selling initiative | Hold short / add on rally |
| Falling, narrow range | High | Effort/result divergence — demand appearing | Prepare to cover / tighten stops |
| Falling, wide range | Low | Weak sell-off — no institutional pressure | Do NOT short / look for long |
| Range development | Decreasing | Absorption — floating supply exhausted | FAVOR accumulation bias |
| Range development | Increasing/high | Urgency to distribute | FAVOR distribution bias |
| Breakout of range | High + wide range | Genuine breakout — intentionality | Enter in breakout direction after test |
| Breakout of range | Low or wick | Potential shakeout — suspect | Wait for confirmation before entry |

## Entry / Exit Rule Summary

### Entry Triggers (ONE of the following must appear at the trading zone):
- **SOSbar**: Bullish, wide range, close upper third, high volume
- **SOWbar**: Bearish, wide range, close lower third, high volume
- **Order Flow Buy Initiative**: ASK imbalances at/above closing price + follow-through up
- **Order Flow Sell Initiative**: BID imbalances at/below closing price + follow-through down

### Entry Order Types:
- **Primary**: BUY STOP (above high of trigger candle) / SELL STOP (below low of trigger candle)
- **Do NOT use**: LIMIT orders to anticipate the zone

### Stop Loss (choose one, in order of preference):
1. Beyond opposite extreme of trigger candle (tightest)
2. Beyond the structure level (Creek for longs, Ice for shorts)
3. Beyond nearest LVN (rejection zone)
4. Beyond VPOC of the profile (widest — only if R:R acceptable)

### Take Profit (choose one, in order of preference):
1. Nearest HVN in direction of trade (target zone)
2. VPOC of that HVN (exact target level)
3. Naked VPOC (previous session VPOC not yet tested)
4. DVPOC (developing VPOC not yet tested)
5. Previous relevant high/low (liquidity zone)

## Wyckoff Methodology Events Quick Reference

| Event | Phase | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| SC (Selling Climax) | A | High-volume selling exhaustion |
| AR (Automatic Rally) | A | Short-covering bounce |
| ST (Secondary Test) | A | Retest of SC area for supply remaining |
| UA (Upward Action) | B | Test to top of range — shows strength |
| UT (Upthrust) | B/C | Test to top — may be shakeout in distribution |
| Spring | C | False breakdown below lows (accumulation) |
| LPS | C | Pullback after Spring — conservative buy zone |
| SOS | D | Sign of Strength — breakout with volume |
| JAC | D | Jump Across the Creek — initial bullish breakout |
| BUEC | D/E | Back Up to Creek — test after breakout |
| SOW | D | Sign of Weakness — breakdown with volume |
| LPSY | C | Pullback after Upthrust — conservative sell zone |

## Order Flow Pattern Summary

| Pattern | Components | Location | Entry Signal |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Bullish Turn | Selling absorption (BID volume) + Buying initiative (ASK volume) | Support zone | SOSbar or ASK imbalance + price follow-through |
| Bearish Turn | Buying absorption (ASK volume) + Selling initiative (BID volume) | Resistance zone | SOWbar or BID imbalance + price follow-through |
| Continuation | Control (2+ imbalances in trend) + Test (pullback to control zone) | Pullback in trend | New initiative at control level |
