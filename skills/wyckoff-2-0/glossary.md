# Wyckoff 2.0 Glossary

## A
- **Absorption (Takeover)**: Limit-order blockade where large traders prevent price from continuing in a direction. High volume + little/no price movement + close against imbalance direction.
- **Accumulation**: Process where large traders buy shares quietly within a range. Cause that leads to bullish effect. Characterized by decreasing volume during range development.
- **AR (Automatic Rally)**: Phase A event — the initial upward reaction after Selling Climax, caused by short-covering and bargain hunting.
- **Auction Market Theory (AMT)**: Framework describing markets as auctions seeking equilibrium. Market moves between balance (ranges) and imbalance (trends).

## B
- **Back Up (BUEC)**: The test after a bullish breakout, where price returns to the broken structure level (Creek) before continuing upward.
- **b-shape Profile**: Volume profile showing bearish imbalance then range. Suggests weakness, future accumulation or redistribution.

## C
- **Cause & Effect (Law of)**: The trading range IS the cause (accumulation/distribution); the subsequent trend IS the effect. Duration and width of cause determines potential effect.
- **Change of Character (CHoCH)**: When price transitions from trend to sideways (or vice versa). Marks the boundary between Phases A and B.
- **Composite Profile**: Multi-session Volume Profile aggregated to identify macro context (HVNs, LVNs, VPOC).
- **Continuous Validation**: "If X, then Y" protocol for building scenarios and updating bias as new information arrives.
- **Control (Order Flow)**: Initiative imbalances occurring within an established trend. Confirms direction and provides a level (control zone) for potential re-entry.
- **Creek**: The upper resistance level of an accumulation structure. A bullish breakout above Creek confirms accumulation.

## D
- **Delta**: Difference between aggressive buying (ASK trades) and aggressive selling (BID trades). Positive = buying pressure; negative = selling pressure.
- **Distribution**: Process where large traders sell shares quietly within a range. Cause that leads to bearish effect. Characterized by high/unusual volume during range development.
- **DVPOC (Developing VPOC)**: A price level that was VPOC at some point during a session but is not the final VPOC. Useful as target level if not yet tested.

## E
- **Effort & Result (Law of)**: Volume (effort) should match price movement (result). Divergence signals hidden supply/demand and potential reversal.
- **Equilibrium**: State where buyers and sellers agree on value; represented by sideways price movement (range). Synonymous with accumulation/distribution.

## F
- **Failed Structure**: A structure that had all traces favoring one direction but reverses at the breakout test. A "failed accumulation" = distribution.
- **Finished Auction**: Decreasing volume at profile extreme. Low Volume Node = rejection. Price unlikely to revisit.
- **Fixed Range Profile**: Manual Volume Profile applied to a specific price range. Best for analyzing structures or trend legs.

## H
- **HVN (High Volume Node)**: Zone of high trading activity, representing balance/acceptance. Acts as a price magnet for future TP targets.

## I
- **Ice**: The lower support level of a distribution structure. A bearish breakout below Ice confirms distribution.
- **Imbalance (Order Flow)**: Disproportionate volume (200-400%+) between diagonal BID/ASK levels. Signals aggressive participation.
- **Initiative**: Market order aggression in a direction. Large volume + close in favor of imbalance + wide range = directional entry by large traders.
- **Initiative Buying**: ASK imbalances at the bottom of a candle, suggesting aggressive long entry.
- **Initiative Selling**: BID imbalances at the top of a candle, suggesting aggressive short entry.

## J
- **JAC (Jump Across the Creek)**: The initial bullish breakout of an accumulation structure with momentum.

## L
- **LPS (Last Point of Support)**: Phase C test event in accumulation — a pullback near the Spring/low after initial strength. Conservative entry point.
- **LPSY (Last Point of Supply)**: Phase C test event in distribution — a rally near the Upthrust/high after initial weakness. Conservative entry point.
- **LVN (Low Volume Node)**: Zone of low trading activity, representing imbalance/rejection. Acts as support/resistance. Excellent stop loss location.

## N
- **Naked VPOC**: A previous session's VPOC that has not yet been tested. High probability of being revisited. Used as target level.

## P
- **Phase A**: Trend stop events — SC (Selling Climax), AR (Automatic Rally), ST (Secondary Test).
- **Phase B**: Cause building — price oscillates in a range, accumulation or distribution occurs.
- **Phase C**: Test event — Spring (accumulation) or Upthrust (distribution). The shakeout.
- **Phase D**: Initial breakout — SOS/SOW bars marking the effect beginning.
- **Phase E**: Trend development — price moves out of the range.
- **P-shape Profile**: Volume profile showing bullish imbalance then range. Suggests strength, future distribution or reaccumulation.

## S
- **SC (Selling Climax)**: Phase A event — high-volume selling that exhausts supply. Usually the lowest point of the structure.
- **Shakeout**: The dominant event — a false breakout (Spring at lows or Upthrust at highs) that captures liquidity and triggers the real move.
- **SOS (Sign of Strength) / SOSbar**: Wide-range bullish candle, close in upper third, high volume. Confirms buying initiative.
- **SOT (Shortening of the Thrust)**: Pattern of momentum loss after 3+ pushes. Signals exhaustion and potential reversal.
- **SOW (Sign of Weakness) / SOWbar**: Wide-range bearish candle, close in lower third, high volume. Confirms selling initiative.
- **Spring**: Phase C shakeout in accumulation — a brief move below the range lows (into stops) that reverses immediately.
- **ST (Secondary Test)**: Phase A event — a retest of the SC/AR area to gauge remaining supply/demand.
- **Structural Failure**: Price cannot reach the opposite extreme of a validated structure. Early warning of hidden strength/weakness.
- **Supply & Demand (Law of)**: The universal driver of price. Price rises when demand > supply; falls when supply > demand.

## U
- **UA (Upward Action) / UT (Upthrust)**: Phase B events — a test to the high end of the range that denotes strength (accumulation) or weakness (distribution).
- **Unfinished Auction**: High volume at the profile extreme, suggesting price will return to finish the auction.
- **Upthrust (UT)**: Phase C shakeout in distribution — a brief move above the range highs (into stops) that reverses immediately.
- **UTAD (Upthrust After Distribution)**: The shakeout event in a distribution structure that captures longs above the range.

## V
- **VA (Value Area)**: The central 68.2% of volume in a profile, bounded by VAH and VAL. Represents the acceptance zone.
- **VAH (Value Area High)**: Upper boundary of the value area. Resistance zone.
- **VAL (Value Area Low)**: Lower boundary of the value area. Support zone.
- **VPOC (Volume Point of Control)**: The price level with the highest volume. Fair price. Price above = buyer control; below = seller control.
- **VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)**: Average price weighted by volume. Institutional benchmark. Above = "bought low"; below = "sold high."

## W
- **Weis Wave**: Volume indicator that accumulates volume per price wave. Used for Effort/Result analysis.
- **Wyckoff 2.0**: Integration of Wyckoff Methodology + Auction Market Theory + Volume Profile + Order Flow into a unified trading framework.
