# Wyckoff 2.0: Structures, Volume Profile and Order Flow
**Author**: Rubén Villahermosa Chaves | **Chapters**: 8 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### The Three Fundamental Laws
1. **Supply & Demand**: The engine of all price movement. Price rises when demand > supply; falls when supply > demand. Every pattern, event, and structure is an expression of this imbalance.
2. **Cause & Effect**: The trading range IS the cause (accumulation/distribution); the subsequent trend IS the effect. Duration and price range of the cause determine the magnitude of the effect. You cannot have a significant trend without prior preparation.
3. **Effort & Result**: Volume (effort) should confirm price movement (result). Divergence signals hidden institutional activity: high volume + no price movement = absorption (supply/demand being quietly accumulated).

### Accumulation / Distribution Schematics (5 Phases)
- **Phase A — Trend Stop**: Selling Climax (SC) with high volume → Automatic Rally (AR) → Secondary Test (ST) confirms supply exhaustion. For distribution: Buying Climax (BC) → Automatic Reaction (AR) → Secondary Test.
- **Phase B — Cause Building**: Price oscillates in a range. Accumulation = decreasing volume (supply being absorbed). Distribution = high/unusual volume (urgency to distribute). Test events to range extremes (UA/UT) reveal the developing bias.
- **Phase C — The Shakeout (Dominant Event)**: The false breakout that captures liquidity. Accumulation = Spring (break below lows, immediate reversal). Distribution = Upthrust (break above highs, immediate reversal). This is the single most important event — bias in its direction.
- **Phase D — Initial Effect**: SOS (Sign of Strength) bars with wide range + high volume for accumulation breakouts. SOW (Sign of Weakness) bars for distribution breakdowns. Volume must confirm the shakeout direction.
- **Phase E — Full Effect**: Trend develops away from the range. Price pulls back to structure levels (Back Up / BUEC for accumulation; test for distribution) before continuing. Trade these pullbacks at volume confluence zones.

### Volume-Price Relationships
- **Healthy trend**: Wide-range candles + high volume in the direction of the trend.
- **Effort/Result divergence**: Narrow-range candles + high volume = hidden supply/demand. Prepare for reversal.
- **Exhaustion**: Narrow-range candles + low volume = momentum fading. SOT pattern after 3+ pushes.
- **Absorption**: High volume at a level with no price movement = large traders blocking the move with passive orders.
- **Confirmation**: After a shakeout, the breakout move must have wide range + high volume. Without it, the shakeout signal is invalid.

### Spring / Upthrust / Shakeout Setups
- **Spring (Buy Setup)**: Price breaks below range lows (capturing short stops), reverses immediately. Requires SOSbar confirmation. Conservative entry: LPS (Last Point of Support) — pullback near the Spring level. Trade management: stop beyond the Spring low, target at Creek (top of range) or beyond.
- **Upthrust (Sell Setup)**: Price breaks above range highs (capturing long stops), reverses immediately. Requires SOWbar confirmation. Conservative entry: LPSY (Last Point of Supply) — pullback near the Upthrust level. Trade management: stop beyond the Upthrust high, target at Ice (bottom of range) or beyond.
- **LPS/LPSY**: The test after the shakeout. LPS = pullback after Spring showing weak volume (no supply). LPSY = rally after Upthrust showing weak volume (no demand). These are the most reliable entry points.

### Key Volume Profile Levels
- **VPOC**: Most traded price. Above = buyer control. Below = seller control. Avoid trading near it.
- **VAH/VAL**: Value Area boundaries. Reversal zones at extremes. Re-entry after breakout → 80% rule.
- **HVN**: High Volume Nodes = targets. Price is magnetically attracted.
- **LVN**: Low Volume Nodes = support/resistance. Place stops beyond them.
- **VWAP**: Institutional benchmark. Price above = cheap; below = expensive.

### Wyckoff 2.0 Operating Scheme
1. **Context**: Range (at extremes? inside?) or Trend (interacting with value zone? away?)
2. **Zones**: Structure levels + Volume levels (VAH, VAL, VPOC, VWAP, HVN, LVN)
3. **Scenario**: Continuous validation — "If price does X, then expect Y." Always prepare alternative.
4. **Management**: Entry trigger (SOS/SOWbar or Order Flow initiative) → Stop (beyond LVN/structure) → Target (HVN/VPOC)
