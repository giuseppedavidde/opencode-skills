# Price Action Patterns & Setups

## Pattern Break (Standard Breakout)
- **When to use**: Buildup cluster present at a clear pattern boundary (line or box), market in line with dominance, no major obstacles to target.
- **How**: Identify signal bar (bullish bar closing against resistance for longs, bearish bar closing against support for shorts). Enter on market order when next bar breaks the signal bar's high/low. Manage with 20/10 bracket.
- **Trade-offs**: Highest frequency setup. Most reliable when all four steps are present (attack, defense, buildup, break). Requires patience — do not enter on first touch.

## Pattern Break Pullback (Second-Instance Entry)
- **When to use**: Initial break lacked buildup or had an uncomfortable signal bar. Post-breakout action either clings at the barrier or pulls back to test it.
- **How**: Monitor post-breakout price action. Look for a new buildup and signal bar forming at the broken barrier or at the pullback extreme. Enter on break of this new signal bar.
- **Trade-offs**: Lower frequency than standard pattern break. Provides a second chance when the first break was untradeable. The pullback must not reverse so far that it invalidates the original break.

## Pattern Break Combi (Two-Bar Squeeze)
- **When to use**: Within a buildup, a tall bar (powerbar) is followed by a small inside bar. The inside bar indicates a pause in the tug-of-war — tension is concentrated.
- **How**: Enter on the break of the inside bar's high (for longs) or low (for shorts). Alternatively wait for the powerbar break. The inside bar should preferably share color with the powerbar.
- **Trade-offs**: Less common but powerful signal of concentrated tension. A three-bar combi (two inside bars) is even stronger. Do not treat the combi as a standalone signal — it must appear within valid buildup.

## Pullback Reversal (Trend Resumption)
- **When to use**: A correction against the dominant trend has reached the 25ema (ideally perforated it), preferably coinciding with an S/R level. Trend must be clear.
- **How**: Identify the correction type — diagonal (price, 50-60% retracement) or horizontal (time, flag-like). Wait for a signal bar indicating the turn. Enter on break of the signal bar in the dominant direction.
- **Trade-offs**: The contrarian element requires extra caution. The correction must be mature — 40% minimum retracement in strong trends. The 25ema filter prevents premature entries.

## Break for Failure (Trap Trade)
- **When to use**: A breakout has all tradable characteristics (boundary, buildup, signal bar) but clearly goes against the dominant pressure. The breakers are likely trapped.
- **How**: Do NOT take the initial breakout. Wait for the first break to fail — watch for a reversal signal bar that sets up a counter-break in line with dominance. Enter on break of this counter-signal bar.
- **Trade-offs**: Potentially very powerful (immediate follow-through as trapped participants bail). Advanced setup — master the other four first. Requires strong conviction about dominance. Not every failed break should be faded.
