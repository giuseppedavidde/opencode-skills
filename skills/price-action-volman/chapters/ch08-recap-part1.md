# Chapter 8: Recap Part 1

## Core Idea
A consolidated review of all Part 1 concepts through fresh chart examples. Emphasis on reading the full picture — pressure, conditions, and setup as an integrated whole — rather than isolated patterns.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Integrated reading approach**: Assess each chart with an overview perspective first. Identify line of least resistance, round number fights, obstructive elements, cluster progressions, crucial highs/lows. Then drill into specific trade opportunities.
- **Five trade setup recap**: Pattern break, pattern break pullback, pattern break combi, pullback reversal, and break for failure (Ch 7). All evaluated using the 20/10 bracket standard.

## Key Concepts
- **Conservative mode first**: Do not expand into aggressive or contrarian tactics until consistently profitable with conservative breakout methods.
- **Two-page layout**: Charts remain visible while reading annotations — practice maintaining the overview while absorbing detail.
- **Full picture questions**: Before any trade: what is the dominant pressure? Is there buildup? Is there a valid signal bar? Are there obstacles to target?

## Anti-patterns
- **Focusing on setups alone**: A wager is always a consequence of pressure, conditions, and setup — never just the setup.
- **Expanding to aggressive tactics prematurely**: Master conservative trading first. The urge to expand is natural but dangerous if undercapitalized.

## Key Takeaways
1. Read the full chart, not just the setup zone.
2. Five setups cover the conservative toolkit — do not add more until fluent.
3. The 20/10 bracket is the evaluation baseline; understand why it works before changing it.
