# Chapter 6: Manual Exits

## Core Idea
The 20/10 bracket is the default, but three situations warrant discretionary override: news events, resistance encounters, and reversal signals.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **News Report Exit**: Before high-impact releases (NFP, interest rate decisions, housing/production data, central bank speeches), close positions or reduce exposure. Breakouts near news are poisonous — the spike risk exceeds the 10-pip stop. Economic calendars are essential preparation.
- **Resistance Exit**: If price approaches a clear S/R level (round number, former high/low, pattern boundary) before the 20-pip target is reached, consider taking profit manually. Price stalling or building up at resistance is the cue.
- **Reversal Exit**: If the breakout shows reversal signs immediately after entry — a dominant opposing bar, a failed follow-through bar — exit manually even if the stop has not been hit. Do not hope for recovery.

## Key Concepts
- **Three exit options near news**: (a) stay in and gamble, (b) reduce exposure, (c) exit entirely. Option (c) is preferred for high-impact events.
- **EUR/USD sensitive times**: 14:30 CET (US stock open) and 16:00 CET. European releases at 08:00 and 11:00.
- **Resistance as profit zone**: Target the far side of the next obstacle, not necessarily 20 pips.

## Anti-patterns
- **Holding through a news spike**: The 10-pip stop offers no protection against gap or slippage.
- **Letting a reversal hit the stop when signs were clear early**: Trust the reversal bar — scratch the trade.
- **Exiting too early at minor resistance**: Distinguish between a genuine S/R level and a random pause.

## Key Takeaways
1. The bracket is the baseline; manual exits are for specific, defensible situations.
2. News events demand respect — exit before high-impact releases.
3. Resistance exits and reversal exits improve expectancy when applied selectively.
