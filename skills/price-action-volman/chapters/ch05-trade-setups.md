# Chapter 5: Trade Setups

## Core Idea
There are four specific entry setups derived from buildup/break behavior. Every entry must satisfy three requirements: line with dominance, break from buildup, and a setup-defining signal bar within that buildup.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Pattern Break**: The standard breakout. Buildup at a pattern boundary (line or box), a signal bar closing against the barrier, entry on the next bar breaking the signal bar. Four-step ideal: (1) attack boundary, (2) defense holds, (3) alternating bar cluster builds tension, (4) break with follow-through.
- **Pattern Break Pullback**: For when the initial break is untradeable (too little buildup, sloppy signal). Price either clings around the broken barrier or follows through then pulls back to test it. A second-instance signal bar within this post-breakout action sets up the trade.
- **Pattern Break Combi**: A two-bar pattern: a tall bar (powerbar) followed by a small inside bar that stays within the powerbar's range. The break of the inside bar (or powerbar) is the entry trigger. The combi structure signals concentrated tension.
- **Pullback Reversal**: Trade the resumption of dominance after a correction. Two types: correction in price (diagonal, 50-60% retracement) or correction in time (horizontal flag). The pullback should reach the 25ema. Entry on the break of a signal bar at the turn.

## Key Concepts
- **Signal bar**: The crucial bar within buildup from which break takes position. In a bull breakout, a bullish bar closing against the pattern barrier.
- **Entry bar**: The subsequent bar that breaks the signal bar's high/low.
- **Three core requirements**: (1) trade in line with dominance, (2) break from buildup, (3) identifiable signal bar within the buildup.
- **One-pip chart**: Use full-pip increments so barrier levels align cleanly.

## Anti-patterns
- **Trading the break without a clear signal bar**: A hunch is not a setup.
- **Entering on the first touch of a boundary without buildup**: Contrarians feast on premature breaks.
- **Mistaking low-volume sideways action for buildup**: Dead market phase ≠ pre-breakout tension.

## Key Takeaways
1. Four setups cover all conservative breakout scenarios: pattern break, pattern break pullback, pattern break combi, pullback reversal.
2. The signal bar is your trigger discipline — without it, no trade.
3. Buildup is the common thread across all four setups.
