# Cheatsheet: 5-Minute Price Action Scalping

## Candle Pattern Reference (5-min context)
| Pattern | Signal | Action |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Bullish bar closing at high against barrier | Buildup pressure | Signal bar candidate for long |
| Bearish bar closing at low against barrier | Buildup pressure | Signal bar candidate for short |
| Inside bar after powerbar | Tension concentrated | Combi setup — break inside bar |
| Tall doji at boundary | Indecision within buildup | Wait for next bar confirmation |
| Failed break bar (long upper wick at resistance) | Contrarian pressure | Potential trap, watch for reversal |

## Support / Resistance Rules
- **Identify by**: Former highs/lows, round numbers, pattern boundaries (line/box), 25ema slope changes
- **Role reversal**: Broken support → resistance (above); broken resistance → support (below)
- **When to act**: Wait for buildup at the level. Do not trade first touch.
- **Stop reference**: Above the last distinctive high (shorts) or below the last distinctive low (longs) within buildup

## Entry / Exit Rules for Scalping (20/10 Bracket)
| Phase | Action | Rule |
|-------|--------|------|
| **Pre-entry** | Check dominance | Overall slope must support direction |
| **Pre-entry** | Check buildup | Cluster of alternating bars at boundary required |
| **Pre-entry** | Identify signal bar | Bull bar closing high for longs; bear bar closing low for shorts |
| **Entry** | Fire market order | On break of signal bar high/low by next bar |
| **Stop** | 10 pips | Market order OCO; do not widen |
| **Target** | 20 pips | OCO take-profit; manual exit if strong S/R is closer |
| **Manual exit override** | News | Exit before high-impact releases (NFP, rate decisions) |
| **Manual exit override** | Resistance at target | Take profit early if price stalls at clear S/R |
| **Manual exit override** | Reversal | Exit if entry bar reverses and a dominant opposing bar forms |

## The Three Core Questions (Before Every Trade)
1. Is this break **in line with dominant pressure**?
2. Is there **clear buildup at the boundary**?
3. Is there a **valid signal bar** to break?

If any answer is "no", skip the trade.
