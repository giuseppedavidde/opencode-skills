# Understanding Price Action: Practical Analysis of the 5-minute time frame
**Author**: Bob Volman | **Chapters**: 12 | **Generated**: 2026-05-28

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Double Pressure
Prices follow through when both camps join forces — bulls buying and bears covering. Anticipate double-pressure pops from buildup (pre-breakout tension). A cluster of alternating bars battling at a boundary signals the sweetspot. The break of a signal bar within the cluster triggers entry.

### Support & Resistance Role Reversal
Former support becomes resistance when touched from below; former resistance becomes support when touched from above. Use these levels to gauge dominant pressure, not to blindly enter. Wait for buildup at the barrier before committing.

### False Break / Tease Break / Proper Break
- **False break**: price pierces a barrier without buildup, instantly reverses — trap for the unwary
- **Tease break**: some buildup but not at the critical boundary; price may initially counter then succeed on second attempt
- **Proper break**: clear buildup at the barrier, tight signal bar, close against the boundary — highest odds for follow-through

### Buildup / Pre-breakout Tension
The defining characteristic of a high-odds breakout. A tight cluster of alternating bars at a support/resistance level indicates a tug-of-war. The side with the most defined boundary is the break side. Without buildup, decline the offer.

### Dominant Pressure
Never trade against the prevailing slope. In a bull-dominant chart, prices make higher highs and corrections struggle to undercut former lows. Strong dominance takes time to roll over — first reversal attempts are unreliable.

### 25 Exponential Moving Average (25ema)
Used as a gauge of market pressure, not a trading signal. Sloping up with bars above = bulls in control; sloping down = bears in control. Also serves as a filter for pullback reversals (correction should reach or perforate the 25ema before turning).

### Ceiling Test
After a failed breakout attempt, price retraces inside the pattern then launches a new attack on the same boundary. Often sets up the proper break. The re-break after ceiling test carries high odds.

### Round Number Effect
Psychological levels (e.g., 1.3500) act as support/resistance. Price often fights, stalls, or reverses at these levels. Treat them as additional boundary confirmation.

### 20/10 Bracket (Target/Stop)
Standard management: 20-pip target, 10-pip stop. Tight stop forces discipline; the bracket is an OCO (one-cancels-other). The target aligns with typical double-pressure follow-through on 5-min eur/usd.
