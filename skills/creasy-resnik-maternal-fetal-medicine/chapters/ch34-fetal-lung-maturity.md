# Chapter 34: Assessment and Induction of Fetal Pulmonary Maturity

## Core Idea
Respiratory distress syndrome (RDS) results from deficient surfactant phospholipid and protein production, causing alveolar collapse, muscular exhaustion, and respiratory failure. Antenatal pulmonary maturity testing estimates the risk of RDS but cannot fully separate the mature from the immature fetus, because infection, hypoxia, hypotension, and central respiratory drive also determine the clinical course. A mature test is highly reassuring; an immature test is not a diagnosis. Antenatal corticosteroids are the most effective intervention and should not be withheld for a maturity test when preterm delivery is imminent.

## Frameworks Introduced
**Amniotic fluid maturity tests.** Lecithin-to-sphingomyelin (L/S) ratio ≥2:1 indicates maturity. Phosphatidylglycerol (PG) is one of the last phospholipids to appear and is highly predictive of maturity, though it has a high false-immaturity rate before 36 weeks. The surfactant-to-albumin ratio (TDx FLM, later TDx FLM II) was accurate but has been removed from the U.S. market. Lamellar body count (LBC) uses an automated particle counter calibrated for platelets, because lamellar bodies are 1–5 µm (1.28–6.4 fL) and similar in size to platelets.

**Lamellar body count interpretation (consensus).** No centrifugation. ≥50,000/µL indicates maturity; ≤15,000/µL indicates immaturity; 15,000–50,000/µL is transitional and needs another test. Meconium lowers the count; blood contamination may raise it initially through platelets and then lower it through coagulation; interpret cautiously with >1% blood.

**Antenatal corticosteroid protocol.** Two doses of betamethasone 12 mg intramuscularly 24 hours apart, or four doses of dexamethasone 6 mg intramuscularly 12 hours apart. No other regimen has proven efficacy. Benefit begins within 24 hours and lasts at least 7–14 days.

## Key Concepts
- The predictive value of a test indicating maturity is 97–100%. The risk of RDS after an immature result ranges from 5% to almost 100%, depending on gestational age and the degree of immaturity.
- Even with a mature L/S ratio or positive PG, preterm birth before 34 weeks carries RDS risk of 19.2%, severe intraventricular hemorrhage 8.1%, and necrotizing enterocolitis 4.8%. Morbidity falls with advancing gestational age, not with the test result alone.
- Blood contamination: maternal serum has an intrinsic L/S ratio of 1.3:1 to 1.9:1, so blood can falsely lower an immature value. A mature result remains reassuring because blood cannot raise an immature value to mature.
- Meconium raises the L/S ratio by 0.1–0.2 before term and by up to 0.5 after 35 weeks; use cutoffs of 2.2:1 before term and 2.5:1 at term when meconium is present. PG is unaffected by blood or meconium.
- Diabetes: class A, B, and C diabetes may delay pulmonary maturation despite an L/S ratio of 2:1. Classes D, R, and F may show accelerated maturation. Good glycemic control resolves the difference by 38 completed weeks. Use an L/S cutoff of 3:1, or rely on PG, an S/A ratio ≥70 mg/g, or a mature LBC.
- Vaginal pool specimens after preterm premature rupture of membranes correlate well with amniocentesis: L/S r = 0.88, 89% concordance, PG r = 0.94. Avoid specimens with blood, meconium, or mucus; vaginal mucus can falsely elevate the LBC.
- Antenatal corticosteroids reduce RDS (RR 0.66), intraventricular hemorrhage (RR 0.54), necrotizing enterocolitis (RR 0.46), and neonatal death (RR 0.69) in a meta-analysis of 21 trials and 4269 infants, without increasing maternal or neonatal infection.
- Treatment is recommended for all pregnant women at 24–34 weeks who are at risk of delivery within 7 days, unless corticosteroids would harm the mother or delivery is imminent. For preterm premature rupture of membranes before 30–32 weeks without clinical chorioamnionitis, treatment is recommended because of the high risk of intraventricular hemorrhage.
- Benefit is present even when delivery occurs before the second dose. Rescue therapy may reduce composite neonatal morbidity (43.9% versus 63.6% in one trial), but routine repeat courses are not recommended outside randomized trials because of reduced birth weight and head circumference.
- Betamethasone reduces fetal breathing, body movements, and heart rate variability; these effects are more pronounced than with dexamethasone and resolve within about 3 days. Do not misinterpret them as fetal compromise.
- Corticosteroids raise maternal leukocyte count and glucose; screen for gestational diabetes at least 1 week after administration.

## Anti-patterns
- Delaying delivery for a maturity test when there is nonreassuring fetal testing, suspected intrauterine infection, or deteriorating maternal or fetal status.
- Treating an immature L/S ratio as a diagnosis of RDS, or a mature ratio as a guarantee of no RDS.
- Using an L/S ratio of 2:1 in a poorly controlled diabetic pregnancy without PG or a higher cutoff.
- Interpreting an LBC in a bloody or meconium-stained specimen without caution.
- Giving repeated corticosteroid courses routinely; reserve rescue therapy for a subsequent threatened preterm birth remote from the first course.
- Assuming corticosteroids other than betamethasone or dexamethasone provide fetal maturation; prednisone, prednisolone, hydrocortisone, and cortisol do not reach equivalent fetal doses.
- Using thyrotropin-releasing hormone or other adjuncts; they have not shown benefit and may cause adverse neurologic outcomes.

## Key Takeaways
1. Use L/S ≥2:1, positive PG, S/A ≥70 mg/g, or LBC ≥50,000/µL as evidence of maturity; a mature result is more reliable than an immature one.
2. For diabetic pregnancies, require PG, an L/S of 3:1, or an S/A ratio ≥70 mg/g.
3. Give betamethasone 12 mg intramuscularly twice, 24 hours apart, or dexamethasone 6 mg four times, 12 hours apart, at 24–34 weeks when delivery is expected within 7 days.
4. Do not delay indicated delivery for maturity testing; give steroids when preterm birth is anticipated unless delivery is imminent.
5. Use a single rescue course only for a new threatened preterm birth remote from the initial course.
