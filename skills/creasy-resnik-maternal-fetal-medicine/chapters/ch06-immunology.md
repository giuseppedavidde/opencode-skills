# Chapter 6: Immunology of Pregnancy

## Core Idea

The fetus is a semi-allograft that expresses paternal major histocompatibility complex antigens, yet it is not rejected. The classical explanations were systemic immunosuppression and a shift from T helper 1 to T helper 2 cytokines. The chapter rejects both as sufficient. It argues that pregnancy is a sequence of inflammatory states, and that innate immune cells at the interface are required for implantation, angiogenesis, and trophoblast invasion rather than being a threat to the fetus. Tolerance is local, active, and permissive.

## Frameworks Introduced

**Medawar allograft analogy.** The fetus is semi-allogeneic because it carries paternal antigens. The analogy predicts rejection unless an escape mechanism exists.

**Three-phase inflammatory model.** The first trimester is proinflammatory (T helper 1 environment), required for implantation and repair. The second trimester is anti-inflammatory (T helper 2 environment), coinciding with rapid fetal growth. The third trimester is proinflammatory again, required for parturition.

**HLA expression model.** Villous trophoblast in contact with maternal blood is class I negative. Extravillous trophoblast expresses HLA-C, HLA-E, and HLA-G, but not HLA-A or HLA-B.

**Decidual leukocyte model.** Uterine natural killer cells, macrophages, dendritic cells, and regulatory T cells each carry a defined role, and depletion of any of them impairs pregnancy in animal models.

**Pattern recognition receptor model.** Toll-like receptors sense extracellular and endosomal pathogen-associated molecular patterns. NOD-like receptors sense intracellular bacterial products and assemble the inflammasome.

## Key Concepts

- In the first trimester, 70% of decidual leukocytes are natural killer cells, 20% to 25% are macrophages, and about 1.7% are dendritic cells.
- Decidual natural killer cells are CD56^bright CD16^negative and are not cytotoxic. They support angiogenesis and trophoblast invasion.
- Decidual natural killer cells make up about 40% of cells in the human decidua.
- Decidual macrophages are CD14+, HLA-DR+, and CD68+. They are predominantly M2, oriented to tissue repair.
- Depletion of uterine natural killer cells or dendritic cells harms pregnancy outcome in animal models. This is the opposite of the rejection model.
- HLA-G was originally described as monomorphic and immunologically neutral. Alternative splicing yields membrane-bound and soluble variants, and limited polymorphism is now recognised.
- HLA class I genes lie at 6p21.3.
- Fas ligand is secreted by trophoblast in microvesicles rather than expressed on the cell surface.
- Decidual stromal cells silence chemokine genes CXCL9 and CXCL10 through the repressive histone mark H3K27me3. This limits maternal T cell recruitment.
- Eleven mammalian Toll-like receptors exist, but only the first 10 are expressed in humans.
- TLR2 heterodimerises with TLR1, TLR6, or TLR10. TLR3 senses double-stranded RNA, TLR5 senses flagellin, TLR8 senses single-stranded RNA, and TLR9 senses CpG DNA.
- TLR signalling proceeds through MyD88. TLR3 and TLR4 can also signal through TRIF/TICAM1 to produce type I interferon.
- NOD1 recognises γ-D-glutamyl-meso-diaminopimelic acid from gram-negative bacteria. NOD2 recognises muramyl dipeptide from all bacteria.
- NALP1 and NALP3 assemble the inflammasome with ASC and caspase-1, which cleaves pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 into active forms.
- Infection-driven preterm labor is attributed to excessive innate inflammation or apoptosis at the interface, not to classical rejection.

## Anti-patterns

- Describing pregnancy as a state of systemic immunosuppression. Pregnant women do not show excess severe infection, and HIV disease does not accelerate in pregnancy.
- Requiring a T helper 2 shift for a successful pregnancy. The evidence is contradictory, and the model is an oversimplification.
- Treating uterine natural killer cells as cytotoxic killers of trophoblast. They are regulatory and pro-angiogenic.
- Assuming the trophoblast expresses no major histocompatibility complex class I. It expresses HLA-C, HLA-E, and HLA-G.
- Assuming HLA-G is monomorphic and therefore tolerogenic by default. This premise is now questioned.
- Equating decidual inflammation with rejection. Inflammation is required for implantation and for labor.

## Key Takeaways

1. Interpret first- and third-trimester inflammation as physiology, not as failure of tolerance.
2. Treat the decidua as an active regulator of maternal immune cell traffic.
3. Read infection-associated preterm labor as a pattern-recognition receptor-driven process.
4. Do not counsel that pregnancy suppresses immunity in a clinically meaningful way.
5. Expect HLA-C, HLA-E, and HLA-G, but not HLA-A or HLA-B, at the interface.
6. Use the three-phase model when explaining trimester-specific disease patterns.
