# Creasy and Resnik's Maternal-Fetal Medicine: Principles and Practice
**Edition**: Seventh | **Chapters**: 72 | **Generated**: 2026-11-07

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. Maternal adaptation (Ch 7)
- Plasma volume +45% (1200–1600 mL) by 32 wk; red cell mass +20–30% → dilutional anemia.
- Cardiac output +30–50% (+43% at term); systemic vascular resistance −21%; blood pressure nadir 24–32 wk.
- Supine aortocaval compression lowers cardiac output up to 25%; use left lateral tilt.
- GFR +50%; creatinine 0.5–0.7 mg/dL; proteinuria up to 300 mg/24 h is normal.
- Tidal volume +40%; PaCO2 28–31 mmHg; bicarbonate 18–22 mEq/L; pH 7.44.

### 2. Fetal oxygenation (Ch 12, 14)
- PO2 cascade: maternal artery 72 → umbilical vein 28 → umbilical artery 19 mmHg.
- Umbilical venous saturation 81% ± 7% (lower limit 67%); arterial saturation 47%.
- Exchange occurs in terminal villi; oxygen transfer is limited by blood flow and diffusion.
- Maternal alkalosis shifts the maternal curve left and can impair fetal oxygenation.
- Streaming: ductus venosus → foramen ovale → left ventricle → brain; superior vena cava → right ventricle → ductus arteriosus → descending aorta.
- Umbilical artery Doppler: increased pulsatility → absent → reversed flow.

### 3. Labor (Ch 5, 43)
- Parturition = myometrial activation + cervical remodeling + decidual and membrane activation.
- Stages: first (onset to full dilation), second (full dilation to birth), third (birth to placenta).
- Contemporary curves are slower; NICHD 2012 rejected 1.2/1.5 cm/h.
- Arrest: ≥6 cm, ruptured membranes, ≥4 h with >200 Montevideo units, or ≥6 h inadequate.
- Shoulder dystocia: 0.24–2.00%; 40–50% <4000 g.

### 4. Fetal surveillance (Ch 32, 33)
- Category I: 110–160 bpm, moderate variability 6–25 bpm, no late or variable decelerations.
- Category II: all tracings not I or III; resuscitate and reassess.
- Category III: absent variability with late or variable decelerations, bradycardia, or sinusoidal → resuscitate; deliver if unresolved.
- Resuscitation: left lateral, fluids, stop oxytocin, correct hypotension, tocolysis, amnioinfusion.
- NST: two accelerations ≥15 bpm for ≥15 s after 32 wk; ≥10 bpm for ≥10 s before 32 wk.
- BPP: five components/30 min. 10/10, 8/8, 8/10 normal; 8/10 with oligohydramnios = chronic compromise; 6/10 repeat; ≤4/10 deliver.
- Doppler: umbilical artery for growth restriction; middle cerebral artery peak velocity >1.5 MoM for anemia.

### 5. Hypertension (Ch 48)
- Chronic: ≥140/90 before 20 wk or beyond 84 d postpartum. Gestational: new after 20 wk without proteinuria.
- Preeclampsia: ≥140/90 on two occasions plus proteinuria ≥300 mg/24 h or ≥30 mg/dL.
- Severe: ≥160/110 twice ≥6 h apart; proteinuria ≥5 g/24 h or 3+; oliguria <500 mL/24 h; cerebral/visual symptoms; pulmonary edema; RUQ pain; liver dysfunction; thrombocytopenia; growth restriction.
- Delivery is the only cure: term → deliver; severe at ≥32–34 wk → deliver; earlier → stabilize, corticosteroids, magnesium.
- Magnesium sulfate 4–6 g over 15–30 min, then 1–2 g/h; calcium gluconate is the antidote.
- Acute severe hypertension: hydralazine, labetalol, or nifedipine; aspirin 81 mg/day before 16 wk.

### 6. Preterm prevention (Ch 39–42)
- Pathways: preterm labor, preterm PROM, cervical insufficiency, indicated delivery.
- Progesterone: 200 mg vaginal daily for cervical length ≤20 mm before 25 wk; 17-OHPC 250 mg IM weekly after prior preterm birth.
- Cerclage: history 12–14 wk; cervical length <25 mm before 24 wk; physical exam 16–23 wk.
- Steroids 24–34 wk: betamethasone 12 mg IM ×2, 24 h apart, or dexamethasone 6 mg IM ×4, every 12 h; one rescue before 32 6/7 wk.
- Magnesium sulfate before 32 wk: 4–6 g over 30 min, then 1–2 g/h for 12–24 h.
- Tocolysis buys 48 h; no perinatal benefit.
- Preterm PROM: ampicillin plus erythromycin 48 h, then oral 5 d; corticosteroids; expectant to 34 wk.

### 7. Diabetes (Ch 59)
- One-step 75 g OGTT: fasting ≥92, 1 h ≥180, 2 h ≥153 mg/dL; one value.
- Two-step: 50 g challenge ≥135 or ≥140 → 100 g OGTT with ≥95, ≥180, ≥155, ≥140 mg/dL; two values.
- Overt diabetes: fasting ≥126 mg/dL, HbA1c ≥6.5%, random ≥200 mg/dL.
- Targets: preprandial 65–95; 1 h postprandial ≤130–140 mg/dL.
- Insulin is first-line; metformin and glyburide cross the placenta.
