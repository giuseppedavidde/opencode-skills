# Cheatsheet — Creasy and Resnik

## Gestational age

| Category | Cutoff |
|---|---|
| Preterm | <37 0/7 |
| Late preterm | 34 0/7–36 6/7 |
| Term | 37 0/7–41 6/7 |
| Early term | 37 0/7–38 6/7 |
| Full term | 39 0/7–40 6/7 |
| Post-term | ≥42 0/7 |

## Hypertension

| Disorder | Definition |
|---|---|
| Chronic | ≥140/90 before 20 wk or >84 d postpartum |
| Gestational | New ≥140/90 after 20 wk; no proteinuria |
| Preeclampsia | ≥140/90 ×2 + proteinuria ≥300 mg/24 h or ≥30 mg/dL |
| Severe | ≥160/110 ×2 ≥6 h; protein ≥5 g/24 h; oliguria <500 mL/24 h; cerebral/visual; pulmonary edema; RUQ pain; platelets; FGR |
| Eclampsia/HELLP | Seizures; hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, low platelets |

## Diabetes

| Test | Threshold (mg/dL) |
|---|---|
| 75 g OGTT | FPG ≥92; 1 h ≥180; 2 h ≥153; one value |
| 50 g screen | ≥135/140 → 100 g OGTT |
| 100 g OGTT | ≥95; 180; 155; 140; two values |
| Overt | FPG ≥126; HbA1c ≥6.5%; random ≥200 |
| Targets | Premeal 65–95; 1 h postmeal ≤130–140 |

## Fetal heart rate

| Category | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| I | 110–160; variability 6–25; no late/variable | Routine |
| II | Not I or III | Resuscitate; reassess |
| III | Absent variability + late/variable; bradycardia; sinusoidal | Resuscitate; deliver |

## Labor

| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Protraction | <1.2 cm/h nullipara; <1.5 multipara (historical) |
| Arrest | ≥6 cm + ROM + ≥4 h >200 MVU or ≥6 h inadequate |
| Oxytocin | 4 mU/min; +6 every 15 min; max 40 mU/min |

## Preterm prevention

| Item | Dose or criteria |
|---|---|
| Steroids | 24–34 wk; betamethasone 12 mg IM ×2/24 h or dexamethasone 6 mg IM ×4/12 h |
| Magnesium | <32 wk; 4–6 g/30 min, then 1–2 g/h 12–24 h |
| Progesterone | Cervical length ≤20 mm <25 wk; 200 mg/day |
| Cerclage | History 12–14 wk; length <25 mm <24 wk; exam 16–23 wk |
| GBS | Colonized; preterm; ROM ≥18 h; temperature ≥38.0°C |
| pPROM | Ampicillin 2 g q6h + erythromycin 250 mg q6h 48 h, then oral 5 d |

## Drugs

| Indication | First-line |
|---|---|
| Severe HTN | Hydralazine 5–10 mg IV q20 min; labetalol 20–80 mg IV; nifedipine 10 mg PO |
| Eclampsia | Magnesium 4–6 g load, 1–2 g/h; seizure: 2 g bolus or diazepam 5–10 mg |
| GDM | Insulin; metformin or glyburide |
| GBS | Penicillin G 5 MU, then 2.5–3 MU q4h |
| Rh prophylaxis | RhIG 300 µg at 28 wk and ≤72 h postpartum |
| Preeclampsia | Aspirin 81 mg/day before 16 wk |

Reference ranges: creatinine 0.5–0.7 mg/dL; PaCO2 28–31 mmHg; HCO3 18–22 mEq/L. Magnesium toxicity: reflexes lost 9–13 mg/dL; respiratory depression 14 mg/dL.
